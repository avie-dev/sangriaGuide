The Casino is a set of betting machines that pay in Ourosangue. Walk up to one and interact to open the CASINO panel. Each machine serves one player at a time.

## How to bet

1. Choose the bet: 500, 1,000, 1,500, 2,000, 2,500 or 3,000.
2. Check the **Prize multiplier** line.
3. Click **SPIN**. The button shows the amount it will take.

<Aside type="caution">
Each SPIN charges immediately, with no confirmation. The bet and prize are settled the instant you spin, and the animation is just a show.
</Aside>

## Paytable

For one matching row at the **minimum bet (500)**:

| Three of | Pays |
|---|---|
| Fish | 250 |
| Gem | 750 |
| Jewel | 2,000 |
| Twilight Call | 5,000 |
| Magic Stone | 15,000 |
| Demon Fragment | 100,000 |
| Tattered Claws | Nothing, and **cancels the whole spin**, even other winning rows |

Prizes scale with the bet: double at 1,000, six times at 3,000. **Betting more doesn't change your chance of winning**, only the prize size. The panel's *How it works* tab has the current paytable. If it differs from this page, the game is right.

The official guide doesn't publish the odds of each symbol, so this wiki can't say whether the Casino pays back more or less than you put in.

## Tracking

The **History** tab shows spins, amount wagered, amount received, biggest prize and your balance (up or down). In the Bank statement, bets show as *Casino - bet* and prizes as *Casino - prize*.
