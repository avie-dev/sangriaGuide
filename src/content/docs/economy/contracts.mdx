Deliver materials for Ourosangue, or pay other players to deliver them to you. Contracts are handled by **Balthazar, the Master of Contracts**, at City Hall in the City of Sangria Falls.

## Opening the panel

Talk to Balthazar and choose *Open the contracts panel*, or from anywhere:

<Command cmd=".contract panel">Opens the contracts panel.</Command>

The panel has four tabs (**Balthazar**, **Players**, **Mine**, **Create**), with your rank, daily limits and balance on the left.

## Balthazar's contracts

<Steps>

1. **Balthazar** tab: each offer shows the item, quantity and pay. Click **ACCEPT**.
2. Gather the full quantity in your inventory. The line shows how much you already have.
3. **Mine** tab → *Accepted contracts* → **Deliver**. You're paid instantly and earn 1 rank point.

</Steps>

**Abandon** frees the slot, but the day's accept doesn't come back. Offers only ask for materials from regions you've reached, based on the V Bloods you've defeated.

<Aside type="tip" title="New players">
While you've only defeated Act 1 V Bloods (Alfa to Quincey), Balthazar adds **2 extra raw-material offers a day at 500 Ourosangue each**. They count towards your 3 accepts. The tutorial's apprentice contract (1,000 wood for 1,500 Ourosangue) comes once and doesn't count.
</Aside>

## Delivering on player contracts

**Players** tab: each line shows the item, how much is left, the remaining pay, who created it, and the deadline. Click **Deliver all** or **1 batch**. Delivery is always in batches, and you're paid in proportion. A *complete only* line must be filled in one go. You can deliver on up to **5 different players' contracts per day**.

Anything above an item's base value is capped at **7,500 Ourosangue per day** on player contracts. Delivering at base value doesn't use that cap.

## Creating a contract (buying materials)

<Steps>

1. **Create** tab: click the item.
2. Set **Batches** (−10, −1, +1, +10).
3. Set **Total reward**: Minimum, Maximum, or a value between them.
4. Choose the deadline (6, 12 or 24 hours) and delivery type: **Partial** or **Complete**.
5. Check the total cost and click **Create contract**.

</Steps>

The total is the reward **plus a 10% fee**, taken from your Bank immediately. The reward is held for whoever delivers. **The fee is never refunded**, not even if you cancel. You can create 3 contracts a day and have 3 open.

When someone delivers, **Mine → My buy orders → Collect** gives you the items, even if you were offline. If nobody has delivered, **Cancel** returns the reward. When the deadline passes, any unused reward returns on its own. You can't deliver on your own contract, and you can't choose who delivers.

## Ranks

Each completed Balthazar contract gives 1 point. Your first delivery of the day on each player contract also gives 1. Points never reset.

| Rank | Points |
|---|---|
| Apprentice | Start |
| Envoy | Not published |
| Trader | Not published |
| Magnate | Not published |
| Hand of Balthazar | 300 |

Higher ranks make Balthazar pay more, from your next offers onwards.
