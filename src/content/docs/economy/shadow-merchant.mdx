The **Shadow Merchant** is an automatic buyer. There's no NPC or shop: you open it wherever you are. It pays a fixed price per unit, straight into your Bank.

## Opening and selling

<PlayModes>
	<Fragment slot="interface">
		Use the **Taunt** emote, or type `.inv view`. (In combat, the emote doesn't open it.)

		1. Under ITEMS IN INVENTORY, only items the Merchant buys appear. Click the ones to sell, or **Select all**.
		2. Check each value and the **Total** under SELECTION.
		3. Click **SELL SELECTED**.

		The **?** button shows the full price table, including items you don't have.
	</Fragment>
	<Fragment slot="chat">
		<Command cmd=".inv view">Calls the Shadow Merchant's chest in front of you (or brings it back and renews its time).</Command>
		<Command cmd=".inv sell">Sells everything in the chest.</Command>
	</Fragment>
</PlayModes>

<Aside type="caution">
Sales are final, with no undo.
</Aside>

It buys only listed items: basic materials, hides, fish, ingots, flawless gems, seeds, paper, coins, ancestral weapon fragments and Soul Shards.

## Merchant, Market or contracts?

| Option | Pays | Speed |
|---|---|---|
| Shadow Merchant | Fixed, low for common materials | Instant |
| Market automatic sale | 15% more per unit than the Merchant (not ancestral weapon fragments) | After a 7–30 day deadline |
| Market listing | Your price | When a player buys |
| Balthazar's contracts | Per contract | When you deliver |

The official guide's advice: the Merchant is for clearing your inventory, not for getting rich.
