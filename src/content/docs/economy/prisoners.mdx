You capture prisoners as in the base game: dominate the unit and lock it in a Prison Cell in your castle. The **Prisoner Market** lets you trade them for Ourosangue, keeping their blood type, quality and level.

Open it from anywhere with the **chained fist** button by the minimap, or:

<Command cmd=".pri painel">Opens the PRISONER MARKET (tabs: MARKET, MY SALES, MY STOCK).</Command>

## Buying

<Steps>

1. **MARKET** tab: filter by blood type and minimum quality (steps of 10%). **Lowest price** sorts cheapest first.
2. Click **Buy**, then **Confirm**.
3. The prisoner goes to **MY STOCK**. You don't need to be anywhere in particular to buy.

</Steps>

Blood types in the filter: Warrior, Scholar, Brute, Rogue, Worker, Mutant, Draculin, Creature, Corrupted and Frailed.

## Putting it in a cell

Stand within **3 metres** of an empty Prison Cell in your castle (or your clan's). Put away your pet, leave Dominating Presence, and have no other follower. Then click **Call** in MY STOCK. If the cell refuses it, it returns to your stock, so nothing is lost.

## Selling

1. Stand next to the cell holding the prisoner. It must be **well fed: at least 99% health and at most 1% misery**.
2. **MY SALES**: type a price (1,000–15,000) and click **List prisoner next to you**.
3. The prisoner leaves the cell and becomes a listing. The Ourosangue reaches your Bank when it sells, even offline.

**Cancel** returns a listing to your stock.

<Aside type="caution">
With a full stock (2) you can't buy or cancel a listing, so call one into a cell first. If you go **more than 15 days** without logging in, **your stock is deleted**. Listings and prisoners already in cells are unaffected.
</Aside>

## Can't buy?

Not enough balance, a full stock, less than 10 minutes since your last purchase, or the listing is yours or a clanmate's.
