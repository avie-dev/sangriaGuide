It's automatic. Stay connected with your character in the world, and there's no NPC, panel or command needed to get paid. It works the same with or without the interface.

<Command cmd=".online">Shows your streak, today's online time, time to the next reward and its value, and what you've earned today and in total. `.onl` also works.</Command>

## Streak values

| Streak day | Per reward |
|---|---|
| 1 | 0.40 |
| 2 | 0.45 |
| 3 | 0.50 |
| 4 | 0.55 |
| 5 | 0.60 |
| 6 | 0.65 |
| 7 and after | 0.70 |

A day counts towards the streak only when you complete its 30 minutes. A whole day without them resets the streak to day 1. The streak keeps counting after day 7 (for your best), but the value stops rising.

## Examples (official)

- 1 hour online on day 1: rewards at 30 min, 45 min and 1h, so 3 × 0.40 = **1.20**.
- 1 hour with a 7-day streak: 3 × 0.70 = **2.10**.
- 2 hours with a 7-day streak: 7 rewards = **4.90**.

<Aside type="caution" title="Midnight">
Time played before midnight stays with the day that ended. The new day starts from zero, so playing past midnight doesn't carry your minutes over. Leaving the game doesn't reset the day's time, though. If you left at 40 minutes, your next reward comes when the day's total reaches 45.
</Aside>

## Lost your streak to server downtime?

Open a ticket on Discord (discord.gg/SangriaFalls) so the staff can review it.
