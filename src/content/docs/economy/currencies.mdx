Your Bank holds five balances. None of them takes inventory space or is lost on death.

<FilterTable placeholder="Filter currencies…">

| Currency | Comes from | Spent on | As an item? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ourosangue** (everyday money) | Events, Balthazar's contracts, Market sales, the Shadow Merchant, gathering, Outpost missions, exchanging Sangricoins | Market and shop purchases, the Vault, Arena entry, character/weapon/blood prestige, Transfusions, class evolutions, Sangrulho fusion, fortification, the Casino | No |
| **Sangricoins** (special) | Reality Rupture, Goreswine's Judgment, Tower of the Immortals, the Online Reward, Battle Pass, Outpost weeklies, donations | Meditation, speeding up Sangrulho incubation, some resets (pet Cristais, plasma reset), profession prestige and paid profession changes, furniture | No |
| **Quitina** (Chitin) | Outpost missions, Battle Pass | 2x XP for 30 minutes on Character, Weapon or Blood | **Yes**, the only one you can withdraw |
| **Ressonador** | Illusory Arena (rare prize), Master and Nightmare dungeons, Battle Pass, events; Richard's quest gives the first 2 | Rolling Sangrulho eggs (1 per use) | Exists as an item; deposit it |
| **Cristais** | Master and Nightmare dungeon rewards, drops | Upgrading pet attributes (1 per attempt) | Exists as an item; deposit it |

</FilterTable>

<Aside type="note" title="Exchange">
**1 Sangricoin = 35 Ourosangue**, and only that way round. You can't turn Ourosangue into Sangricoins.
</Aside>

## Starting balance

You start with **25 Sangricoins** and earn **10,000 Ourosangue** during the tutorial.

## Retired currency

The old *Greater Essence* item was retired, and its balance was converted to Ourosangue. If you still have some, deposit it through `.menu bank` → *Deposit everything from inventory*. Each unit becomes 1,000 Ourosangue.

## Decimals

Some systems pay or charge fractions, such as the Shadow Merchant and meditation (0.35 Sangricoin per minute), so balances can show decimals.
