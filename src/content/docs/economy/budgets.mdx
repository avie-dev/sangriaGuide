## Character progression

| Goal | Ourosangue | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Character prestige 1–20 | 22,760 | Published total |
| One weapon type, prestige 1–20 | 22,685 | Same price range as character prestige ([Q-017](/about/open-questions/#q-017)); the first (75) is free with Vlad Parker |
| One blood type, prestige 1–20 | 22,685 | As above |
| Character + one weapon + one blood, all 20 | 68,130 | Sum of the three |

The weapon and blood totals assume their prestige costs match the character's, as the official guides say ("from 75 up to 4,467"). Only the endpoints are published.

## Endgame

<CostTable id="transfusion" />

| Goal | Without VIP | With VIP |
|---|---|---|
| 10 Transfusions (Luminance achievement) | 100,000 | 80,000 |
| 30 Transfusions (Thanatos achievement) | 300,000 | 240,000 |
| All 250 Transfusions | 2,500,000 | 2,000,000 |

Pulses themselves are free. Each Pillar achievement echo costs 200,000 from Asterion, and the Pillar of Megara ends with a 200,000 tribute.
