The Sangria Falls economy runs through your **Bank**. It works from anywhere, holds five currencies, and is where almost every system charges or pays. Balances belong to your account: they take no inventory space and aren't lost when you die.

| Need to… | Go to |
|---|---|
| Understand the five currencies | [Currencies](/economy/currencies/) |
| Deposit, withdraw, exchange | [The Bank](/economy/bank/) |
| Earn Ourosangue | [Earning guide](/economy/earning-guide/) |
| Plan big purchases (prestige, evolutions, Transfusions) | [Budgets](/economy/budgets/) |
| Buy or sell with players | [Market](/economy/market/), [Contracts](/economy/contracts/), [Prisoners](/economy/prisoners/) |
| Sell materials instantly | [Shadow Merchant](/economy/shadow-merchant/) |
| Store more items | [Vault](/economy/vault/) |

**There's no transfer of balance between players.** Ourosangue only changes hands through Market sales, contracts and profession services.
