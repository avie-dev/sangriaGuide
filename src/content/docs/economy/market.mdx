Go to the city (the golden button by the minimap, the teleport panel, or `.mercado go`) and press **L**. The panel doesn't open outside the city.

## Buying

<Steps>

1. Press **L**. Pick a category (WEAPONS, RESOURCES, CONSUMABLES…) or search by name. In WEAPONS, the **Blue**, **Purple** and **Orange** buttons show only legendaries of that colour.
2. Click **PRICE** to sort cheapest first.
3. Click a row to see the item's details.
4. Click **Buy**.

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution">
Buying is **instant, with no confirmation screen**, and can't be undone. Check the item, amount and price first.
</Aside>

**View Shops** lists the open shops and everything a seller has. Each row has a **star** (keep it in Favourites, up to 30) and a **crossed-out eye** (hide it, up to 200; unhide from the Hidden tab).

## Getting a shop

In the city, find a stall named **Empty shop**, interact, and confirm the 5,000 Ourosangue. The shop starts closed.

## Selling

<Steps>

1. Press **L** → **Sell** on the bottom bar.
2. Click an item in your inventory and check **Lowest price on the market**.
3. Type the price for the **whole stack** and click **List**. Repeat for other items.
4. Click **Manage shop** → **Open shop**. It now sells on its own, even while you're offline.

</Steps>

To add, remove or cancel listings, close the shop first (**Manage shop** → **Close shop**), make the changes, and reopen it. Each slot holds at most 4,000 units, so split bigger stacks. Sale proceeds go straight to your Bank. **History** shows your purchases and sales. When your last item sells, the shop closes itself.

## Minimum prices

| Item | Minimum |
|---|---|
| Dracula armour piece or Soul Shard | 5,000 |
| T08 necklace | 1,000 |
| Blue legendary weapon | 5,000 |
| Purple legendary weapon | 10,000 |
| Orange legendary weapon (unique) | 20,000 |

On these items, each **fortification level adds 1,000** and each **refinement adds 2,000** to the minimum. For example, an Orange weapon at fortification 10 with 3 refinements needs at least 36,000. The sell panel shows the minimum.

## Automatic sale

When listing, you can set a deadline of **7, 15, 20 or 30 days** (off by default). If nobody buys by then, the server buys the item:

| Item | Server pays |
|---|---|
| Dracula armour piece | 1,000 |
| T08 necklace | 1,500 |
| Blue legendary weapon | 1,000 |
| Purple legendary weapon | 3,000 |
| Orange legendary weapon (unique) | 10,000 |
| Anything the Shadow Merchant buys | 15% more per unit than the Merchant (a Soul Shard: 920) |

The value is the same for every deadline. Items with fortification or refinement, and ancestral weapon fragments, are excluded. Removing the item cancels it, and a closed shop waits until it reopens.

## Losing your shop

If you go **5 days without logging in**, the shop is freed and your items go to market storage. They aren't lost:

<Command cmd=".market storage">Opens your market storage.</Command>

A shop that stays **empty and closed for 48 hours** is also freed. You can rename yours under **Manage shop → Rename**.

## Can't buy?

The usual reasons: a full inventory, not enough balance, the seller's shop has closed, or the item just sold.
