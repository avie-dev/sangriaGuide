<Unverified q="Q-012">The staff don't publish income per hour for any activity, and this wiki has no tested numbers. The tables below list **official payouts and limits**. The "ceilings" are arithmetic from those payouts, not what a typical player earns.</Unverified>

## Ourosangue

<FilterTable placeholder="Filter sources…">

| Source | Official payout | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Reality Rupture | Adventurer 3,000 · Veteran 4,000 · Master 5,000 · Nightmare 6,000 (plus Sangricoins) | 1 rewarded win per window; 3 windows a day |
| Goreswine's Judgment | 2,500–3,500 (plus Sangricoins) | 1 rewarded victory per day |
| Outpost daily | 1,500–2,500 by rank (Cursed Forest up to 4,000) | 1 per day in total |
| Outpost weekly | 7,500–10,000 by rank (Cursed Forest up to 15,000) | 1 per week in total |
| Balthazar's contracts | Per offer; higher with rank | 3 accepts a day |
| Player contracts | The buyer's price | 5 players' contracts a day; 7,500 a day above base value |
| Gathering (Miner / Fisher / Woodcutter) | 25 per vein / 70 per catch / 10 per tree; +10% / +15% / +10% per prestige | None |
| Profession orders | Profession XP + Ourosangue | 2 daily, 1 weekly special, 1 weekly hunt |
| Market sales | Your price, no fee | 56 shop slots |
| Shadow Merchant | Fixed per unit (e.g. Soul Shard 800, The Fish 4,000) | None |
| Profession services | Your price, plus 2,000 per level that goes to the game | 2 orders a week per craft prestige |
| Aquarium fish sales | 30–85 per fish by rarity and level | Production per day by tank level |
| Tower of the Immortals | Weekly top 3 per class: 20,000 / 15,000 / 10,000 | Weekly |
| Illusory Arena win | 500 bonus | Doesn't cover the 2,000/8,000 entry fee |
| Exchange | 1 Sangricoin = 35 Ourosangue | One way |

</FilterTable>

## Sangricoins

| Source | Official payout | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Reality Rupture | Adventurer 15 · Veteran 20 · Master 25 · Nightmare 30 | 1 rewarded win per window |
| Goreswine's Judgment | 20–30 | 1 per day |
| Online Reward | 0.40–0.70 every 15 minutes after 30 minutes online | No cap |
| Outpost weekly | 75 | 1 per week |
| Tower of the Immortals | Top 3 per class: 150 / 100 / 50; participation 200 (wave 20 ten times) | Weekly |
| Battle Pass | Some levels | Per season |

## Daily ceilings from events

<Aside type="note">
The rest of this page is this wiki's arithmetic from the payouts above.
</Aside>

| If you win… | Ourosangue | Sangricoins |
|---|---|---|
| Nightmare Rupture in all 3 windows | 18,000 | 90 |
| Adventurer Rupture in all 3 windows | 9,000 | 45 |
| Goreswine's Judgment once | 2,500–3,500 | 20–30 |
| Outpost daily (Rank I–V, outside Cursed Forest) | 1,500–2,500 | — |

The Rupture rewards one win per window, so the official guide advises choosing the hardest difficulty your group can beat on the first try.

## Suggested daily routine

This is a suggestion. It covers the capped sources first, because those are easy to miss, and leaves the rest of your time to whatever you prefer.

1. Outpost daily (and the weekly, once a week).
2. Accept 3 Balthazar contracts.
3. Your profession orders.
4. A Rupture win in each window you're online for, and the Judgment once.
5. 30+ minutes online for the Online Reward.
6. Sell spare materials (Market automatic sale, or the Shadow Merchant for instant money).

See [Budgets](/economy/budgets/) for what you're saving towards.
