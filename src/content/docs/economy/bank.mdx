The Bank is your account's wallet. It works **from anywhere on the map**, with no NPC needed. Market, Vault, Arena, prestiges and the other systems charge and pay straight into it, so you never carry currency in your pocket.

## Opening it

<PlayModes>
	<Fragment slot="interface">Press **F5**, or click the bank icon on the minimap. The five balances are at the top, and the **STATEMENT** with your latest transactions is below.</Fragment>
	<Fragment slot="chat"><Command cmd=".menu bank">Opens the Bank menu: balances, statement, deposit, withdrawal and exchange.</Command> `.banco open` also works.</Fragment>
</PlayModes>

## Depositing currency items

Some currencies also exist as items: **Primal Stygian Shard** (becomes Ourosangue), **Quitina**, **Ressonador** and **Cristal**. To turn them into balance, which you can't lose on death:

<Steps>

1. Type `.menu bank`. A numbered menu appears.
2. Type `2` for *Deposit everything from inventory*.
3. Chat shows what was deposited. It also appears in the statement.

</Steps>

The panel has no general deposit button, so interface users use this chat menu too. Stand still, because the menu closes if you walk more than 5 metres. Cristais also have their own command:

<Command cmd=".banco depositar cristal">Deposits the Cristais in your inventory.</Command>

## Exchanging Sangricoins for Ourosangue

<Steps>

1. `.menu bank`, then `4` (*Currency exchange*).
2. `1` (*Sangricoins → Ourosangue (1 = 35)*).
3. Type how many Sangricoins, for example `10`, to receive 350 Ourosangue.

</Steps>

<Command cmd=".banco cambio 10">Exchanges 10 Sangricoins for 350 Ourosangue directly.</Command>

<Aside type="caution">
The exchange only goes one way. Ourosangue can't become Sangricoins. Transactions made with the direct commands don't show in the statement; those made through the menu do.
</Aside>

## Quitina

On the Quitina card (F5), **WITHDRAW · DEPOSIT** moves Quitina between the Bank and your inventory, and **USE** activates it on Character, Weapon or Blood. Without the interface: `.banco sacar quitina 2` withdraws 2. See [Quitina](/character/quitina/).

## Reading the statement

Each line shows the amount, currency, reason and date. **Green +** is income and **red −** is spending. Identical consecutive transactions are grouped with **×** and a count. The panel shows up to 30 lines.

## Common questions

### Can I send Ourosangue to a friend?

No. Ourosangue only changes hands through Market sales, contracts and profession services.

### Can I withdraw Ourosangue or Sangricoins as items?

No. Only Quitina can be withdrawn.
