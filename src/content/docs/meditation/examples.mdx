<Aside type="note" title="How these numbers are made">
Every example multiplies **published** figures: 0.35 Sangricoin per minute per path, and level 1 → 90 in about 1h14 (74 minutes), or 50 minutes with VIP. The guide rounds those times, so treat the results as estimates. They assume you start each climb at level 1, meditate only on the Character path, and have no server XP event running.
</Aside>

## One climb, level 1 → 90

| | Time | Sangricoins |
|---|---|---|
| Normal | ~74 min | 74 × 0.35 ≈ **25.9** |
| VIP | ~50 min | 50 × 0.35 = **17.5** |

## All 20 character prestiges

Reaching prestige 20 takes 20 climbs from level 1 to 90.

| | Time | Sangricoins | Ourosangue (prestige costs) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Normal | 20 × 74 min ≈ **24h 40m** | ≈ **518** | 22,760 |
| VIP | 20 × 50 min ≈ **16h 40m** | **350** | 22,760 |

## One Transfusion's worth of Pulses

At prestige 20, each climb to 90 becomes a Pulse. A Transfusion needs 10 Pulses plus Ourosangue.

| | Time for 10 Pulses | Sangricoins | Ourosangue |
|---|---|---|---|
| Normal | 10 × 74 min ≈ **12h 20m** | ≈ **259** | 10,000 |
| VIP | 10 × 50 min ≈ **8h 20m** | **175** | 8,000 |

Transfusions are capped at 250, so the whole run is 2,500 Pulses. At the rates above, that's thousands of hours of meditation, which is why the endgame also uses hunting, events and the Kill Points race. See [Endgame](/endgame/).

## An overnight session

8 hours on one path costs **168 Sangricoins** (336 on Double Meditation) and gives **5,040,000 XP** (7,560,000 with VIP). For the character, that's about 6.5 climbs from 1 to 90 (about 9.6 with VIP). Without Auto-Prestige, though, the character path stops at 90 after the first climb.

## Sangricoins in perspective

- The Bank exchanges **1 Sangricoin = 35 Ourosangue**, one way only, so an hour of meditation (21 Sangricoins) is worth 735 Ourosangue at the exchange.
- The Online Reward pays 4.90 Sangricoins for 2 hours online with a 7-day streak (official example).
