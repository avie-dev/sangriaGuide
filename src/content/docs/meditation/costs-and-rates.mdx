## Cost

The cost comes from your **Sangricoin** Bank balance. It isn't paid in Ourosangue.

| Duration | One path | Double Meditation (two paths) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 minute | 0.35 | 0.70 |
| 1 hour | 21 | 42 |
| 8 hours | 168 | 336 |

**VIP doesn't make meditation cheaper.** It raises the XP, but the cost stays the same. A path that isn't yielding (max level, unarmed, no blood, no pet) is not charged.

## XP rates

| | Per tick (minute) | Per hour | Level 1 → 90 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Normal | 10,500 | 630,000 | About 1h14 |
| VIP <Label type="vip" /> | 15,750 | 945,000 | About 50 minutes |

These are the official figures for meditation. The panel shows each path's XP per minute.

## Which bonuses count

| Bonus | Counts in meditation? |
|---|---|
| VIP | Yes, +50% |
| Server XP events | Yes |
| Quitina | No |
| Welcome gift (3-day double XP) | No |

## Paying for it: Sangricoins

Sangricoins come from events (Reality Rupture, Goreswine's Judgment, Tower of the Immortals), the **Online Reward**, the Battle Pass, Outpost weeklies and donations. You start with 25. You can't convert Ourosangue into Sangricoins, because the exchange only goes the other way (1 Sangricoin = 35 Ourosangue).

The Online Reward keeps paying while you're connected, including while you meditate: 0.40 to 0.70 Sangricoin every 15 minutes after your first 30 minutes of the day. See [Worked examples](/meditation/examples/).
