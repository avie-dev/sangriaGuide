## Why each item matters

- **Sangricoins.** Meditation stops when your balance drops below 0.35. Meditation doesn't pay Sangricoins, but the Online Reward does (see below).
- **Path conditions.** The Weapon path levels the weapon equipped *that minute*, and the Blood path levels the current blood. If the condition fails, the panel shows UNARMED, NO BLOOD or NO PET, and you gain nothing, though you're not charged.
- **Max level.** At 90 (character) or 100 (weapon, blood, pet), the path stops yielding. Without VIP Auto-Prestige, the rest of the night is wasted, so for long sessions without VIP consider a path that's far from its cap.
- **Auto-Prestige caps** <Label type="vip" />. Auto-Prestige spends Ourosangue without asking. A spending cap stops surprises. Auto pulse is **on by default** at prestige 20, so decide before you get there.
- **AutoContinue.** The launcher's optional AutoContinue mod reconnects you after a disconnect or server restart. The staff recommend it for meditating away from the keyboard.

<Aside type="caution" title="Restarts and disconnects">
Meditation stops if you disconnect, and the server restarts at set times each day. The official guides say that after a reconnect your character returns to where it was, but they don't say whether meditation resumes by itself (see [Q-003](/about/open-questions/#q-003) for restart times). Check for the aura when you come back.
</Aside>

## The Online Reward offsets some of the cost

You get the Online Reward just for being connected, and meditating counts. After 30 minutes online in a day, it pays every 15 minutes: 0.40 Sangricoin on day 1 of a streak, rising to 0.70 from day 7.

For example, 8 hours online from the start of the day, with a 7-day streak, gives rewards at 30 minutes, 45 minutes, and so on up to 8 hours. That's 31 rewards × 0.70 = **21.7 Sangricoins**, against 168 for 8 hours of one meditation path. This is a calculation from the published rates, not an official figure.

See [Worked examples](/meditation/examples/) for more numbers.
