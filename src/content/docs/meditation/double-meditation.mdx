**Double Meditation** makes two paths yield at the same time. Each one gets **full XP** and each one charges its **own 0.35 Sangricoin per minute**, so it costs double.

<Aside type="note" title="How it's unlocked">
It's a **separate unlock, by donation**: open a ticket on the official Discord (discord.gg/SangriaFalls) and the staff enable it on your account. **It isn't part of VIP**, and neither is the Bloody Pass.
</Aside>

## Setting it up

<PlayModes>
	<Fragment slot="interface">
		1. Press **F10**. The footer of YOUR SESSION shows *Double Meditation unlocked*.
		2. Each path now has two small buttons, **1st** and **2nd**.
		3. Click **1st** on your main path and **2nd** on the other. They must be different.
		4. YOUR SESSION shows both, as PRIMARY and SECONDARY.

		To go back to a single path, click **SELECT** on any path.
	</Fragment>
	<Fragment slot="chat">
		<Command cmd=".meditate double xp pet">Turns on Double Meditation with the Character XP and Pet paths (requires the unlock). Use the path names `xp`, `weapon`, `blood` or `pet`.</Command>
	</Fragment>
</PlayModes>

Each path still needs its own condition: the weapon in hand, an active blood, or the pet summoned inside the chamber.

## Cost

| Duration | Double Meditation |
|---|---|
| 1 hour | 42 Sangricoins |
| 8 hours | 336 Sangricoins |

VIP's +50% applies to the XP, not the cost.
