**Auto-Prestige** is a VIP perk. It prestiges your character, weapon, blood and pet **automatically, the moment each reaches max level**. You don't need to be meditating or standing still, only online.

## Opening it

<PlayModes>
	<Fragment slot="interface">**F8** (VIP panel) → **CONFIGURE** on the Auto-Prestige perk. Or use the Profile (F1) → ACCOUNT → Auto-Prestige → **Config.**, or type `.ap panel`. Without VIP, the panel shows *AUTO-PRESTIGE LOCKED*.</Fragment>
	<Fragment slot="chat"><Command cmd=".ap status">Shows the types turned on, targets, Pulses and cap.</Command> The character menu (`.level`) also has an Auto-prestige option.</Fragment>
</PlayModes>

## Turning types on

Each type has its own card. Click **OFF** to switch it **ON**, and click again to turn it off. The band at the top shows how many are active (for example "2/4 types active").

| Type | Acts on |
|---|---|
| Character XP | Your character, at level 90 |
| Weapon Mastery | **The weapon in your hand**, at 100 |
| Bloodline | **Your active blood**, at 100 |
| Pet | **Your summoned pet**, at 100 |

<Command cmd=".ap enable exp">Turns a type on. Use `exp`, `arma`, `sangue` or `pet`; `.ap disable exp` turns it off.</Command>

<Aside type="caution" title="It spends without asking">
With a type on, Ourosangue leaves your Bank without a prompt. **Set a spending cap first.**
</Aside>

## Target: how far to go

On each card, use **−** and **+**. **MAX** means prestige 20, and a number such as 10/20 means stop at 10. When the target is reached, the type **pauses** and tells you in chat. It doesn't switch off, so raise the target to continue.

<Command cmd=".ap target arma 10">The weapon stops at prestige 10 (`max` removes the target).</Command>

## Spending cap

In the **LIMITS** board, type an amount in **Spending cap** (for example 5000) and click **OK**. Type 0 to remove the limit. The board shows how much has been spent. When the next prestige wouldn't fit under the cap, Auto-Prestige pauses and tells you. Setting a new cap resets the count.

<Command cmd=".ap cap 5000">Limits Auto-Prestige to 5,000 Ourosangue (0 removes the limit).</Command>

## Pulses

At **prestige 20**, the Character XP type starts giving **Pulses** when you reach 90. A Pulse returns you to level 1 for free (see [Endgame](/endgame/)).

- **Auto pulse is ON by default.** To stop it, click **Auto pulse** in the LIMITS board until it reads OFF, ideally before reaching prestige 20. Prestiges continue normally.
- **Pulses to give:** type a number and click OK to cap how many it gives (0 = no limit).
- After 250 transfusions, Pulses end and auto pulse stops by itself.

<Command cmd=".ap pulse desligar">Turns auto pulse off (`.ap pulse ligar` turns it on).</Command>
<Command cmd=".ap pulses 5">Lets auto pulse give at most 5 Pulses (0 removes the limit).</Command>

Auto-Prestige **never transfuses**. Click TRANSFUSE yourself.

## Common questions

### I turned on Weapon Mastery and nothing happened.

It only watches the weapon in your hand, the active blood and the summoned pet, and only acts at max level. It also does nothing without Ourosangue in your Bank.

### My VIP ended.

Auto-Prestige turns off with it. Everything already prestiged stays.
