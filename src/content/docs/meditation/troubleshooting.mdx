## I'm not gaining XP

The panel (F10) shows the reason in red on the path:

| Reason | Fix |
|---|---|
| UNARMED | Hold the weapon you want to level |
| NO BLOOD | Have a blood type active |
| NO PET | Summon your pet **inside** the chamber |
| MAX LEVEL | Prestige that track, or switch path |

If none of these is shown, you're short on **Sangricoins**. The minimum is 0.35. Without the interface, a path the menu shows in grey isn't yielding right now.

## The MEDITATE button does nothing

It only works once the **starter tutorial is finished** and a **path is chosen**. The chat option *Go to the meditation area* has the same requirements.

## Am I charged when nothing is happening?

No. A path that isn't yielding (max level, no weapon, no pet) costs nothing.

## My Quitina or welcome gift isn't counting

That's expected. **Personal XP bonuses don't count in meditation.** Only VIP (+50%) and server XP events do.

## Does VIP make it cheaper?

No. VIP raises the XP; the cost is the same 0.35 per minute per path.

## I can't summon my pet

The city doesn't allow pets, but the Meditation Chamber does. Go in first, then summon it (F2 or `.pet`). On the Pet path the aura shows on the pet, not on you.

## Meditation stopped

It stops if you leave the chamber, disconnect, or run out of Sangricoins. Standing still is fine. You don't have to move.

## I want to level my character and weapon at the same time

One path runs at a time. Two at once needs [Double Meditation](/meditation/double-meditation/), a separate donation unlock.
