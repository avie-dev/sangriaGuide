A **Pulse** is what prestige becomes after prestige 20. At level 90 you pulse: your **level goes back to 1** and you gain **+1 Pulse**. It costs nothing, and you keep your prestige, buffs and plasmas.

## The first Pulse

When you reach prestige 20, **Nyxaris** sends the quest *The Call of Blood*. Talk to her, climb to 90, pulse, then **go back to her** to close the quest. After that, just repeat.

## How to pulse

<PlayModes>
	<Fragment slot="interface">At level 90, click **PULSE** on the prestige bar (it appears there when available). The Profile (F1) has the same button.</Fragment>
	<Fragment slot="chat"><Command cmd=".tf pulso">Gives a Pulse immediately, without confirmation.</Command></Fragment>
</PlayModes>

- **Pulses stack with no limit.** You can save more than 10.
- Each Transfusion uses 10 Pulses.
- After the **250th transfusion**, Pulses end, the button disappears, and any saved Pulses turn into Kill Points.

## Auto-Pulse <Label type="vip" />

With VIP Auto-Prestige's **Character XP** type on, prestige 20 makes it **pulse automatically** at 90. **Auto pulse is on by default.** Turn it off in the LIMITS board, or cap it with *Pulses to give*. It never transfuses. See [Auto-Prestige](/meditation/auto-prestige/).

<Aside type="tip">
Meditation is a steady way to climb back to 90: about 1h14 per Pulse, or 50 minutes with VIP. See [Worked meditation examples](/meditation/examples/).
</Aside>
