## A way to decide

1. **Know what your class uses.** Each class page says what it benefits from and what it doesn't use. For example, Draekhar doesn't use Physical Power, and Mortak Unshakable has no crit.
2. **In F12, read each ancestor's bonuses *and* penalties.** Penalties grow with bonuses.
3. **Prefer bonuses your class uses**, and penalties to stats it doesn't.
4. **Build slowly.** Each Transfusion adds only 10 plasmas, and moving them costs a 50-Sangricoin Reset.
5. **VIP:** keep alternative distributions in presets, for example one per class you play.

| Profile | Classes | Doesn't use |
|---|---|---|
| Physical | Noctarin, Bastien, Carmesyne, Skarn, Umbrellis, General | Spell Power |
| Physical, no crit | Mortak (Unshakable) | Spell Power, Physical Crit |
| Magic | Draekhar, Sangrael, Sepulkros (Summoner) | Physical Power, Weapon Skill Power |
| Hybrid | La Muerte | Physical Power |

If you've tested ancestors, please share your results on the [open questions page](/about/open-questions/#q-013).
