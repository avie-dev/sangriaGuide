Each ancestor gives **bonuses and penalties**. **The penalties grow at the same pace as the bonuses.** The more plasmas, the stronger both.

<Unverified q="Q-013">The official guide doesn't list each ancestor's bonuses and penalties (they're shown in game, as "current / maximum"), or whether they have caps or diminishing returns beyond the 1,000-plasma limit.</Unverified>

## Distributing

<Steps>

1. Press **F12**. The PLASMA TYPES panel shows your free plasmas, distributed plasmas and presets.
2. Click an ancestor. The right column shows its **BONUSES** and **PENALTIES**.
3. Add plasmas with **+**, **+10**, a typed amount and **Set**, or **Max** (everything that fits).
4. Repeat for other ancestors if you're splitting.
5. Click **Save and Activate**. The bonuses only apply now.

</Steps>

**Clear** undoes unsaved changes for free.

<Aside type="caution">
Once saved, you **can't take plasmas out** of an ancestor, except with **Reset** (50 Sangricoins), which frees all of them. Read the penalties first.
</Aside>

## Presets <Label type="vip" />

A preset is a saved distribution of the same plasmas. With VIP you can build **3** and switch for free. Click *Create build/preset* on card 2 or 3. **Save** stores it, and **Save and Activate** also applies it. The card marked **IN USE** is active, and you can name a preset (up to 20 letters). If your VIP ends, the one in use stays active, presets 2 and 3 lock, and transfusions cost 10,000 again.

`.tf preset-carregar 2` activates saved preset 2 without the interface.
