## What changes

- **The PULSE button disappears.** Your level stays at **90**.
- **Every enemy you defeat gives Kill Points** instead of XP. The stronger the enemy, the more points, and XP bonuses also apply to points.
- **Saved Pulses become Kill Points** immediately.
- VIP Auto-Pulse stops by itself.
- The last Lost Book chapter (Book III, chapter 23) comes with transfusion 250.

Hover over your XP bar to see the month's points.

## The Progression ranking

Open the rankings with the **trophy** by the minimap, or `.top` → **PROGRESSION**.

| View | Ranks |
|---|---|
| Overall | Everyone, by Kill Points, then Transfusions, then Pulses, then Prestige, then Level |
| Current month | Only players who've finished 250, by Kill Points earned this month |
| Previous month | The same, for last month |

The month resets on the **1st**. Pulses converted to points count only in **Overall**. If *Current month* is empty, nobody has finished 250 or earned points this month.
