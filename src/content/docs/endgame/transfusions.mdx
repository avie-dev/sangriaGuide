## How to transfuse

1. Gather **10 Pulses** and leave the Ourosangue in your **Bank**.
2. The first time, the quest *The First Transfusion* arrives. Talk to **Nyxaris**, who unlocks transfusion 1.
3. Transfuse:

<PlayModes>
	<Fragment slot="interface">Click **TRANSFUSE** on the prestige bar (it appears when available).</Fragment>
	<Fragment slot="chat"><Command cmd=".tf transfundir">Transfuses immediately, without confirmation.</Command></Fragment>
</PlayModes>

You receive **10 free plasmas**. If you're short on Ourosangue, nothing is spent and your Pulses stay saved. VIP Auto-Prestige **never** transfuses for you.

## Sealed transfusions

<Aside type="caution">
Some transfusions arrive **sealed**: the TRANSFUSE button disappears and the game warns you. Go to Nyxaris, hear the chapter (an **Echo** quest), and the seal lifts.
</Aside>

Sealed: **transfusion 1** (via *The First Transfusion*) and every chapter transfusion: **5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40, 45, 50, 65, 80, 95, 110, 125, 140, 155, 170, 190, 205, 220, 235, 250**. See [Nyxaris](/quests/nyxaris/).

## Milestones

| Transfusions | What happens |
|---|---|
| 10 | **Luminance** achievement available (permanent sun immunity) |
| 30 | **Thanatos** achievement available (get back up at full health once an hour) |
| 250 | Pulses end; Kill Points begin ([After 250](/endgame/after-250-transfusions/)) |

The game's maximum is **2,500 plasmas** (250 × 10).

<Command cmd=".tf info">Shows your Pulses, transfusions done, free plasmas and each ancestor's plasmas.</Command>
