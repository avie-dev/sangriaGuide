## Evolving your class

Each class has **3 evolutions**, done in order. Each one is a quest from the class's Master that ends with a **tribute** taken from your Bank. Evolution bonuses stack and are permanent.

<CostTable id="class-evolution" />

<Steps>

1. With the class active, talk to its Master.
2. Choose the first evolution's option, for example *I want to begin the Initiate's Oath (Evolution 1).*
3. Complete the quest steps (track them in F3 or with `.quest`).
4. On the last step, choose *Pay 15,000 Ourosangue.* If you're short, the quest waits. Deposit and talk to the Master again.

</Steps>

## Unlocking another class

After *The Path of Blood*, any Master offers their class's journey, for example *Yes. I want to start the Mortak journey.* Finish the quest and the class is unlocked; you switch to it immediately. Unlocked classes are yours forever. Switching back to one costs 3,000 Ourosangue at its Master, and you keep everything: evolutions, Currents, variants and attributes.

To reach La Muerte, for example, you need Draekhar and Sepulkros to Evolution III and Carmesyne to Evolution II, which means unlocking and evolving those classes first.

## Extra quests along the way

- **Draekhar:** the five other Currents are quests from Master Kaelmor (*Variant* in his dialogue).
- **Mortak and Sepulkros:** the second variant is a Master quest with kills, two bosses, a night challenge, blood and more.
- **La Muerte, Sepulkros and Skarn:** locked summons unlock by defeating that creature while playing the class with a **Minion Collector** in your inventory. Collectors come from dungeon rewards.
