The four schools share the same 44 spells. Only the story, headquarters and NPCs differ, so pick whichever appeals to you. A school pays no currency or items.

## 1. Enrol

<Steps>

1. Go to the city and press **F11** (or `.school`). Click **Mark on map**. Sebastian is on the first floor of the city's main building.
2. Talk to him and choose **View the schools**.
3. Click **I'M INTERESTED** on the school you want. Enrolment is immediate. A full school shows *SCHOOL FULL*, so choose another or come back later.
4. Use your school's teleporter at the city teleport hub. Only students can pass.

</Steps>

## 2. Reach rank 1

You start at rank 0, with no spell. Tier 1 opens at rank 1 (800 points), and **two daily missions** get you there.

**Daily mission (+600):** at your headquarters, talk to your professor and choose *Today's tasks*. You get 3 simple tasks inside the headquarters. One mission per day, resetting at midnight (Brasília time).

## 3. Choose your spell

In **F11 → GRIMOIRE**, click **Select** on a spell. Only one is active at a time. It fires automatically with your basic attack, from any weapon, whenever its cooldown ends. Each rank shortens its cooldown by 5%.

## 4. School events

Three 20-minute events a day, Monday to Saturday (Brasília time), worth up to 800 points each:

| Days | Times |
|---|---|
| Monday, Tuesday | 10:00, 15:00, 20:00 |
| Wednesday, Thursday | 8:00, 17:00, 22:00 |
| Friday, Saturday | 11:00, 16:00, 21:00 |

The points your school earns during the week become **Sunday bonus** time from 19:00 (Brasília time), up to 3 hours at 10,000 points. During it, your pet capture chance doubles.

<Aside type="caution" title="Stay active">
Score at least once every 7 days to keep your enrolment. If you go 7 days without scoring, or 14 days without joining the server, you leave the school. Your rank and points are kept, so you can just enrol again.
</Aside>

<Command cmd=".school info">Shows your school, rank, points, active spell and time left to score.</Command>
