## Where gear comes from

**Dungeons.** Every win rolls a chance at a **Blue, Purple or Legendary weapon**, plus materials. Before entering, set your **affinity** at Mark: when a weapon drops, it has a 40% chance of being the type you chose. Affinity doesn't raise the chance of getting a weapon. Start with the Adventurer and Veteran tiers. See [Dungeons](/dungeons/).

**Outposts.** Rank III and above adds specialty loot. **Silverlight Hills** (Cassandra) specialises in armour and **Ruins of Mortium** (Velanna) in necklaces. At Rank V, the weekly at some outposts can even give a legendary weapon.

**The Market.** Other players sell gear in the city (press L). Legendary weapons have minimum prices: 5,000 Ourosangue for Blue, 10,000 for Purple and 20,000 for Orange (unique), plus 1,000 per fortification level and 2,000 per refinement. Purchases are instant, with no confirmation.

## Making gear stronger: fortification

Your crafting profession fortifies one kind of gear:

| Craft | Works on | Per fortification level |
|---|---|---|
| Blacksmith | Weapon | +2 Physical Power |
| Tailor | Chest, gloves, legs, boots | +9 Health per piece |
| Enchanter | Necklace | +2 Magic Power |

- You can fortify up to your **craft prestige** (at most 10). You need at least 1 prestige.
- It costs **2,000 Ourosangue per level gained**, the same whether you go one level at a time or straight up. The Tailor pays per piece.
- Fortification only goes up, and it's saved on the item. Each level also raises maximum durability.
- Open the panel from your mentor (*Open Fortification & Refinement panel*) or with `.blacksmith panel`, `.tailor panel` or `.enchanter panel`.

<Aside type="tip" title="Not the right craft?">
Hire another player through **Profession Services** (F4 → Services, or `.services`). Providers need 5+ prestiges in their craft. You pay their price plus the normal 2,000 per level. See [Stage 13](/walkthrough/13-advanced-enhancement/).
</Aside>
