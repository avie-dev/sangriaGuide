## How prestige works

At level 90, XP stops counting. Prestiging returns you to **level 1** and gives you one **permanent buff**, 20 in all, always in the same order. Buffs stack and are never lost. Prestiging doesn't slow your XP. **Vlad Parker** teaches prestige in the quest *The Cycle of Blood*, in the Winery (Adegaria) of the City of Sangria Falls.

## How to prestige

<PlayModes>
	<Fragment slot="interface">At level 90, click **PRESTIGE** on the Prestige Bar next to the HUD, or on the Profile (F1) below the XP bar. **It's instant, with no confirmation screen.**</Fragment>
	<Fragment slot="chat"><Command cmd=".level">Opens the character menu. Its Prestige option shows the cost.</Command><Command cmd=".prestige exp">Prestiges immediately, without confirmation.</Command></Fragment>
</PlayModes>

<Aside type="tip">
To see the cost first, type `.level`, or talk to Vlad Parker: *I want to prestige.* → *Character.* He only prestiges after you click **Confirm.**
</Aside>

| 5 | Spell Resistance +2% |

| 7 | Shield Efficiency +4% |
| 8 | Movement Speed +0.5 |

| 10 | Shapeshift Speed +0.2 |

In game, `.level buffs` lists them and marks the ones you have.

## Getting back to 90 quickly

- **Meditation:** 630,000 XP per hour (945,000 with VIP), at 0.35 Sangricoin per minute. From level 1 to 90 takes about **1h14** (50 minutes with VIP). Press F10 or type `.meditate`. See [Meditation](/meditation/).
- **Hunting:** V Bloods, groups and quests, as in [Stage 3](/walkthrough/03-early-levelling/). Quitina gives 2x XP for 30 minutes, but not while meditating.
