## Why Master and Nightmare

Every win returns 20% of the key's materials and rolls materials and a weapon chance. On **Master** and **Nightmare** there's also a **Special roll**: Perfect Gems, **Cristais** (pet attributes) or **Ressonador** (Sangrulho eggs). The panel at Mark shows each prize's chance by tier.

## Keys

| Tier | Recipe for 1 key | Other ways |
|---|---|---|
| Master | 500 Greater Stygian Shard + 15 Dark Silver Ingot | Fuse 3 Veteran keys; incursion II–III boss drop |
| Nightmare | 1,000 Greater Stygian Shard + 5 Power Core | Incursion II–III boss drop only (no fusion) |

**Greater Stygian Shards** come from **Rift Incursions II and III**. Each portal or gate destroyed gives 50 fragments to everyone in its circle, so a full rift gives 150. Their gate bosses also give each nearby player a draw for a ready-made key of any tier, delivered by Mail. High-tier keys are the rarest. Keys can't be crafted at a workbench, and dungeons themselves don't give keys.

## Finding a group

Open the **group board** (the two-people button by the minimap, or `.group`) and click the tier on the Dungeon row to mark your interest for 30 minutes. Players forming a group at the Events Hub see you and can **Call** you. Accept, then travel to the Hub through the city teleporter, and the invite arrives on its own. For dungeons, you need the tier's key before you mark.

## Running it

- Talk to **Mark** at the Events Hub, choose the tier, invite players near Mark, and click **Enter the Dungeon**. Every key is spent at that moment.
- The dungeon is drawn at random from the free ones. You have **15 minutes**. Enemies are level 90. Pets are put away for the whole run.
- Stay together: doors close 10 seconds after the first player goes through. If everyone goes down, it's a defeat. A loss or time-out gives no prize and the key isn't returned.
- Pressing X while downed means giving up. You leave with no reward, and your bag stays inside.
- Rewards arrive by Mail (F6). Keep 15 free inventory slots.
