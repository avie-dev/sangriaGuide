**Goal:** add refinements on top of fortification, and trade crafting services with other players.

<Requirements items={['A crafting profession with prestige (or a provider to hire)', 'Refining materials in your inventory']} />

## Refinement

Each piece accepts up to **5 refinements**. Each one rolls a random bonus: **+100, +200 or +300 durability** and **+1, +2 or +3 power** (the Tailor rolls +3, +5 or +7 Health instead). Refinement number *n* uses *n* of each material.

| Craft | Materials |
|---|---|
| Blacksmith | Perfect Ruby + Precious Stone |
| Tailor | Perfect Topaz + Exceptional Tailoring Component |
| Enchanter | Perfect Amethyst + Exceptional Magical Component |

- **REFINE AGAIN** re-rolls a row. You lose the old bonus and spend the materials again. A best-possible roll shows **MAX REFINEMENT**.
- **Refinement can't be undone.**

**Unlocking rows:** craft prestige 7 unlocks up to 3 refinements and prestige 9 up to 4. Your mentor's achievement (a long quest) unlocks the first 2 right away, and the 5th once you reach prestige 10.

## Where the components come from

Gathering feeds crafting. From **gathering prestige 5**, each trade can drop one component, with a small chance that improves up to prestige 10. No achievement is needed.

| Gathering trade | Drops | Used by |
|---|---|---|
| Miner | Precious Stone | Blacksmith |
| Fisher | Exceptional Magical Component | Enchanter |
| Woodcutter | Exceptional Tailoring Component | Tailor |

They also drop at the **Dunley Outpost** at its highest rank, and other players sell them on the Market. Perfect gems are among the Special rewards from Master and Nightmare dungeons ([Stage 14](/walkthrough/14-master-and-nightmare/)).

## Profession Services

Hire another player to fortify or refine a piece from a craft you don't have, or sell your own services. Open it with **F4 → Services** or `.services`.

- **Hiring:** anyone can. Fortification costs the provider's price plus the normal 2,000 Ourosangue per level gained. Refinement costs the provider's price × the refinement number, using your materials or theirs. The provider doesn't need to be online.
- **Selling:** needs **5+ prestiges** in your active craft. Publish your prices from F4 → Details → Provide Services. Since 30/09/2026, prices must stay within a floor and ceiling per level, a price can be lowered only once every 24 hours, and each provider takes at most 2 orders per week per prestige.

<Aside type="tip">
Your craft's limits apply to your own panel. A provider's limits apply when you hire them, and the 5th refinement needs a provider with the achievement and prestige 10.
</Aside>
