**Goal:** become a class, equip it properly and understand what makes its bonuses work.

<Requirements items={['The tutorial quest The Path of Blood, up to the Classes Hall step']} />

## 1. Choose your class

When *The Path of Blood* sends you to the Classes Hall, talk to the Master of the class you want, then click **Choose this class** and **Yes, I choose this class**. The first class is free. Unsure which one to pick? See [Choosing your first class](/getting-started/first-class/), which shows every class's weapons and a filterable list.

Three classes can't be chosen first: Sangrael, La Muerte and General need evolutions in other classes.

## 2. Equip the starter gear

You become the class immediately and receive its **iron weapons and a starter kit**. Anything that doesn't fit in your inventory goes to your **Mail** (F6 or `.mail`), so claim it.

<Aside type="caution" title="Hold the class weapon">
Class bonuses only apply with the class's weapon in hand. Draekhar and Sangrael use the game's **Spear** as their Staff. It works and looks like a staff for those classes.
</Aside>

## 3. Pick your variant or Current

- **Mortak** and **Sepulkros**: a *Choose your Variant* panel opens (Unshakable or Relentless; Summoner or Reaper). The other variant is a later Master quest.
- **Draekhar**: choose your first of 6 Currents. The others are later quests from Kaelmor.
- **La Muerte, Sepulkros and Skarn** summon an ally with their basic attack. Choose it with the round button next to your class crest.

Closed a panel without choosing? Click the round button next to your class crest.

## 4. Set your mastery and blood attributes

Each class uses 3 weapon attributes and 3 blood attributes, which grow with your weapon's and blood's level and prestige. They come pre-selected, and changing them is free.

<Steps>

1. Open your class info (click the class crest or F1 → See more) and go to the **MASTERY & BLOOD** tab.
2. Check 3 weapon attributes and 3 blood attributes. To pick a different one, uncheck one first.
3. Click **CONFIRM SELECTION**. RESET returns to the class default.

</Steps>

Without the interface: `.weapon` → *Change mastery attributes* and `.blood` → *Change lineage attributes*.

## You're done when

You have a class, its weapon in hand, and your attributes set. Remember that switching to another unlocked class later costs 3,000 Ourosangue and keeps all your progress.
