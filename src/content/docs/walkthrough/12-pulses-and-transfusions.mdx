Your guide is **Nyxaris, the Last Oracle**, in her laboratory in Farbane. Her quests arrive on their own.

## Pulses

At prestige 20, the PRESTIGE button becomes **PULSE** when you reach level 90. A Pulse returns you to level 1 and adds +1 Pulse. It costs nothing, and you keep your prestige, buffs and plasmas. The first time, return to Nyxaris to close *The Call of Blood*. Pulses stack without limit.

## Transfusions

<CostTable id="transfusion" />

Gather **10 Pulses** and leave the Ourosangue in your Bank. The first time, the quest *The First Transfusion* arrives, and Nyxaris unlocks transfusion 1. Then click **TRANSFUSE** on the prestige bar for 10 free plasmas. If you're short on Ourosangue, nothing is spent. Your Pulses stay saved.

<Aside type="caution" title="Sealed transfusions">
Some transfusions arrive **sealed**: the TRANSFUSE button vanishes and the game warns you. Go to Nyxaris and hear the chapter she tells (an *Echo* quest). The seal lifts and the chapter goes into your **Lost Book**. Book I's chapters come at transfusions 5, 10, 15 … 50; Book II's at 65, 80 … 170; Book III's at 190, 205, 220, 235 and 250.
</Aside>

## Plasmas

Press **F12** (or `.tf`). Click an ancestor (Nosferatu, Vladimir, Cazador, Barnabas, Akasha, Carmilla, Selene or Katherine) to see its **bonuses and penalties**. Both grow at the same pace as you add plasmas. Add plasmas with **+**, **+10**, a typed number or **Max**, then click **Save and Activate**.

- Once saved, plasmas can't be taken out of an ancestor except by **Reset** (50 Sangricoins). **Clear** undoes unsaved changes for free.
- At most 1,000 plasmas per ancestor. The game's maximum is 2,500 plasmas, so nobody fills all 8.
- Without VIP you have 1 preset; with VIP, 3 that you can switch between for free.

The guides don't publish whether ancestor bonuses have caps or diminishing returns (see [Q-013](/about/open-questions/#q-013)), so read the penalties before saving.

<Command cmd=".tf info">Shows your Pulses, transfusions done, free plasmas and how much is in each ancestor.</Command>

## If you use Auto-Prestige

Auto pulse is **on by default** at prestige 20. You can cap how many Pulses it gives, or switch it off, in the Auto-Prestige LIMITS board. It never transfuses for you.
