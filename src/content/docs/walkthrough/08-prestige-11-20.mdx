## Cost

Costs rise with each prestige. The twentieth costs <Currency id="ourosangue" amount={4467} />, and all twenty together cost <Currency id="ourosangue" amount={22760} />. The guide doesn't publish the individual costs for prestiges 2 to 19 (see [Q-017](/about/open-questions/#q-017)). Type `.level` before each one to see the next cost.

## Auto-Prestige <Label type="vip" />

VIPs can let **Auto-Prestige** prestige the character, weapon in hand, active blood and summoned pet automatically, the moment each hits max level. It works anywhere while you're online, and you don't need to be meditating. It charges the normal cost from your Bank, and pet prestige is free.

- Open it with **F8 → Auto-Prestige → CONFIGURE**, or `.ap panel`. Turn each type on separately.
- Set a **target** (stop at prestige N, or MAX for 20) and a **spending cap** in Ourosangue.

<Aside type="caution" title="Auto pulse is on by default">
At prestige 20, the Character type starts giving **Pulses** at level 90 (Stage 12). If you're not ready, turn **Auto pulse** off in the LIMITS board before you reach prestige 20. Auto-Prestige never transfuses; that's always manual.
</Aside>

## What prestige 20 unlocks

- **Vampiric Schools.** Enrolment opens at prestige 20. See [Stage 11](/walkthrough/11-schools/).
- **Pulses.** The **PRESTIGE** button becomes **GIVE PULSE**, and Nyxaris sends you the quest *The Call of Blood*. See [Stage 12](/walkthrough/12-pulses-and-transfusions/).
