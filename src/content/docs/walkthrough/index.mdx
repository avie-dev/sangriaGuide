A chronological route from your first login to an optimised endgame character, in 15 stages.

Each stage lists what you need first, the steps, the costs from the official guides, and a checklist saved in your browser. The order follows the real unlock dependencies in the official guides. Some stages run in parallel: professions and income (Stage 4) keep going for the whole game, and class evolutions (Stage 9) can start as soon as you can afford them.
