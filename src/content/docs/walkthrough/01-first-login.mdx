**Goal:** get into the game and complete the starter tutorial, which ends in the City of Sangria Falls.

<Requirements items={['V Rising on Steam (PC)', 'Optional: the ScarletInterface, installed with the Sangria Falls Launcher']} />

## 1. Prepare your client

Decide whether you'll use the interface. It's free and optional, and the official guides say the experience is much better with it. Install it before you join: [Installing the interface](/getting-started/interface/). If you'd rather not, read [Playing without the interface](/getting-started/playing-without-the-interface/).

## 2. Join and set the language

Open V Rising (from the launcher's PLAY button if you use it), search for **Sangria Falls** in the server list and join. See [Joining the server](/getting-started/joining/).

With the interface, the server uses your game's language. Without it, three NPCs ask you to choose when you leave the coffin. Either way, you can switch at any time:

<Command cmd=".lang en">Switches the server to English.</Command>

## 3. Play the tutorial

The first quest, *Where am I?!*, is active from the start. The tutorial has 8 quests. It teaches your level, the classes, prestige, a gathering profession, ways to earn Ourosangue and the Sangrulhos, and ends in the city. Follow the objective on the right of the screen (or `.quest` in chat) and talk to NPCs with **F**.

You choose your **free first class** during the tutorial, in the Classes Hall. That's [Stage 2](/walkthrough/02-first-class/). Details: [The Path of Blood tutorial](/getting-started/path-of-blood/).

<Aside type="tip">
Every tutorial step pays XP, and choosing your class starts a **3-day double character XP** welcome gift. Don't rush past the tutorial.
</Aside>

## You're done when

You're standing at the teleport hub in the City of Sangria Falls with a class. You start with 25 Sangricoins and earn 10,000 Ourosangue during the tutorial, so check your Bank (F5 or `.menu bank`).
