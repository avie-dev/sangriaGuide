## The long goal: 250 transfusions

Transfusions are capped at **250**. When you complete them:

- Pulses end, the PULSE button disappears, and your level stays at 90.
- **Every enemy you defeat gives Kill Points** instead of XP, with stronger enemies giving more. Any Pulses you had saved turn into points. XP bonuses also apply to Kill Points.
- The final Lost Book chapter arrives with transfusion 250.

## The Progression ranking

Open the rankings with the **trophy** button by the minimap, or `.top`. The PROGRESSION tab ranks players by **Kill Points, then Transfusions, then Pulses, then Prestige, then Level**. Before 250, more transfusions put you ahead. The **Current month** and **Previous month** views rank only players who've finished all 250, by Kill Points earned that month, and they reset on the 1st. The DAMAGE and BOSSES tabs count lifetime damage by weapon or class, and open-world boss kills.

## Tower of the Immortals

An endless solo challenge with waves of bosses, each wave hitting 10% harder than the first. It's free and open 24 hours, entered through the Herald at the Events Hub.

- **Each class has its own weekly ranking.** Your attempt counts for the class you entered with. The highest wave wins, and ties go to the faster time.
- **Weekly prizes, per class:** 1st place 150 Sangricoins + 20,000 Ourosangue; 2nd 100 + 15,000; 3rd 50 + 10,000. Each player takes only one slot, in their best-placed class.
- **Participation prize:** reach wave 20 ten times in a week (any class) for 200 Sangricoins, regardless of placement.
- The week resets on Monday at 00:00 (server clock).

## Tuning plasmas

Plasma bonuses come with penalties that grow at the same rate. Moving plasmas costs a 50 Sangricoin reset, and VIPs can keep 3 presets. <Label type="vip" />
