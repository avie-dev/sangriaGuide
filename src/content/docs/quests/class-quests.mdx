Class quests come from the **Class Masters** in the Classes Hall. They're among the few quests you **can abandon**, so step skipping is also available on their counting steps.

## Quest types

| Quest | Requirement | Ends with |
|---|---|---|
| **Unlock journey** (e.g. *Yes. I want to start the Mortak journey.*) | *The Path of Blood* finished; prerequisites for Sangrael, La Muerte, General | The class unlocked and active |
| **Evolutions I–III** | The class active; done in order | Tributes of 15,000 / 30,000 / 45,000 Ourosangue |
| **Draekhar Currents** (5, shown as *Variant* by Kaelmor) | Draekhar | The Current unlocked |
| **Second variant** (Mortak or Sepulkros) | The class active | The variant unlocked; you stay on your current one |
| **Mortak achievements** | Mortak active | *Furious Roar* or *Collector of Suffering* |

## Official quest names (Portuguese)

Unlock journeys, from the class pages: *Provação de Mortak*, *A Flecha da Névoa* (Noctarin), *A Lança Alugada da Luz* (Bastien), *A Pistola Prateada* (Carmesyne), *O Uivo entre as Ruínas* (Skarn), *O Arquitetador da Pós-Vida* (Sepulkros), *O Mapa Começa Aqui* (Draekhar), *A Arte da Sobrevivência Silenciosa* (Umbrellis), *O Voto do Plantão* (Sangrael), *A Mãe dos Lamentos* (La Muerte), *A General Carmesim* (General).

Each class page lists its three evolution journeys. See [Classes](/classes/).

**Variants:** *O Caminho do Inabalável* / *O Caminho do Implacável* (Mortak) and *O Caminho do Invocador* / *O Caminho do Ceifador* (Sepulkros). The second variant's quest asks for kills, two bosses, a night challenge and blood, among other steps.

**Draekhar Currents:** *O Traço que Não Derrete* (Glacial), *A Linha que se Rompe* (Chaos), *A Frequência da Tempestade* (Storm), *A Seiva Corrompida* (Corrupted), *A Cartografia Sagrada* (Luminous), *A Corrente que Devolve* (Blood).

## Mortak's achievements

With Razor Mortak, choose the journey of the **Unshakable Will** (*Furious Roar*: Blood Rage taunts within 20 m) or of **Mortak's Suffering** (*Collector of Suffering*: defeated units become suffering, which Blood Rage turns into weapon durability). The Unshakable Will asks for:

- 500 Silverlight enemies and 500 Mortium enemies
- 5 Illusional Arenas won through the last wave
- 400 Radium Alloy and 300 Dark Silver Ingots delivered to Razor
- 100 Frost Magic Bosses
- 2,000 Oakveil enemies
- 2 dungeons on Nightmare

Both work only with Mortak as your active class.

<Aside type="tip">
Short on Ourosangue at an evolution's last step? The quest waits. Deposit and talk to the Master again.
</Aside>
