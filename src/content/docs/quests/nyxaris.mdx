## Echo quests

When your next transfusion brings a chapter, an **Echo** quest appears in F3, for example *Echo 1 — The Age Before Blood*. It marks Nyxaris on your map. Talk to her to hear the chapter, then talk again for the ending. Chat announces that the chapter has entered the **Lost Book** and the transfusion's seal is lifted.

- There are **23 Echoes**, one per chapter.
- **Echo quests can't be abandoned**, and the sealed transfusion stays locked until you finish one.
- Chapters always come in order. If you've already passed several chapter transfusions, Nyxaris gives you one Echo after another until you're caught up.

| Book | Chapters | Transfusions |
|---|---|---|
| I | 1–10 | 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40, 45, 50 |
| II | 11–18 | 65, 80, 95, 110, 125, 140, 155, 170 |
| III | 19–23 | 190, 205, 220, 235, 250 |

The other transfusions aren't sealed, except transfusion 1, which *The First Transfusion* unlocks.
