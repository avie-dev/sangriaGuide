A quest is a sequence of steps: talk to someone, defeat enemies, deliver items, go somewhere. Most are given by NPCs in the City of Sangria Falls, and some arrive on their own when you hit a milestone. The current step's objective is marked on the map. Rewards vary: XP, items, Ourosangue, even permanent bonuses.

## Opening

<PlayModes>
	<Fragment slot="interface">Press **F3** (or the quests button by the minimap, or `.quest panel`). **ACTIVE** and **COMPLETED** tabs are on the left (completed quests show their date), and details are on the right: **JOURNEY** (all steps) and **REWARD**.</Fragment>
	<Fragment slot="chat">
		<Command cmd=".quest">Lists your active quests and each one's current step.</Command>
		<Command cmd=".menu quests">The quests menu: pin, abandon, skip a step.</Command>
	</Fragment>
</PlayModes>

Quests are grouped by origin, each group with a coloured title and a count: **CLASS, PROFESSIONS, OUTPOSTS, SCHOOL, ACHIEVEMENTS, DUNGEONS, CHRONICLES, VAULT, FIRST STEPS, OTHER**. Click a group to collapse it (the panel remembers). Under each name you see where it comes from. Daily, Weekly and Hunt quests show the time to reset.

## Talking to NPCs

Interact with **F**. Click **Continue** to advance, **Back** to go back one line, and **Finish** on the last line. **A step only counts when you reach Finish.** Closing early with *End conversation* or the X means talking to them again.

NPCs with services open a menu. If they're in one of your quests, the first option is *Continue: quest name*. *See available quests* lists what they offer. An NPC in two of your quests asks which one you want to handle.

## Pinning

F3 → the quest → **Pin to HUD** (click again to unpin). The pinned quest rises to the top of the on-screen list and shows first within its group.

## Delivering items

Carry the items and talk to the quest's NPC. The **Delivery** panel shows only the items in your bag that the quest accepts. Click them (or **Select all**) and then **Deliver**. You can deliver a bit at a time. The weapon in your hand never appears there. For "one of each piece" quests, only undelivered pieces are accepted, worn pieces must be taken off first, and a *Missing:* line shows what's left.

## Abandoning and skipping

- **Abandon:** F3 → the quest → **Abandon** → *Yes, abandon*. Only a few quests allow it, such as class quests. The tutorial, story, achievements, dungeons, Outposts and profession commissions don't. Abandoning erases progress.
- **Skip a step:** a **Skip for…** button on the current step, priced in Sangricoins. It's only available on abandonable quests, only on counting steps (for example "Kill 40 enemies"), and only with at least 30% done. Talk-to-NPC steps never skip.

<Aside type="caution">
Skipping spends Sangricoins from your Bank, and there's no refund.
</Aside>

## Resets

| Quest type | Resets |
|---|---|
| Daily | Midnight (Brasília time) |
| Weekly and Hunt | Monday midnight (Brasília time) |

An unfinished quest disappears at the reset, and chat tells you.

## Common questions

**I died mid-quest.** Progress is generally kept. Only some challenges restart when you go down, such as survive-for-a-time ones.

**The NPC just repeats a line.** You already have the quest, or you're missing a requirement. Check your current step in F3.

**The on-screen quest list vanished.** Inside a dungeon it gives way to the dungeon board, and it comes back when you leave.
