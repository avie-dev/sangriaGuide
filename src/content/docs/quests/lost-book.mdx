The **Lost Book** is the story of Sangria Falls: **23 chapters** in three books, with text and illustrations. Reading costs nothing and **gives no attribute bonus**. Unlocked chapters stay in the book to reread.

| Book | Chapters |
|---|---|
| BOOK I | 1–10 |
| BOOK II | 11–18 |
| BOOK III | 19–23 |

## Unlocking chapters

Each chapter comes with one of Nyxaris's transfusions. First reach prestige 20 (*The Call of Blood*) and gather 10 Pulses (*The First Transfusion*). Then each chapter transfusion brings an **Echo** quest. See [Nyxaris](/quests/nyxaris/) for the schedule.

## Reading

<PlayModes>
	<Fragment slot="interface">Click the **book button** by the minimap (it appears after the first tutorial quest). The panel *THE LOST BOOKS OF SANGRIA FALLS* opens. Choose BOOK I, II or III. Unlocked chapters show **READ**, and locked ones show **SEALED**. Use **Next** and **Previous** to turn pages, and **End** or **Index** to return to the chapters. Close it with the X, since there's no hotkey. If a tab says *Book not found*, you haven't unlocked any of its chapters yet.</Fragment>
	<Fragment slot="chat"><Command cmd=".codex">Opens the book as a reading menu in chat, with next and previous pages. The text is the same as in the interface, without covers or illustrations.</Command></Fragment>
</PlayModes>

Clicked READ and got "sealed"? You haven't heard that chapter yet. Do its Echo quest with Nyxaris.
