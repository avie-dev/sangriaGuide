<Aside type="caution">
Choosing an outpost uses up the day's (or week's) slot. You **can't switch or abandon** an Outpost mission. You can take the daily at one outpost and the weekly at another.
</Aside>

## Taking a mission

<Steps>

1. In the city, press **F** at a **Mission Board** (the chalkboard labelled *Quadro de Missões*), or type `.outpost` anywhere. It shows the 7 commanders and your rank with each. The one asking for help today is marked *Needs help · +50%*.
2. Click **Track** on a commander to mark them on your map. The board only shows the way, and the commander hands out the mission.
3. Go to the outpost and talk to the commander. The COMMAND POST panel shows the DAILY and WEEKLY missions, their pay and time left.
4. Click **Accept daily mission** or **Accept weekly mission**. Complete the steps (F3), and the pay goes into your Bank automatically.

</Steps>

## Ranks

Reputation is **per outpost**. Rising in Gloomrot changes nothing in Oakveil.

| Rank | Reputation | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| I · Outsider | 0 | Ourosangue and Quitina on every mission |
| II · Recognized | 100 | More Ourosangue |
| III · Ally | 250 | The region's specialty loot |
| IV · Honored | 500 | More specialty loot, better materials |
| V · Confidant | 900 | The most loot, plus a chance at a rare item; at some outposts the weekly can give a legendary weapon |

On the daily, specialty loot is a chance. On the weekly, it always comes. Doing the daily and weekly at the same outpost takes **about a month** to reach Rank V.

## Pay

| Mission | Base pay |
|---|---|
| Daily | 1 Quitina + 1,500–2,500 Ourosangue (by rank) |
| Weekly | 4 Quitina + 75 Sangricoins + 7,500–10,000 Ourosangue (by rank) |

## The seven outposts

| Outpost | Commander | Specialty |
|---|---|---|
| Farbane Woods | Ekundayo | Gathering: woods, ores, fish |
| Dunley Farmlands | Alistair | Assorted loot (refining components at top rank) |
| Ruins of Mortium | Velanna | Necklaces |
| Gloomrot | Moira | Crafting materials and weapon repair |
| Cursed Forest | Yharn | More Ourosangue: base pay up to 4,000 daily and 15,000 weekly |
| Oakveil Woodlands | Eir | Pets, souls and Cristais (and Vampiric Dust quests) |
| Silverlight Hills | Cassandra | Armour |

The commander's panel shows what each rank can give under *Specialization loot*.

## Which bosses?

Requested bosses depend on your prestige. At **prestige 0 and below level 50**, missions ask for **Farbane's V Bloods**, and the enemies spawned for it match your level (later they spawn above it). So start at Farbane. There, while you have up to 1 transfusion, bosses stop at names like Cyril, Domina and Ziva. The "needs help" outpost is personal and changes daily.
