## The Boss Tracker

It covers the **open-world** V Bloods only, not dungeon, arena, tower or incursion bosses. It's free, has no requirement, and works anywhere. It shows each boss as **alive** (green) or with a **countdown** (red), the same timer as the game's Blood Altar. During the countdown, the boss is already in its spot as a red shape you can't attack yet.

<PlayModes>
	<Fragment slot="interface">
		Click the **minimap gear** → **Boss Tracker** card → **Manage**. Bosses are listed by Act 1–4, alphabetically. A ×3 means the boss spawns in 3 places and shows alive if any one is.

		- **Pin** a boss (the pin left of its name) to add it to the on-screen **BOSSES** panel, which updates every second. Pin up to **8**, and **Unpin all** clears the list. Pins are saved between sessions.
		- Drag the panel to move it, and use the sun icon for transparency (100/80/50/30%). Size (P, M, G, GG) and opacity are on the gear's Boss Tracker card. **X** hides it, and **Show HUD** brings it back.
	</Fragment>
	<Fragment slot="chat">
		<Command cmd=".boss tempo">Lists the bosses spawning right now and each one's time.</Command>
		<Command cmd=".boss tempo tristan">Shows whether one boss is alive or how long until it's back.</Command>
	</Fragment>
</PlayModes>

<Aside type="note">
The tracker tells you **when**, not **where**. Use the game's **Blood Altar** to track the way. You can't teleport into a boss's fight area, but you can teleport out.
</Aside>

## "It said alive, but the boss isn't there"

Someone just killed it. The countdown only starts when the red shape appears, which needs a player nearby. Wait there, and the shape appears and the timer starts.

## What V Bloods give

| Reward | Detail |
|---|---|
| Character XP | Much more than normal creatures |
| Weapon and blood XP | More for weapon mastery; blood lineage by the boss's level |
| Battle Pass XP | 2–10 per open-world V Blood, not split |
| Pet | 1% capture chance (a soul if you already own it); some bosses can't be captured |
| Soul Shards | Solarus, Megara, Dracula, Talzur and Adam: a chance by mail for the killer and anyone near the body |
| Boss ranking | Only open-world kills count |
