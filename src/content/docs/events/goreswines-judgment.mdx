The reward is rolled within its range for each player, and **prisoners get the full prize too**. There's only **one rewarded victory per day**. The three windows exist for people who play at different times.

## Joining

1. Talk to **Mordekai** and click **APPEAR IN COURT**. You go to the Waiting Room.
2. The first to appear becomes the leader, and the server is told a session has opened.
3. **With 3 players**, the leader can click **START**. If they don't, the session starts by itself 3 minutes after the third player arrives. **With 5**, it starts at once.

<Aside type="caution" title="30 seconds to enter">
When the session starts, the gates open and you have **30 seconds** to enter the arena. Anyone left behind returns to the Hub with no prize. It doesn't use up your victory, so just appear for the next session.
</Aside>

## The fight

- Goreswine has **650,000 health**, the same for 3 or 5 players.
- **Every minute**, he releases a court guard from a cell, so long fights get crowded.
- The central **pillar** attacks the group as Goreswine loses health.
- **Fire** barely hurts him, and **knockback** doesn't move him.
- Group members are **allies**: your hits don't hurt each other.
- Each player can have **at most 4 summons** alive, and a fifth vanishes as it's born.

## Prison, not death

- Fall, and you're **jailed in a cell** at full health but **silenced** (you can't cast). You can watch through the bars.
- A free teammate **hits your cell door from outside**. 15 seconds later the door opens for **5 seconds**, so walk out. Miss it and you're jailed again. Knocking from the inside doesn't work.

## Ending

- **Victory:** everyone (prisoners included) is paid and returns to the Hub, and a damage ranking appears.
- **Defeat:** everyone is jailed or the 15 minutes run out. No prize, but nothing is blocked, so appear again while the window is open.

## Help runs

After your rewarded win, you can appear to help (**APPEAR (HELP, NO REWARD)**) until you reach **3 wins** that day. Then the stamp reads *SENTENCE SERVED*. Check exactly what you earned in your Bank statement, on the line labelled *Goreswine's Judgment*.
