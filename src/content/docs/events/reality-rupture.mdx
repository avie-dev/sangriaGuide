## Rewards (per player, not split)

| Difficulty | Sangricoins | Ourosangue |
|---|---|---|
| I · Adventurer | 15 | 3,000 |
| II · Veteran | 20 | 4,000 |
| III · Master | 25 | 5,000 |
| IV · Nightmare | 30 | 6,000 |

A win also gives **20–70 Battle Pass XP**, depending on difficulty. Rewards go straight to your Bank.

<Aside type="tip">
Only **one rewarded win per window**, so pick the hardest difficulty your group can beat on the first try.
</Aside>

## Entering

<Steps>

1. Go to the Events Hub and talk to **Orek**. The panel shows the time to opening and the NEXT SOVEREIGN when closed. You can set up early, and your choice is saved.
2. Choose a difficulty card (Difficulty I–IV) and click **SELECT**. In a group, **the leader's difficulty applies**. Anyone who has never chosen enters on Veteran.
3. To group up, everyone stands in the Hub. The leader clicks **INVITE** in PLAYERS IN THE HUB, and the invited player clicks **Cross over** within 30 seconds.
4. With the window open, the leader clicks **ENTER THE RUPTURA**.

</Steps>

If anyone has a blocker, nobody enters, and the leader sees who and why in chat. Common blockers: below level 90, outside the Hub, wounded, in combat, in bat form. There are 3 arenas, so if all are busy, wait.

## The fight

- In the 30 seconds before the Sovereign arrives, drink potions, summon your pet, and **get out of the centre**, where it spawns.
- The Sovereign is the same for 1 or 4 players. **Group members are allies, and your hits don't hurt each other.**
- If you go down, you wait for a revive. Everyone down, or time out, is a loss (solo, going down is a loss). You can retry immediately while the window is open.
- If the window closes mid-fight, the fight continues and the win counts.

<Aside type="danger">
Don't press X while downed. That's giving up: you really die, get no reward, and your item bag drops where you fell.
</Aside>

## Help runs

After your rewarded win, the button becomes **ENTER (HELP, NO REWARD)**. You can help others until you reach **3 wins** in that window. A lost help run doesn't use a slot. The reward returns in the next window.

The panel's **?** opens *HOW THE RUPTURA WORKS*.
