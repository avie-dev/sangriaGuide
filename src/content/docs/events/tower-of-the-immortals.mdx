## The climb

- The first wave arrives 7 seconds after entering, and each next wave 10 seconds after the last.
- **Each wave adds +10% damage** over the first, with no limit (wave 11 hits twice as hard).
- Every **15 waves**, you go up a floor. On the 5th floor, waves continue until you fall.

| Waves | Bosses |
|---|---|
| 1–10 | One weak boss |
| 11–20 | One strong boss |
| 21–40 | One strong + one weak |
| 41+ | Two strong |

**There's no revive.** Fall, and the run ends. Falling off the edge just puts you back on the floor, with no penalty. You return to the Hub at full health, and a summary appears (class, wave, floor, time, damage, bosses defeated). You can't leave mid-run by command, and a disconnect ends the run.

## Rankings

- **11 weekly rankings, one per class.** Your run counts for the class **you entered with**, so pick it before entering.
- Within a class, the **highest wave** wins, and ties go to the **shortest time**.
- **One slot per player:** you appear only in the class where your *placement* is best. For example, 3rd with Bastien (wave 50) and 2nd with Mortak (wave 20) puts your slot at 2nd with Mortak. Your records in other classes stay saved.
- Tabs: CURRENT WEEK, PREVIOUS WEEK, ALL-TIME (with an **All** view of each player's best).

## Prizes

| Place (per class) | Sangricoins | Ourosangue |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | 150 | 20,000 |
| 2nd | 100 | 15,000 |
| 3rd | 50 | 10,000 |

Up to 33 players are rewarded each week. **Participation:** reach **wave 20 ten times** in a week, with any classes (progress adds up), for **200 Sangricoins**, once a week. Reaching wave 20 means winning the first 19, and you can earn both prizes.

Prizes go straight to your Bank, even offline, when the week closes. That happens the first time anyone uses the Tower or opens the ranking after **Monday 00:00 (server clock)**, or at a server restart.

<Unverified q="Q-003">The Tower guide says "server clock" for the Monday reset, while other guides say Brasília time. Whether they're the same isn't stated.</Unverified>

## Other rewards

Each boss gives a little **Battle Pass XP** (half the open-world value, and only to whoever got the kill). If you already own that boss as a pet, it can give a **soul**. Bosses drop no items, and the Tower never gives a new pet.

<Aside type="tip">
Entry is greyed out if you're outside the Hub, wounded, in combat, in bat form, classless, mid class-test, or all three towers are busy (**Free towers: 0/3**).
</Aside>
