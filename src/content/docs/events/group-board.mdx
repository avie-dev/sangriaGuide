The group board finds company for the four Events Hub events: **Dungeon, Rift of Reality, Illusory Arena and Judgment of Goreswine**. It's free and works anywhere on the map.

<Command cmd=".group">Opens the group board (also the round two-people button by the minimap, next to the Battle Pass).</Command>

## Marking interest

- **Dungeon:** click the tier (Adventurer, Veteran, Master or Nightmare). That click marks you, so **don't click MARKED afterwards**, or you'll unmark yourself. The row tells you whether you have that tier's key, and you need it for the invite to work.
- **Other events:** click **MARK**, and it shows MARKED with the expiry time below.
- A mark lasts **30 minutes**, and opening the board renews it. It disappears when you enter the event, disconnect or decline a call.
- Each event has its own mark, so you can mark several. Each row shows how many players are marked.

## Answering a call

<Steps>

1. A card appears on the right with the caller, the event and the slots left. It closes after a minute.
2. Click **On my way** (or **Not now** to remove your interest in that event).
3. Travel to the Events Hub through the city teleporter. **Nobody is teleported for you.**
4. The group invite arrives on its own when you arrive.

</Steps>

## Calling people (from the Hub)

Open the event's panel at its NPC. The **LOOKING FOR GROUP** column lists marked players (grouped by tier for dungeons). Click **Call** next to anyone outside the Hub. You can call the same person again after 2 minutes, and anyone called keeps their mark for at least 10 minutes. For the **Judgment**, which has no invites, **Call everyone** notifies everybody.

At the Hub, the event panel's own **MARK MY INTEREST** button (or **MARK · NIGHTMARE** and so on for dungeons) marks you without opening the board.

## Called but no invite?

The caller may have left or the group filled up. For the Rift, the window may have closed. For dungeons, the group may be a different tier, or the leader may lack the key for your tier. Chat tells you when an invite doesn't go out.
