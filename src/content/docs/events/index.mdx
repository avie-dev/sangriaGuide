| Event | Players | When | Entry | Main reward |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Reality Rupture](/events/reality-rupture/) | 1–4 | 06:00–11:20, 12:00–17:20, 18:00–23:20 | Free, level 90 | 15–30 Sangricoins + 3,000–6,000 Ourosangue per window |
| [Goreswine's Judgment](/events/goreswines-judgment/) | 3–5 | 00:15–11:30, 12:15–17:30, 18:15–23:30 | Free, level 90 | 20–30 Sangricoins + 2,500–3,500 Ourosangue once a day |
| [Illusory Arena](/events/illusory-arena/) | 1–2 | 24 hours | 2,000 / 8,000 Ourosangue | 2.5× character XP; 5% chance of Ressonador or Pet Soul |
| [Tower of the Immortals](/events/tower-of-the-immortals/) | 1 | 24 hours | Free, a class | Weekly per-class ranking prizes; 200 Sangricoins participation |
| [Dungeons](/dungeons/) | 2–4 | Any time | A key | Weapons, materials, Special roll |
| [Rift Incursions](/events/rift-incursions/) | Open world | Automatic | Eye of Twilight in your castle | Key fragments; key drops |

Times are **Brasília time**. The Rupture, Judgment, Arena, Tower and Dungeons all start with an NPC at the **Events Hub** (city teleport hub), and their panels only open there. Use the [group board](/events/group-board/) to find people.

**Help runs:** after your rewarded win, you can still enter the Rupture (per window) or the Judgment (per day) to help others, without reward, up to 3 wins in total.
