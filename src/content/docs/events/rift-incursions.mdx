Incursions are game events that happen on their own from time to time. There's no panel or command. When one starts, the **ACTIVE EVENTS** board on the right shows the *Rift Incursion* line, the recommended level (for example *Level 57+*), the time left, and a seal for the difficulty **I, II or III**. The board hides inside dungeons and other closed events.

## How to take part

1. Open the map, find the rift, and go there.
2. Each **portal** has a circle on the ground. Fight the rift's creatures inside it until the portal falls.
3. When a gate's portals fall, the **gate** appears with its circle and boss. Defeat the boss.
4. Each portal or gate destroyed pays **50 fragments** straight into the inventory of everyone in its circle. Chat shows **[INCURSION]**.

A full rift has **2 portals and 1 gate**, so **150 fragments** if you were at all three. When the gate boss dies, the fragments go to the killer, everyone in the circle, and anyone near the body, even if the fight moved outside the circle.

<Aside type="caution">
Be **on the ground, inside the circle** when the portal falls. With a full inventory, only what fits goes in. If the incursion ends by time, the portal closes and pays nothing.
</Aside>

## Fragments

| Incursion | Pays | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| I (weakest) | Stygian Shard | Adventurer and Veteran keys |
| II and III | Greater Stygian Shard | Master and Nightmare keys |

## Gate bosses and keys

The gate boss is a rift version of a known V Blood. When it dies, each player in the circle or near the body gets **their own draw** for a dungeon key (at most one key per player per boss). The killer is always in the draw. Keys arrive by mail from *The Order of the Observers*. The chance is small.

| Incursion | Bosses | Possible key |
|---|---|---|
| I | Lidia, Keely, Rufus, Quincey, Vincent, Goreswine, Tristan, Kriig, Polora | Adventurer or Veteran |
| II and III | Frostmaw, Terrorclaw, Willfred, Ungora, Jade, Foulrot, Domina, Angram, Octavian, Leandra, Bane, Ben | Any tier (high tiers rarest) |

These bosses can also become **pets** (Primordial category). A boss summoned in your castle with the Stygian Summoning Circle (Blood Soul ritual) **doesn't** give a key.
