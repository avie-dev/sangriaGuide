The official guides mention one seasonal event in detail, **Sangrisween 2026**. Other events aren't documented. This page will grow as they are.
</Aside>

## Sangrisween 2026

What the guides mention:

- **Event caves**, including Nightmare (T3) caves. These don't count towards the dungeon medals.
- **Haunted Candies** to earn.
- **Hallo's shop**: talk to Hallo or type `.sw shop`. Players without the interface can also see and redeem it on the website panel's **Extension** tab.
- **Exclusive portraits** from the Sangrisween shop, shown first with an orange ring in the portrait picker.
- A **Sangrisween Panel** HUD box, shown during the event.

### Event medals (Feats → Events tab)

| Medal | For |
|---|---|
| Haunt Hunter | Creatures defeated in the Sangrisween caves |
| Cursed Harvest | Haunted Candies earned |
| Explorer of the Beyond | Sangrisween caves completed |
| Living Nightmare | Nightmare caves (T3) completed |
| Nightmare Survivor (Legendary) | Complete all 10 different event caves |
| Lord of Sangrisween (Legendary) | Reach Gold on the first 4 |

These can only be earned during the event but stay forever. They unlock titles (Lord or Lady of Sangrisween, for example) but **give no Fame** and don't count towards your rank.

## Event pets

**Special Pets** and the **Sangris** pets (Alfa Sangris, and Homem & Brutus Sangris) only appear in server events.

## Battle Pass seasons

The Battle Pass runs in seasons. The current one is *Dracula vs Solarus*, with 150 levels. See [Battle Pass](/support/battle-pass/).
