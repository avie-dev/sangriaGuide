## Modes

| | Standard | Guided |
|---|---|---|
| Entry | 2,000 Ourosangue | 8,000 Ourosangue |
| Enemy faction | Random each wave | You pick, for all 30 waves |
| Factions | — | Bandits, Militia, Undead, Mortium, Luz Argenta, Oakveil, Gloomrot |
| Rare-prize Pet Soul | Acts I–II | Acts I–IV |

## Rewards

- Enemies give **2.5× character XP**.
- **A win** (killing the wave-30 boss): **500 Ourosangue** and a **5% chance** of a rare prize, either **1 Ressonador** (to your Bank) or **1 Pet Soul** for a boss pet you already own.
- The official guide says it plainly: the 500 doesn't cover the entry fee. People come for the rare prize and the XP.
- A loss, time-out or giving up returns nothing, including the entry fee. If the run breaks because of a server failure, the fee is refunded.
- The Arena gives **no Battle Pass XP**, and bosses killed here don't count for pets or boss rankings.

## Entering

1. Talk to **Master Gale**. Check how many arenas are free and your balance.
2. Choose **Standard Arena** or **Guided Arena** (clicking a faction switches to Guided).
3. Click **ENTER THE ILLUSION**. You get 20 seconds to prepare, which is a good time to summon your pet.

You must be standing, out of combat, out of bat form and not biting anyone.

## Duo

Both players stand in the Events Hub. The leader picks the mode, finds the partner under **THE DUO → IN THE HUB**, and clicks **Invite**. The partner has 30 seconds to accept. **Each pays their own entry and gets the full prize. Enemies are stronger in a duo.**

<Unverified q="Q-006">Unlike the Rupture and Judgment guides, the Arena guide doesn't say whether duo partners can hurt each other.</Unverified>

## Timers and losing

- The next wave arrives when 3 or fewer enemies remain, and the leftovers stay.
- A **normal wave** has a 2-minute limit. If it runs out, the leftovers vanish and the next wave comes. That's not a loss.
- A **boss wave** has a **5-minute** limit. Only the boss matters, and if time runs out it's a loss.
- The **whole run** is limited to **40 minutes**. You can't leave the area, because the edge pulls you back.
- **Solo:** you have 5 seconds to get up on your own, or you lose. **Duo:** you wait for your partner, and it's a loss only when both are down.
- A disconnect holds your spot. If everyone drops, the run waits 3 minutes, then ends.

<Aside type="danger">
Pressing X while downed gives up. You die, your item bag stays inside, and you get no prize, even if your partner wins.
</Aside>
