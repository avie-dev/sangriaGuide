## The healer class

**Sangrael** is the only class the official pages call a true support. It needs **Draekhar at Evolution I**. See [Sangrael](/classes/sangrael/).

- *Sangria Menor* heals allies in its area for **50% of your Spell Power**.
- *Liturgia da Ferida* restores **150% of your Spell Power** to nearby allies.
- *Feixe Carmesim* heals allies in the beam, based on Spell Power, and heals you per target hit.
- *Alta Carmesim* (ultimate) revives every downed vampire within 12 m at full health. It scales with Spell Power and **Ultimate Efficiency**.
- *Reviver Rápido* (passive): revive an ally in under a second, once every 180 s.

## Documented sources

| Source | Gives |
|---|---|

| Prestige 19 | Ultimate Efficiency +8% |
| Prestige 2 | Healing Received +2% (for yourself) |
| Pillars of the Winged Horror / Megara | +10 Spell Power each (plus spell crit) |
