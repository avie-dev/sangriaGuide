<Aside type="caution" title="Only with the class weapon">
Class bonuses apply **only while you hold that class's weapon**. A different weapon switches them off.
</Aside>

These are the published **Total** columns, meaning the base plus all three evolutions. For the bonus at each evolution, see the class's own page. Attribute names are this wiki's translations (see the [glossary](/character/stats/#attribute-glossary)). A ⚠ marks a published total that doesn't match its parts ([Q-018](/about/open-questions/#q-018), [Q-019](/about/open-questions/#q-019)).

## On top of the class bonus

Your character's attributes also come from weapon mastery, blood lineage, prestige buffs, plasmas, achievements and gear fortification. See [Character statistics](/character/stats/). How these combine isn't documented ([Q-009](/about/open-questions/#q-009)).
