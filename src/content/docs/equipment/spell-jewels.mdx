<Unverified q="Q-007">None of the 51 official guides or 12 class pages mentions spell jewels. It's not documented whether they exist on Sangria Falls, whether they work as in the base game, or whether the server changes them.</Unverified>

## What the guides do cover

These systems are documented and might be confused with jewels:

- **Necklaces.** The Enchanter fortifies and refines your necklace, which its panel calls your *grimoire*. See [Armour and jewellery](/equipment/armour-and-jewellery/).
- **Rings, pendants and amulets.** Enchanters make these at the Artisan Table to earn XP.
- **T8 amulets.** These are protected items (they can't be dropped on the ground) with a 1,000 Ourosangue Market minimum for T08 necklaces.
- **School spells.** The Vampiric School's *Grimoire* is a set of 44 spells that fire with your basic attack. It's a school system, not an item. See [Schools](/schools/).
