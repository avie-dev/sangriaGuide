## Classes

According to the official class pages, these benefit from **Physical Power, Physical Crit and Weapon Skill Power** and don't use Spell Power: **Noctarin, Bastien, Carmesyne, Skarn, Umbrellis, General**. **Mortak Relentless** gains crit, and **Sepulkros Reaper** scales physical, though Mortak Unshakable has no crit.

## Documented sources

| Source | Gives |
|---|---|
| Class weapon (evolutions) | See [Bonuses by class](/equipment/bonuses-by-class/) |
| Blacksmith fortification | +2 Physical Power per level (up to +20 at P10) |
| Weapon refinement | +1 to +3 power per row (5 rows) |
| Prestige 13 | Physical Power +2 |
| Prestige 15 | Physical Life Leech +2% |
| Prestige 17 | Attack Speed +4% |
| Prestige 18 | Weapon Skill Power +6% |
| Pillar of Solarus / of the Monster | +10 Physical Power, +5% Primary Attack Speed, +50 Sun Resistance (each; 200,000 Ourosangue each) |
| Weapon mastery / blood lineage | 3 attributes each, your choice of 12 |
| Plasmas | Ancestor bonuses (with penalties) |
