## Why this order

- **Fortification is cheap and certain.** It costs 2,000 per level, has no randomness, and the bonus stays on the item.
- **Refinement is random and needs scarce materials** (a Perfect gem plus a component per refinement number), so it pays off once your gathering trade supplies the component.
- **Pair your trades.** Blacksmith with Miner, Enchanter with Fisher, Tailor with Woodcutter: each gathering trade feeds one crafting trade's components.
- **Tailors pay per piece.** Four armour pieces cost four times as much to fortify. Factor that in if your class wants health.
- **Your weapon matters most to class bonuses**, since they switch off without it.

## Check your progress

`.item check tudo`, or F4 → Details on your craft, shows the fortification, refinement and durability of what you're wearing.
