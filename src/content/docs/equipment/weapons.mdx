## Sources

| Source | What you can get |
|---|---|
| **Dungeons** (every win) | A chance at a weapon: Blue, Purple or Legendary. With [affinity](/equipment/weapon-affinity/), a dropped weapon has a 40% chance of being your chosen type. |
| **Outposts** (Rank V) | At some outposts, the weekly mission can even give a legendary weapon |
| **Market** | Player listings, filterable by Blue, Purple and Orange legendaries |
| Crafting | Blacksmiths forge weapons at the workbench, which is also how they earn XP |

## Legendary tiers

The Market treats legendary weapons in three colours:

| Tier | Market minimum | Server auto-buy |
|---|---|---|
| Blue legendary | 5,000 | 1,000 |
| Purple legendary | 10,000 | 3,000 |
| Orange legendary (unique) | 20,000 | 10,000 |

Each fortification level adds 1,000 to the minimum and each refinement adds 2,000.

<Unverified q="Q-022">The Dungeons guide says "Blue, Purple or Legendary", while the Market calls Blue, Purple and Orange all legendary. It's unclear whether dungeon Blue and Purple weapons are legendaries, and whether "Legendary" there means Orange.</Unverified>

## Rules worth knowing

- **Class bonuses need the class weapon in hand.** Holding another weapon switches them off.
- **Mastery is per weapon type.** XP goes to the weapon you hold at the kill.
- **Draekhar and Sangrael's Staff is the game's Spear.**
- **Protected:** legendary weapons (with Dracula armour, T8 amulets and Soul Shards) can't be dropped on the ground. On death, the game's normal rule applies.
- **Ancestral weapon fragments** sell to the Shadow Merchant for 200, 500 or 1,000 Ourosangue depending on type. They're excluded from the Market's automatic sale.
- **Minion Collectors** (for Skarn, Sepulkros and La Muerte summons) come from dungeon rewards.

<Aside type="tip">
Make weapons stronger with [fortification](/equipment/fortification/) (Blacksmith: +2 Physical Power per level) and [refinement](/equipment/refinement/).
</Aside>
