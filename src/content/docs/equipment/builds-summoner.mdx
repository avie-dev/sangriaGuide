## How minions scale

| Class | Trigger | Minion strength | Duration | Options | Living limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Skarn](/classes/skarn/) | Every 15 hits in combat | 85% of your Physical Power × Minion Damage; 5 levels below you; 50% faster | — | 18 beasts | 4 |
| [Sepulkros](/classes/sepulkros/) Summoner | Every 7 basic hits in combat | Half your Spell Power × Minion Damage; your level; double speed | 15 s | 12 servants | 6 |
| [La Muerte](/classes/la-muerte/) | Every 4 basic hits in combat | Your Spell Power × Minion Damage; your level; double attack speed | 12 s (spirits) | 13 minions | 4 |

**Minions can't crit.** Going over the living limit kills the oldest. Sepulkros **Reaper** has no basic-attack summon. Instead, enemies that die while condemned rise as your skeletons (limit 4).

## Unlocking more summons

Locked summons unlock when you defeat that creature while playing the class with a **Minion Collector** in your inventory. There's a chance per kill, and the Collector is used only when it works. Collectors come from **dungeon rewards**. Sepulkros's **Fallen Angel** comes from the boss **Solarus**.

## Documented sources

- **Minion Damage** appears in Skarn's, Sepulkros's and La Muerte's attribute tables and "benefits from" lists.
- Your **Physical Power** (Skarn) or **Spell Power** (Sepulkros, La Muerte) sources apply too. See [Physical](/equipment/builds-physical/) and [Magic](/equipment/builds-magical/) builds.
