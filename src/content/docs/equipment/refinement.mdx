## The roll

Each refinement rolls a random bonus:

| Craft | Durability | Power / Health |
|---|---|---|
| Blacksmith (weapon) | +100, +200 or +300 | +1, +2 or +3 power |
| Enchanter (necklace) | +100, +200 or +300 | +1, +2 or +3 power |
| Tailor (armour) | +100, +200 or +300 | +3, +5 or +7 Health |

A row that rolls the best result shows **MAX REFINEMENT**.

## Materials

Refinement number *n* uses *n* of each material, so all five use 15 of each.

| Craft | Gem | Component | Component from |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blacksmith | Perfect Ruby | Precious Stone | Miner, prestige 5+ |
| Tailor | Perfect Topaz | Exceptional Tailoring Component | Woodcutter, prestige 5+ |
| Enchanter | Perfect Amethyst | Exceptional Magical Component | Fisher, prestige 5+ |

Components also drop at the **Dunley Outpost** at its highest rank, and other players sell them on the Market. For gems, see [Perfect gems](/equipment/perfect-gems/).

## Unlocking rows

| Unlock | Rows |
|---|---|
| Craft prestige 7 | Up to 3 |
| Craft prestige 9 | Up to 4 |
| Your mentor's achievement | Rows 1–2 immediately |
| Achievement + craft prestige 10 | Row 5 |

A row marked *Locked* or *NOT AVAILABLE* isn't unlocked for you yet. To start the achievement, talk to your mentor (Ornn, Label or Mirana) and choose the option ending in *(achievement: …)*. It's a long quest.

## How to refine

<Steps>

1. In your craft panel, select the piece and scroll to **REFINEMENT** (rows 1–5, each showing its material cost).
2. Click **REFINE** on the next free row, then **Confirm**.
3. Don't like the roll? **REFINE AGAIN** re-rolls that row. The old bonus is lost and the materials are spent again.

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution">
**Refinement can't be undone.** On the Market, each refinement adds 2,000 to a protected item's minimum price, and refined items are excluded from automatic sale.
</Aside>

Not your craft, or rows still locked? A [provider](/professions/services/) can refine within *their* limits. The 5th row needs a provider with the achievement and prestige 10.
