## Officially hybrid

| Class | Profile | Benefits from | Doesn't use |
|---|---|---|---|
| [La Muerte](/classes/la-muerte/) | Hybrid | Spell Power, Spell Crit, Weapon Skill Power, Minion Damage | Physical Power |
| [Awakened](/classes/awakened/) | Hybrid | Physical Power, Spell Power (+5 each from the class) | — |

La Muerte's Q and E are **weapon skills whose damage is all magic**. So Weapon Skill Power and Spell Power both matter, according to its page. The Awakened has no kit, no evolutions, and no mastery or lineage attributes. It's a starting state, not a build.

## Conversions inside physical kits

Some physical classes use effects that start as magic but are **converted to physical**, so they still scale as physical:

- **Bastien:** the Holy Explosion at the whip's tip and at the end of the spear's Q.
- **Carmesyne:** the sword's E (dash, final impact and Holy Explosion).
- **Umbrellis:** Lucile's Lethal Injection burst on the Slashers' E.
