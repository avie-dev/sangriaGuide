## Classes

According to the official pages, these benefit from **Spell Power and Spell Crit** and don't use Physical Power or Weapon Skill Power: **Draekhar**, **Sangrael** and **Sepulkros (Summoner)**. **La Muerte** is hybrid (Spell Power, Spell Crit, Weapon Skill Power and Minion Damage).

Draekhar has **−40% Max Health** from its class table.

## Documented sources

| Source | Gives |
|---|---|
| Class weapon (evolutions) | See [Bonuses by class](/equipment/bonuses-by-class/) |
| Enchanter fortification (necklace) | +2 Magic Power per level (up to +20 at P10) |
| Necklace refinement | +1 to +3 power per row |
| Prestige 14 | Spell Power +2 |
| Prestige 16 | Spell Life Leech +4% |
| Pillar of the Winged Horror | +10 Spell Power, +5% Spell Crit Chance, +50 Silver and Fire Resistance |
| Pillar of Megara | +10 Spell Power, +20% Spell Crit Damage, +50% Corruption Resistance |
| Weapon mastery / blood lineage | 3 attributes each, your choice of 12 |
| Plasmas | Ancestor bonuses (with penalties) |
