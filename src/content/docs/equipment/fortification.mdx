## What it gives

| Craft | Piece | Per fortification level | At P10 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blacksmith | Weapon | +2 Physical Power | +20 Physical Power |
| Tailor | Chest, gloves, legs, boots | +9 Health per piece | +90 Health per piece (+360 for all four) |
| Enchanter | Necklace | +2 Magic Power | +20 Magic Power |

Each level also raises the piece's **maximum durability**. The bonus is **saved on the item**, so it counts for whoever uses it, even after it's sold.

## Rules

- You need **at least 1 prestige** in the craft. You can fortify up to your prestige number (P1–P10).
- **2,000 Ourosangue per level gained**, from your Bank. The price is the same whether you go one level at a time or straight up: P0 → P5 is 10,000, and P4 → P5 is 2,000.
- **Fortification only goes up.**
- **Prestiging your craft doesn't upgrade your pieces.** It unlocks the next level, which you then pay for.

## How to fortify

<Steps>

1. Open your craft's panel: talk to Ornn, Label or Mirana → *Open Fortification & Refinement panel*, or type `.blacksmith panel` / `.tailor panel` / `.enchanter panel`.
2. Click the piece on the left.
3. Under **FORTIFICATION**, click the level (only levels above the current one, up to your prestige).
4. **Confirm Action** shows the cost. Click **Confirm**.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip" title="Not your craft?">
Hire a provider through [Profession services](/professions/services/). They can fortify up to *their* prestige. You pay their price per level plus the game's 2,000 per level.
</Aside>

## Fortification and resale

On the Market, each fortification level adds 1,000 Ourosangue to the minimum price of legendary weapons, Dracula armour and T08 necklaces. Fortified items are excluded from automatic sale.
