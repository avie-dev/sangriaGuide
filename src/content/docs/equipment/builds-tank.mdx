## The tank class

**Mortak Unshakable** is the class the official pages describe as holding the line: lots of health and resistance, **no physical crit**, and Blood Rage that taunts everything within 20 m with the class achievement. Its ultimate, *Glacial Bastion*, gives a shield of 550% of your Physical Power and crowd-control immunity for 6 s. See [Mortak](/classes/mortak/).

## Documented defensive sources

| Source | Gives |
|---|---|
| Mortak Unshakable class table | Max Health, Damage Reduction, Corruption Damage Reduction and more (see the class page) |
| Tailor fortification | +9 Health per armour piece per level (+360 for four pieces at P10) |
| Tailor refinement | +3 to +7 Health per row |
| Prestiges 1–7 | Damage Reduction +2%, Healing Received +2%, Max Health +40, Sun Resistance +8, Spell Resistance +2%, Physical Resistance +2%, Shield Efficiency +4% |
| Thanatos achievement (30 Transfusions) | When downed, get up at full health once per hour |
| *Furious Roar* (Mortak achievement) | Blood Rage taunts enemies within 20 m |
| *Collector of Suffering* (Mortak achievement) | Units you defeat become suffering, which Blood Rage turns into weapon durability |
| Pillar of the Colossus | +50 Garlic and Holy Resistance, +5% Movement Speed, +20% Resource Yield |
