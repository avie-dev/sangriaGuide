| Gem | Used by | Refines |
|---|---|---|
| Perfect Ruby | Blacksmith | Weapons |
| Perfect Topaz | Tailor | Chest, gloves, legs, boots |
| Perfect Amethyst | Enchanter | Necklaces |

Refinement *n* uses *n* gems, so all five rows of one piece use 15.

## Where they come from

- **Master and Nightmare dungeons:** the *Special* roll can give **Perfect Gems** (or Cristais, or Ressonador). The panel at Mark shows the chance by tier.
- **The Market:** buy from other players.
- **Profession services:** hire a provider *with the provider's materials*, so their stock covers the gems.

The official guides don't name other sources, or say whether the base game's gem recipes are unchanged ([Q-008](/about/open-questions/#q-008)).

## Not to be confused with

The Shadow Merchant buys **flawless** gems for 3.33 Ourosangue each. Those are a different grade from the Perfect gems refinement needs.
