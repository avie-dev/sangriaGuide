**Affinity** is the weapon type you'd prefer from dungeon rewards. When a weapon drops in your prize, it has a **40% chance** of being that type.

<Aside type="caution">
Affinity **doesn't raise the chance of getting a weapon**, only which type it is when one drops. Set it **before entering**: the affinity saved at the moment of victory is the one that counts.
</Aside>

## Setting it

<PlayModes>
	<Fragment slot="interface">At the Events Hub, talk to **Mark**. At the top of the Sangria Dungeons panel, click **CHANGE** and pick a weapon type.</Fragment>
	<Fragment slot="chat"><Command cmd=".dungeon">Opens the dungeon menu. Choose *Set weapon affinity*.</Command> Talking to Mark also offers *Set weapon affinity*.</Fragment>
</PlayModes>

## Tips

- Set it to your **class weapon**, since class bonuses only work with it in hand.
- Each player rolls their own prize, so every player in the group can set their own affinity.
- The panel shows the chance of each prize, tier by tier.
