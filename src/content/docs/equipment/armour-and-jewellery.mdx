## Upgrading

| Piece | Craft | Fortification | Refinement roll |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chest, gloves, legs, boots | Tailor (mentor Label) | +9 Health per piece per level | +3/+5/+7 Health, +100/+200/+300 durability |
| Necklace (the panel calls it your "grimoire") | Enchanter (mentor Mirana) | +2 Magic Power per level | +1/+2/+3 power, +100/+200/+300 durability |

The Tailor pays fortification **per piece**. See [Fortification](/equipment/fortification/) and [Refinement](/equipment/refinement/).

## Where pieces come from

| Source | What the guides say |
|---|---|
| Silverlight Hills Outpost (Cassandra) | Specialty: **armour** (from Rank III, better at IV and V) |
| Ruins of Mortium Outpost (Velanna) | Specialty: **necklaces** |
| Tailor / Enchanter crafting | Tailors sew armour, capes and bags; Enchanters make rings, pendants and amulets |
| Market | Player listings; **Dracula armour** pieces have a 5,000 minimum and **T08 necklaces** a 1,000 minimum |

## Protected items

**Dracula armour** and **T8 amulets** can't be dropped on the ground (along with legendary weapons and Soul Shards). A new clan member can't move them in or out of another member's castle chests for 12 hours.

## Checking your gear

`.item check tudo` shows fortification, refinement and durability for everything equipped. Use `peitoral`, `luvas`, `calcas`, `botas` or `colar` for one piece, or press F4 → Details on your craft.
