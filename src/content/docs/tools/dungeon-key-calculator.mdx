## How it works

- **1 key per player per run.** Recipes and fusions are from Mark's panel (see [Dungeon keys](/dungeons/keys/)).
- **Routes:** forge the tier directly, or forge lower tiers and fuse up. Nightmare can't be fused.
- **Returns:** every win returns 20% of the key's materials. If you lose, nothing comes back.
- **Shards:** a full Rift Incursion (2 portals and a gate, 50 fragments each) gives up to 150 if you're in every circle. Incursion I pays Stygian Shards, and II–III pay Greater Stygian Shards.

Ready-made keys can also drop from incursion gate bosses, but the chance isn't published.
