## How it works

Pay per action is official: **Miner 25 (+10% per prestige), Fisher 70 (+15%), Woodcutter 10 (+10%)**, which gives 50, 175 and 20 at prestige 10. Your hourly income is that pay times **your** actions per hour.

<Unverified q="Q-012">How many actions you can fit in an hour isn't published and depends on where and how you gather. Time yourself for a few minutes and enter the rate, and please share your numbers.</Unverified>

It leaves out profession orders, refining components and pet souls (chances not published), selling the gathered resources, and aquariums. See [Comparing the gathering trades](/professions/gathering-comparison/).
