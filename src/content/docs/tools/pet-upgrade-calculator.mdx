## How it works

- **Tiers:** each raise costs **1 soul of the pet itself**, plus **sacrificed souls from its act**: 1 (T2), 2 (T3), 4 (T4) and 8 (T5). So T0 → T5 is 5 + 15.
- **Cristais:** up to 5 attributes, each capped at **+2 per tier** (T5 +10). Each attempt costs 1 Cristal. The calculator lowers targets above your tier's cap.

**Expected attempts can't be calculated.** The evolve chance isn't published. The panel shows a CURRENT CHANCE that rises after each failure. So the result is the **minimum**, with every attempt succeeding. See [Pet Cristais](/companions/pet-crystals/).
