## How it works

- Each Transfusion needs **10 Pulses** and **10,000 Ourosangue** (8,000 with VIP) and gives **10 plasmas**. The limit is 250.
- **Sealed** transfusions (1, and every Lost Book chapter transfusion: 5, 10 … 50, 65 … 170, 190 … 250) need Nyxaris first.
- **Milestones:** 10 transfusions (Luminance), 30 (Thanatos), 250 (Kill Points).
- **Meditation time** treats every Pulse as a full level 1 → 90 meditation climb (about 74 minutes, or 50 with VIP). Hunting also gives Pulses, so it's an upper-end estimate if you only meditate.

See [Transfusions](/endgame/transfusions/).
