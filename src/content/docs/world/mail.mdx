Mail is free and works anywhere. The box keeps your **100 most recent** messages. Press **F6**, click the envelope by the minimap, or type `.mail`. A red dot and a corner notice tell you when new mail arrives. Tabs: **Inbox, Sent, Write, Block**.

## Writing

**Write** tab: in **To:**, the exact in-game name; then **Subject:** and **Message:** (up to 280 letters; `||` makes a line break). Click **Send**. If the name is wrong, chat tells you. Players read it when they log in.

**Between players, mail is text only.** You can't send items or coins.

## Rewards

Rewards come from the **team** (crown icon) and **systems** (gift icon), such as dungeon rewards, incursion dungeon keys and boss Soul Shards. Open the letter → **Claim reward**, or **Claim all** in the Inbox.

<Aside type="caution" title="15 free slots">
Items need **15 free inventory slots**, or the claim doesn't happen and waits in the box. Coins go to the Bank. Team mail may have a **deadline**, so claim it soon.
</Aside>

Letters you'll see:

- **Dungeon System:** your dungeon win reward.
- **The Order of the Observers:** a Soul Shard, or a dungeon key from an incursion gate boss.
- **Red WARNING:** an important server notice, for example *YOUR CASTLE WILL FALL* (low Castle Heart essence). There's no reply and no reward.

## Deleting and blocking

Deleting (the trash can, or **Delete**) is **instant, with no confirmation**, so claim rewards first. **Block** on a player's message stops their mail, and the **Block** tab lists and unblocks. Team and system mail can't be blocked.
