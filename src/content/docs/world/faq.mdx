## Getting started

### Is the interface mandatory?
No. Everything works through chat (`.menu`) and the website panel, though the interface makes it much easier. See [Installing the interface](/getting-started/interface/).

### I installed it and nothing appeared.
Close the game, check that BepInEx and ScarletInterface are installed and switched on in the launcher, and start the game with PLAY. Still nothing? Open a Discord ticket. See [Interface troubleshooting](/getting-started/interface-troubleshooting/).

### How do I switch to English?
`.lang en` in game. The launcher and website panel have their own language settings. See [Setting the language](/getting-started/language/).

### What do I do after the tutorial?
Capture a pet, set up a daily Ourosangue routine (an Outpost daily, Balthazar's contracts, profession orders), and evolve your class. See the [first-week roadmap](/getting-started/first-week/).

### What happens to my character when I log out?
A few seconds later it moves to a safe spot, and returns to where it was when you log back in. That doesn't happen if you left dead, mounted or in your coffin.

## Money and items

### Can I send Ourosangue to a friend?
Not in general. It changes hands through Market sales, contracts and profession services. The exception is married players' Joint Account. See [Bank](/economy/bank/).

### Do I lose my balance when I die?
No. Bank balances belong to your account. Deposit currency items so they're safe. See [Bank](/economy/bank/).

### Can I send items by mail?
No. Between players, mail is text only. See [Mail](/world/mail/).

### I bought or sold the wrong thing.
Market purchases and Shadow Merchant sales are final. See [Market](/economy/market/).

### I was away a long time. What did I lose?
After 5 days offline, your Market shop is freed (items go to market storage). After 15 days you lose your Vault room (not the chests or items) and your prisoner stock. After 7 days without scoring (or 14 offline), you leave your School, but your rank is kept.

## Classes and progression

### Do I lose anything when I switch class?
No. Evolutions, Currents, variants and attributes are all kept. It costs 3,000 Ourosangue. See [Classes](/classes/).

### My class bonuses disappeared.
You're holding a weapon that isn't your class's. See [Choosing a class](/classes/choosing-a-class/).

### I can't prestige.
You're not at max level, or the cost isn't in your Bank (not your inventory). See [Character prestige](/character/prestige/).

### Does prestiging or dying slow my XP?
No. See [Levels and experience](/character/levels-and-xp/).

### I levelled a profession and got no bonus.
That's normal. Profession bonuses come only from prestige. See [Professions](/professions/).

## Meditation

### Why am I not gaining XP?
Check the red reason on the path (UNARMED, NO BLOOD, NO PET, MAX LEVEL) and your Sangricoins (at least 0.35). See [Meditation troubleshooting](/meditation/troubleshooting/).

### Does my Quitina or welcome gift count?
No. Only VIP (+50%) and server XP events count in meditation.

### How much does a night cost?
168 Sangricoins for 8 hours on one path (336 on Double Meditation). See [Costs and XP rates](/meditation/costs-and-rates/).

## Pets

### I killed the boss and got no pet.
It's a 1% chance, some bosses can't be captured, and Arena and dungeon bosses give nothing. See [Capturing pets](/companions/pet-capture/).

### My pet vanished or died.
It's stored when you enter the city, dungeons or the test room, so summon it again. A dead pet isn't lost: leave combat and summon it. See [Pets](/companions/pets/).

## Events and dungeons

### Do I need a group for dungeons?
Yes, 2–4 players, each with a key of the tier. A loss doesn't return the key. See [Dungeons](/dungeons/).

### Is the Rupture or Judgment reward split?
No. Each player gets the full reward. After your rewarded win, you can help others for no reward. See [Reality Rupture](/events/reality-rupture/) and [Goreswine's Judgment](/events/goreswines-judgment/).

### Does teleporting cost anything?
No. See [Teleports](/getting-started/teleports/).

## VIP and support

### How do I get VIP?
By donation, through a Discord ticket. There's no in-game purchase. See [VIP](/support/vip/).

### Does VIP include the Bloody Pass or Double Meditation?
No. Each is a separate unlock. See [Optional purchases](/support/optional-purchases/).

### What do I lose when VIP ends?
The speed, Auto-Prestige, Willfred Primal and your second teleport. Your plasma distribution in use stays. See [VIP](/support/vip/).

More questions? The [mechanics FAQ](/reference/mechanics-faq/) covers how systems work, and the [troubleshooting page](/reference/troubleshooting/) covers fixes.
