Achievements are the server's longest quests, made for advanced players. Each one finished leaves a **permanent bonus** that survives death and logging out. You can work on several at once, but **none can be abandoned**. Open them with **F9** (or the medal by the minimap).

## Starting one

In F9, click **TRACK** on an achievement to mark its NPC on your map (one at a time). Talk to them and choose the achievement, and it becomes a normal quest in F3. Bought Pillars don't become quests: the buff applies immediately. Hover over a name to see its requirements and buffs.

<Aside type="note" title="Pillars changed">
The four Pillars (Solarus, Monster, Colossus, Winged Horror) **are no longer quests**. You buy the echo from Asterion. Without the balance, nothing is charged. Players who had the Pillar deposited on the old server got it free. Old in-progress Pillar missions were removed, and handed-in items weren't returned.
</Aside>

Most achievement steps involve delivering items (a bit at a time is fine), defeating many enemies from certain regions, and defeating V Bloods. The two Classes achievements work only with Mortak active. See [Class quests](/quests/class-quests/) for the Unshakable Will's steps.

## Thanatos cooldown

Once you've finished Thanatos (title *Son of Thanatos*), a **skull** by the ability bar shows a **green ring** when ready, or a **red ring with minutes left** while recharging. Hover for the exact time, or click to post it in chat. Hide it with the minimap gear's *Thanatos Cooldown* card. `.thanatos` (or `.tn`) shows it in chat.

**Hidden achievement** lines are achievements that haven't been revealed yet.
