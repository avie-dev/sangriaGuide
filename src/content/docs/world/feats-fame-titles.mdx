**Feats** are medals the server awards on its own as you play. You don't start anything. Your history from before Feats existed was converted to medals at your first login. Medals are never lost, and Fame never drops. Open the panel with the **hexagon-with-star** button by the minimap. A **NEW FEAT!** notice and sound mark each one you earn.

## Medals and Fame

| Stage | Fame |
|---|---|
| Bronze | 10 |
| Silver | 25 |
| Gold | 50 |
| Legendary (no stages; much harder) | 100 |

**Only a medal's highest stage counts.** Silver replaces Bronze's 10 with 25, rather than adding to it. Each stage counts the **total from the start**. For example, Silver *Explorer of the Depths* needs 100 dungeons in total. The medal's **title** unlocks at its first stage (Bronze or Legendary).

Tabs: All, Progression, Combat, Pets, Activities, Collection, Economy, Events. Hover over a medal to see each stage's requirement. Locked medals show faded.

## Ranks

| Rank | Fame |
|---|---|
| 1 Awakening | 115 |
| 2 Recognition | 285 |
| 3 Ascension | 505 |
| 4 Influence | 785 |
| 5 Nobility | 1,125 |
| 6 Prestige | 1,515 |
| 7 Sovereignty | 1,910 |
| 8 Supremacy | 2,310 |
| 9 Legend | 2,640 |
| 10 Eternity | 2,925 |

With every current medal maxed, you reach **rank 6**. Ranks 7–10 wait for new medals. Each rank unlocks a title too.

## Titles

- **Use title** on a medal you own (it shows *Equipped*). **Rank title** uses your current rank (click again after ranking up). **Remove title** to go without one.
- Titles show above your name in the medal's rarity colour (rank titles in gold), visible to everyone using the interface.
- **Titles: masculine** switches to the feminine form, and back (Lord or Lady of Beasts, for example).
- Marriage adds the *Crimson Consort* and *Soul of Eternity* titles. See [Marriage](/world/marriage/).

<Command cmd=".feats title rank">Uses your rank title.</Command>

## Highlights and other players

Click **Highlight** on up to 3 medals to show them in your Profile highlights and on your F1 picture. Other players see your title, rank, Fame, medal count and highlights in your Player Panel (right-click → **View profile**, or click your name in the Rankings), unless you've set it to Private. If you haven't picked highlights, your 3 highest-stage medals show.

## Event medals

Sangrisween 2026's six medals live in the **Events** tab. They give **titles but no Fame**, and don't count towards your rank. See [Seasonal events](/events/seasonal/).

**Medal didn't appear?** The server reviews progress within a few minutes, and opening the panel checks immediately. *Lord of Illusions* only counts Arena wins you finished without quitting.
