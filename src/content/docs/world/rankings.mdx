The rankings compare everyone on the server, including offline players. They're free, open anywhere, and refresh **about every minute** (**Refresh** at the bottom reloads them now). A fresh level-1 character doesn't appear. Click any name to open their public **Player Panel**.

<Command cmd=".top">Opens SERVER RANKINGS (also the trophy by the minimap, after the tutorial; the nearby medal is Achievements).</Command>

## PROGRESSION

The criterion: **Kill Points, then Transfusion, then Pulse, then Prestige, then Level**. Kill Points only exist after 250 transfusions, so for everyone else more transfusions put you ahead, and level is only a tiebreaker. The podium shows the top 3, then 50 per page.

| Button | Shows |
|---|---|
| Overall | The full ranking by the criterion above |
| Current month | Only players with 250 transfusions, by Kill Points this month (resets on the 1st) |
| Previous month | The same for last month |

Pulses converted to points count only in Overall. See [After 250 transfusions](/endgame/after-250-transfusions/).

## DAMAGE

Choose **By weapon** or **By class**, then **Overall** or one weapon or class. **Overall** shows each player's *best* weapon or class (their highest number, not a sum). Damage is **cumulative** for all time. In By weapon, only combat weapons count, and bare-hand damage doesn't.

## BOSSES

**Total** shows how many *distinct* bosses each player has killed. Pick one boss (listed by act) to see what percentage of each player's kills were on it. **Only open-world kills count.** Arena and dungeon bosses don't.

## Your own numbers

Your **Profile (F1)** holds your personal rankings: Top 3 weapons by damage, most-hunted bosses and favourite pets. The **Tower of the Immortals** has its own per-class weekly rankings. See [Tower of the Immortals](/events/tower-of-the-immortals/).
