<Unverified q="Q-014">The official site has no community rules page, and none of the 51 guides lists the server's rules. They're probably on the official Discord (discord.gg/SangriaFalls). This wiki won't guess them.</Unverified>

## Related safeguards the guides describe

| Safeguard | What it does | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Mail block | Stops a player's mail to you | [Mail](/world/mail/) |
| Private Player Panel | Only you and the staff can open your profile | [Profile](/character/profile/) |
| Protected items | Legendary weapons, Dracula armour, T8 amulets and Soul Shards can't be dropped on the ground | [Castle](/world/castle/) |
| Clan quarantine | New members can't move protected items in others' castles for 12 hours | [Clan](/world/clan/) |
| Website confirmation | Actions on the website panel need your `.site yes` in game | [Website panel](/getting-started/website-panel/) |
| Login phrases and vow phrases | Follow "the server's phrase rules" (not published) | [VIP](/support/vip/), [Marriage](/world/marriage/) |
