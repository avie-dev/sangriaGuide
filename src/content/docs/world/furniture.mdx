Furniture pieces are decorations that aren't in the build menu. They don't break, don't drop loot, and anyone in the clan can move them. The panel has three tabs: **Shop**, **My storage** and **In the castle**.

## Buying

Pick a piece in **Shop**. The display case lets you drag to rotate and scroll to zoom. Click **Buy for … Sangricoin**, then **Confirm**. It goes to **My storage** and shows in your Bank statement as *Furniture purchase*.

## Placing

<Steps>

1. Go inside your (or your clan's) castle territory. The footer shows the castle level and piece count.
2. **My storage** → the piece → **Place in the castle**.
3. Controls: **left click** places, **Q/E** rotate, **C/T** raise and lower (up to 4 m), **R twice** removes the piece under the cursor, **Space** finishes. Each click places another while you have more in storage.

</Steps>

Pieces rest on whatever is below, so you can put books on a table. Entering combat or leaving the territory ends placement, and your ability keys act as these controls meanwhile.

## Moving and removing

**In the castle** shows each piece and who bought it. **Move with the cursor**: click a piece, carry it, and click to drop it. **Remove** sends it back to **the buyer's** storage, and they're notified.

<Aside type="note">
Furniture is never lost. If the castle falls (destroyed, abandoned or taken), every piece returns to its buyer's storage.
</Aside>

<Command cmd=".furniture">Opens the furniture panel.</Command>
