## XP by region

Fishing XP depends on the region: **Farbane, Dunley, Cursed Forest, Silverlight and Oakveil**. The further along the region, the more XP.

## Prestige rewards

| Prestige | Adds |
|---|---|
| Each | +15% Ourosangue per catch |
| 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 | +1 extra fish (up to +5) |
| 5 and up | A small chance of **Exceptional Magical Component** per catch, improving up to prestige 10 |
| 6 to 9 | **Sacrificed Souls**: one more act per prestige. For the Fisher, **each act rolls its own chance per catch**. |

<Aside type="caution">
The extra fish **drops on the ground beside you**. Pick it up before you leave.
</Aside>

## Aquariums

Only an active Fisher can craft **aquariums** and their **food**, and each Fisher prestige gives the owner's aquariums +5% production. See [Aquarium](/professions/aquarium/).

Type `.prof` to open the professions menu, where you can view and prestige the trade without the interface.
