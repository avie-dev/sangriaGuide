## Tank levels

| Level | Tank | Fish per day | Holds | Fish types |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | Small | 8 | 24 | Common |
| 5–9 | Large | 16 | 48 | + Uncommon |
| 10 | Large | 24 | 78 | + Rare, and occasionally **The Fish** |

An active Fisher owner gets +5% production per Fisher prestige.

## Food

Food is a **balance on your account**, not an item. Each food is worth 18, 12 or 6 hours of production depending on the level, and a tank holds up to 8. Without food, or with the fish limit reached, the tank stops and spends nothing. Whoever feeds a tank uses their own food balance.

## Crafting (Fishers)

| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| Aquarium | 20 Iron Ingots, 20 Glass, 50 Oil, 1 The Fish, 2,000 Ourosangue |
| Food (each) | 2 Fish Bones, 10 Oil, 100 Ourosangue |

You can craft 10 food a day, plus 5 per Fisher prestige (60 at prestige 10).

## Installing, feeding and collecting

- On your castle's (or your clan's castle's) grounds: **My aquariums** → the tank → **Install**. Place it with left-click, rotate with Q/E, raise and lower with C/T, and cancel with Space. Entering combat or leaving the grounds cancels placement.
- Press **F** on the tank: **Feed** (+1) or **Fill the tank**, then **Collect** when fish are ready. Without inventory room, the rest drops on the ground.
- Clanmates can feed and collect, and the fish go to whoever collects. Each tank keeps its last 30 withdrawals under **History**.
- **Return to storage** moves a tank. If the castle falls, it returns to the owner's storage with its level, food and fish.

## Upgrading (owner only)

A bar fills with fish produced, then you pay materials. The bar stops at the next goal, and excess doesn't carry over, so upgrade promptly.

| To level | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 | 9 | 10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fish produced | 40 | 80 | 120 | 160 | 288 | 416 | 544 | 672 | 800 |
| Iron Ingots | 50 | 75 | 100 | 125 | 150 | 175 | 200 | 225 | 250 |
| Reinforced Planks | 20 | 30 | 40 | 50 | 60 | 70 | 80 | 90 | 100 |

**The Fish** isn't sold here. Collect it to craft another aquarium, or sell it to the Shadow Merchant for 4,000.
</Aside>

## The aquarium market

- **Sell a tank:** collect, *Return to storage*, set a price (at least 10,000), and list it. You're paid even offline.
- **Sell food in lots** (1–100) from the Craft tab. Anyone with food in their balance can do this, Fisher or not.
- **Buy** tanks (they keep their level and food) or food lots from the Market tab.
- Up to 5 listings at once, tanks and lots combined.

<Command cmd=".aquarium">Opens the aquarium menu.</Command>
<Command cmd=".aquarium sellfish 1 all">Sells all fish in aquarium 1, except The Fish.</Command>
