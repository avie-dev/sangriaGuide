## Earning XP

Make rings, pendants and amulets at the **Artisan Table**. Only items you set to be produced count.

## Fortifying the necklace

Fortify up to your Enchanter prestige (max 10), at 2,000 Ourosangue per level. The panel shows your necklace as **EQUIPPED GRIMOIRE**, plus pieces IN INVENTORY.

## Refining the necklace

Up to 5 refinements, each rolling +100/+200/+300 durability and +1/+2/+3 power. Refinement *n* uses *n* of each material. Prestige 7 unlocks 3 refinements and prestige 9 unlocks 4. **Mirana's achievement** unlocks the first 2 right away, and the 5th at prestige 10.

## Commands

<Command cmd=".enchanter panel">Opens the Fortification & Refinement panel from anywhere.</Command>
<Command cmd=".item check colar">Shows your necklace's fortification, refinement and durability.</Command>

At the mentor, *View equipped necklace.* shows the same.
