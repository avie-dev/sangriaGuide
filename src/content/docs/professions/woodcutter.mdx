## Prestige rewards

| Prestige | Adds |
|---|---|
| Each | +10% Ourosangue per tree, +5% extra wood |
| 5 and up | A small chance of **Exceptional Tailoring Component** per tree, improving up to prestige 10 |
| 6 to 9 | **Sacrificed Souls**: one more act per prestige; at most one soul per tree, rolled separately from the component |

**Castle trees don't count**, for XP or Ourosangue.

See [Comparing the gathering trades](/professions/gathering-comparison/).
