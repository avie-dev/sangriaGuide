## Hiring

<Steps>

1. Open the board and filter by Blacksmith, Tailor or Enchanter. Each card shows prestige, fortification and refinement limits, prices and material stock.
2. Click **Hire**, then click the piece under YOUR GEAR.
3. Choose **FORTIFY** (click a level, e.g. P5), or **REFINEMENT** *With my materials* / *With provider's materials*. **Redo** re-rolls an existing refinement.
4. Check the summary (provider's price, base cost, materials, TOTAL) and click **Confirm**.

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution">
The refinement bonus is random and can't be undone, and fortification only goes up.
</Aside>

## Cost

- **Fortification:** the provider's price per level gained **plus** 2,000 Ourosangue per level. For example, a 500 price over 3 levels costs 1,500 + 6,000 = 7,500.
- **Refinement:** the provider's price × the refinement number. Refinement #3 costs three times their price and uses 3 of each material.
- A provider fortifies up to their own prestige and refines only as far as they've unlocked. The **5th refinement** needs a provider with the trade's achievement and prestige 10.

Materials: Perfect Ruby + gemstone (Blacksmith), Perfect Topaz + Exceptional Tailoring Component (Tailor), Perfect Amethyst + Exceptional Magical Component (Enchanter). The crafts guide calls the Blacksmith's component *Precious Stone*.

## Offering services

<Steps>

1. F4 → **Details** on your craft → **Provide Services** (lit at 5 prestiges).
2. Tick Fortification, Refinement or both.
3. Fill in three prices: fortification per level, refinement with your materials, and refinement with the client's materials.
4. **Save prices** → **Publish to the board**. **Pause offer** removes you from the board but keeps your prices.

</Steps>

To sell refinement with your own materials, stock them via **Manage Materials** (Deposit 1/10/50/All). Refinement #4 takes 4 gems and 4 components from your stock, so price accordingly. Each hire sends a **[SERVICE]** notice, delivered when you log in if you were offline.

## Board rules (since 30/09/2026)

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Fortification price | 3,500–15,000 per level gained |
| Refinement, client's materials | 3,500–15,000 per level |
| Refinement, provider's materials | 6,000–25,000 per level |
| Lowering a price | Once per 24 hours per price; raising is free |
| Weekly slots | 2 orders per week per prestige (P5 = 10, P10 = 20); reset Monday |

You can't hire yourself. For your own craft, use the craft panel.

<Command cmd=".services">Opens the services board (a numbered menu without the interface).</Command>
