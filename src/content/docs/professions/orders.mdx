## Taking and turning in

<Steps>

1. Talk to your profession's mentor in the Professions Hall.
2. Click *Today's work orders.*, *This week's special order.* or *This week's hunt.* **Clicking already accepts it.**
3. Complete it. Progress shows in your Quests (F3 or `.quest`).
4. Return to the mentor to turn it in. For crafting orders, the **Delivery** window opens: click the pieces (or **Select all**), then **Deliver**.

</Steps>

## Rules for crafting orders

- Only production **you set at the workbench** counts. Buying, taking from a chest, or cancelling production doesn't.
- At turn-in, any piece of the requested type works, and the mentor keeps it. The weapon in your hand is never turned in.
- Each crafted piece counts for **one** order. An order asking for that specific piece takes it first. For example, a Scourgestone Pendant goes to *Green Pendant* before *Continuous Work*, and chat tells you.
- Production queued at the workbench when you accepted counts too, if it finishes afterwards.

## Rules for gathering orders

No order needs a particular place on the map. Ore, fish and trees count wherever you gather them (except castle trees for the Woodcutter).

## Better orders

Higher profession levels unlock harder orders that pay more. If your mentor has no hunt for you, your level doesn't reach one yet.

<Aside type="note">
The guides don't publish the order list, the XP or the Ourosangue for each order.
</Aside>
