## The rules

- **Only the active profession earns XP.** You keep one crafting and one gathering trade.
- **Level gives no bonus by itself.** It unlocks better orders and leads to prestige, where the bonuses are.
- **Choosing is free.** The tutorial lets you choose both with Helena Valerius.
- **Changing:** the first change in each category is free. After that, each costs **1,000 Sangricoins** and needs **120 days** since the last one. The old trade's level and prestige are kept for when you return, but its accepted orders are abandoned.
- From **gathering prestige 5**, you get refining components. From **prestige 6**, pet souls. With **5 crafting prestiges**, you can [sell services](/professions/services/).

<Aside type="caution">
The **Prestige** button acts instantly, with no confirmation. XP past level 100 isn't lost: it starts your next climb.
</Aside>

## Choosing and changing

Talk to the mentor in the Professions Hall:

- **To choose:** *Yes. I want…*, then *I'm sure. I want to be…*
- **To change:** *I want to change my trade (… to …)*, then *Confirm the change*. F4 shows whether your change is free, what it costs, or how many days you have left.
