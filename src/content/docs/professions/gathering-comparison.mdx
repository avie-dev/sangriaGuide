## Which earns most?

<Unverified q="Q-012">The guides publish what each **action** pays, but not how many veins, catches or trees you can manage per hour. A Fisher earns 7 times what a Woodcutter does per action, but actions take different amounts of time, so this wiki can't rank them by hourly income. If you time your gathering, please share it.</Unverified>

## A practical way to choose

- **Pick by the crafting you (or your friends) do.** Each gathering trade is the main source of one crafting trade's refining component. A Blacksmith wants a Miner, an Enchanter a Fisher, a Tailor a Woodcutter.
- **Fishers** also get aquariums, which produce fish while you're offline.
- **Pet-focused players** should note the Fisher's per-act soul rolls, versus one soul at most per vein or tree for the other two. The guides don't give the chances, though.

The first change in each category is free, so you can try one trade and switch once.
