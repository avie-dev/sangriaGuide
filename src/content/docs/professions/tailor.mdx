## Earning XP

Sew armour, capes and bags at the **Tailoring Bench**. Only items you set to be produced count.

## Fortifying armour

Fortify up to your Tailor prestige (max 10). Your panel lists EQUIPPED ARMOR and IN INVENTORY.

<Aside type="caution">
The Tailor pays **per piece**. Fortifying all four pieces costs four times as much: 80,000 Ourosangue to take all four from P0 to P10.
</Aside>

## Refining armour

Up to 5 refinements per piece, each rolling **+3, +5 or +7 Health** plus +100/+200/+300 durability. Refinement *n* uses *n* of each material. Prestige 7 unlocks 3 refinements and prestige 9 unlocks 4. **Label's achievement** unlocks the first 2 right away, and the 5th at prestige 10.

## Commands

<Command cmd=".tailor panel">Opens the Fortification & Refinement panel from anywhere.</Command>
<Command cmd=".item check tudo">Shows fortification, refinement and durability for all your gear (`peitoral`, `luvas`, `calcas`, `botas` for one piece).</Command>

At the mentor, *View equipped gear.* shows the same.
