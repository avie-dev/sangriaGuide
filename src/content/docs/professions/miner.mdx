## Prestige rewards

| Prestige | Adds |
|---|---|
| Each | +10% Ourosangue per vein, +5% extra ore |
| 5 and up | A small chance of **Precious Stone** per vein, improving up to prestige 10 (no achievement needed) |
| 6 to 9 | **Sacrificed Souls** for pets: each prestige unlocks one more act. Each unlocked act raises the chance, but at most one soul drops per vein, rolled separately from the Precious Stone. |

The guides don't give the drop chances.

## Commands

<Command cmd=".prof ver minerador">Shows the Miner's level, XP and prestige.</Command>
<Command cmd=".prestige profession minerador">Prestiges at level 100, with no confirmation.</Command>
