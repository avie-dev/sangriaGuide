Sangria Falls Wiki is an **unofficial**, English-first guide to [Sangria Falls](https://www.sangriafalls.com/en/), a heavily customised MMORPG-style V Rising server. It is written by players, for players, and is not operated or endorsed by the Sangria Falls staff.

Its main target is **[GLOBAL] Sangria Falls [RPG 04] [2X]**. Most of what the official guides describe applies to every Sangria Falls server; anything that only applies to RPG 04 is labelled as such.

## How articles are researched

- **Official pages first.** Every mechanic, number, command and NPC comes from an official Sangria Falls guide or class page, and each article links to its sources at the bottom.
- **Portuguese originals settle doubts.** The English guides are translations. When a translation is unclear, or a page is only in Portuguese, we read the original.
- **Nothing is invented.** If the official pages don't say something, we don't guess. It is either left out or marked **Requires verification** and added to the list of open questions.
- **Vanilla V Rising and Bloodcraft don't count as sources.** Sangria Falls changes a lot; a mechanic from another server or the base game is only stated here if a Sangria Falls source confirms it.
- **Advice is labelled as advice.** Recommendations from players or from this wiki are marked **Community recommendation**. There are no tier lists or "best in slot" claims without real testing data.
- **Every article has a last-verified date**, the day its claims were last checked against the official pages.
