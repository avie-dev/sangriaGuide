## Latest quality check

On **2026-11-22**, every page was checked against the official guides:

- **1,275 numbers and 337 commands** were audited against each page's cited sources. Numbers this wiki calculated are listed with their formulas.
- **Every link** was checked: internal links at build time, and 189 links to outside sites.
- **Every page** was tested on a phone-width screen.
- **33 automated tests** cover the calculators.

"Checked" means checked against the official guides, **not** confirmed in game. Things the guides don't settle are on the [open questions page](/about/open-questions/).
