The Sangria Falls staff don't publish a side-by-side comparison with the base game. This page lists only the differences an official Sangria Falls guide states or implies directly, each linked to its source. When a guide doesn't mention something, this wiki doesn't assume it works as in vanilla, and it doesn't assume it's changed either.

<Aside type="tip" title="A safe rule of thumb">
If you know V Rising well, expect **castle building, the open world and V Blood bosses** to look familiar, and **all progression, currencies and group content** to follow Sangria Falls rules. When in doubt, check the system's page here or the official guide.
</Aside>

## Progression

| Topic | On Sangria Falls | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Character power | A **character level from 1 to 90** replaces the game's gear level as the number shown on your vampire. | Level and Prestige |
| XP | Comes from kills, quests and meditation; V Bloods give much more. Dying doesn't cost XP or level. | Level and Prestige |
| Prestige | At 90 you reset to level 1 for one of **20 permanent buffs**, paid in Ourosangue. | Level and Prestige |
| Classes | Everyone starts as an *Awakened*; 11 classes with 3 evolutions each. **Class bonuses apply only with the class weapon in hand.** | Classes |
| Weapons | Each weapon type has its own level (0–100) and prestige (up to 20). | Weapon Mastery |
| Blood | Each blood type has its own level and prestige, raised only by draining (the final bite). | Blood Lineage |
| Staff | For Draekhar and Sangrael, the game's **Spear works as a Staff** and even looks like one. | Classes |

## Economy

| Topic | On Sangria Falls | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Currencies | A **Bank** holds five balances: Ourosangue, Sangricoins, Quitina, Ressonador and Cristais. Balances don't take inventory space and aren't lost on death. | Bank |
| Trading | A player **Market** in the city, Balthazar's contracts, and the Shadow Merchant, an automatic buyer you can open anywhere. | Market, Contracts, Shadow Merchant |
| Professions | 3 crafting and 3 gathering trades. Gathering pays Ourosangue on every vein, catch or tree. | Professions |

## World and castle

| Topic | On Sangria Falls | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Hub | The **City of Sangria Falls** has the NPCs, halls, Bank, Market and Events Hub, linked by a teleport hub. | First steps |
| Teleports | Free saved teleport points (1, or 2 with VIP), usable from anywhere, plus a global teleport to the city. A personal point needs a castle. | Teleports |
| Soul Shards | **No longer drop on the ground.** When Solarus, Megara, Dracula, Talzur or Adam die in the open world, the killer and players near the body each get a chance to receive the shard by mail. | Castle |
| Protected items | Legendary weapons, Dracula armour, T8 amulets and Soul Shards can't be dropped on the ground. | Castle |
| Castle helpers | A key (G) stores your bag in matching chests; a chest list (=) finds any item; the whole clan is warned when the Castle Heart has 3 days of essence or fewer left. | Castle |
| Logging out | A few seconds after you leave, your body moves to a safe spot and returns when you log back in, unless you left dead, mounted or in your coffin. | First steps |
| Rift Incursions | Fragments from rift portals go **straight to your inventory** instead of dropping on the ground. | Rift Incursions |

## Companions and events

| Topic | On Sangria Falls | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Pets | Open-world bosses have a 1% chance per kill to become a pet. Some bosses can't be captured. | Pets |
| Group content | Dungeons (2–4 players), Reality Rupture, Illusory Arena, Tower of the Immortals, Goreswine's Judgment and Rift Incursions. | Dungeons and event guides |

## What this page doesn't cover

This wiki doesn't document the base game. For castle building, crafting stations, the map and V Blood locations, use V Rising resources. Keep in mind that Sangria Falls may change loot, recipes or numbers without an official guide saying so (see [open question Q-008](/about/open-questions/#q-008)).
