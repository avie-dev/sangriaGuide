Each mistake below comes from a rule in the official guides.

## Progression

**Hunting at level 90 without prestiging.** At 90, XP stops counting and is lost. Prestige before you go back to hunting. If you hid the Prestige Bar, you can still prestige from the Profile (F1).

**Leaving prestige money in your inventory.** Prestige, evolutions and most costs come from your **Bank balance**. If the Bank is short, the prestige fails and chat shows the cost and your balance.

**Paying for a weapon's or blood's first prestige.** Vlad Parker does the **first** weapon and the **first** blood prestige for free. When something hits level 100 for the first time, talk to him before clicking PRESTIGE.

**Holding the wrong weapon.** Class bonuses apply **only with your class weapon in hand**. Weapon mastery XP goes to whatever weapon you're holding at the moment of the kill, and fighting unarmed gives none.

**Killing without draining.** Blood lineage XP comes **only from draining** (the final bite). Plain kills raise your character and weapon, not your blood. With Frail blood you get no lineage XP at all.

**Hunting far below your level.** Weaker creatures give less XP, and the bigger the gap, the less. Docile creatures and those from tombs, worm nests and summoning circles also give less, and boss summons give nothing.

## Meditation

**Choosing the wrong path.** Meditation levels one path at a time: character, weapon, blood or pet. The Weapon path needs the weapon in your hand, the Blood path needs an active blood, and the Pet path needs your pet summoned inside the chamber.

**Expecting personal bonuses to apply.** Quitina and the welcome gift don't count in meditation. Only VIP and server XP events do.

**Running out of Sangricoins.** Meditation stops when your balance drops below 0.35. Eight hours on one path costs 168 Sangricoins.

## Instant buttons with no confirmation

These act the moment you click, with no "are you sure?":

- character, weapon and blood **PRESTIGE** on the HUD (the Ourosangue is charged at once)
- profession **Prestige** in F4
- Market **Buy**
- removing a teleport point (the trash can)
- using Ressonador in the Sangrulhos panel
- deleting mail with `.mail deletar`

<Aside type="tip">
To see a prestige's cost before paying, type `.level` or ask Vlad Parker (*I want to prestige.*). He only prestiges after you click **Confirm.**
</Aside>

## Professions and income

**Changing professions casually.** Your first change in each category is free. After that, each change costs 1,000 Sangricoins and needs 120 days since the last change.

**Expecting bonuses from profession levels.** Level alone gives nothing. Bonuses come from **prestige**, and level unlocks better orders.

**Picking the wrong Outpost.** You get **one** daily and **one** weekly Outpost mission in total, not one per outpost, and you can't switch or abandon them. At prestige 0 and below level 50, start at Farbane.

**Accepting contracts you can't finish.** Balthazar allows 3 accepts a day. Abandoning one frees the slot but doesn't give you back the day's accept.

## Getting around

**Trying to teleport out of trouble.** Teleports are refused in combat, while downed, while talking to an NPC and inside events. Teleporting **into** a boss arena is refused, but teleporting **out** is allowed.

**Losing your teleports.** Personal points need a castle (yours or your clan's). Let the Castle Heart run dry and they're erased.

## Quests

**Closing a conversation early.** A quest step counts only when you reach **Finish**.

**Letting dailies expire.** Daily quests reset at midnight, and weekly and hunt quests on Monday at midnight (Brasília time). An unfinished one disappears.

**Skipping the tutorial.** It can't be abandoned, it pays XP at every step, and other classes, meditation and dungeon invites wait until it's done.
