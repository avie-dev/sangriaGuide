Sangria Falls is a V Rising server that layers a full RPG on top of the base game. Your vampire gets a character level, prestiges, a class, weapon and blood progression, captured pets, professions and a set of instanced events. It all ties back to a shared hub, the **City of Sangria Falls**.

The staff run the server and its official site, [sangriafalls.com](https://www.sangriafalls.com/en/). This wiki is an unofficial, English-first companion to that site. The main server it targets is **[GLOBAL] Sangria Falls [RPG 04] [2X]**.

## What the server adds

Each item below is a system the official guides describe. The rest of this wiki has a section for each.

- **Level and prestige.** Your vampire goes from level 1 to 90. That level replaces the game's gear level as the number shown on your character. At 90 you prestige back to level 1 for a permanent buff, 20 times in all. See [Character](/character/).
- **Classes.** Everyone starts as an *Awakened*. There are 11 classes, taught by Masters in the Classes Hall, each with its own weapons and three evolutions. Your first class is free. See [Classes](/classes/).
- **Weapon mastery and blood lineage.** Every weapon type and every blood type levels up and prestiges on its own, and each feeds bonuses to your class.
- **Pets.** World bosses you defeat have a small chance to become a pet that fights beside you. See [Pets and companions](/companions/).
- **Professions.** Pick one crafting and one gathering trade, level them to 100 and prestige them up to 10 times. See [Professions](/professions/).
- **A server economy.** A Bank holds five currencies (Ourosangue, Sangricoins, Quitina, Ressonador and Cristais), with a player Market, contracts and an automatic buyer. See [Economy](/economy/).
- **Events.** Dungeons for groups of 2 to 4, the Reality Rupture, the Illusory Arena, the Tower of the Immortals, Goreswine's Judgment and open-world Rift Incursions. See [Dungeons](/dungeons/) and [Events](/events/).
- **Endgame.** After prestige 20 come Vampiric Schools, then Pulses, Transfusions and Plasmas. See [Endgame](/endgame/).

## How your first hour goes

1. Create your character and leave the coffin. A tutorial starts on its own and pays XP at every step.
2. The tutorial walks you through levels, classes, prestige, a gathering profession, ways to earn Ourosangue and the Sangrulhos. You pick your first class during it.
3. It ends by taking you to the City of Sangria Falls, where a teleport hub links every hall on the server.

The [Progression walkthrough](/walkthrough/) follows the whole journey from there, stage by stage.

## Common questions

### Is Sangria Falls the same as vanilla V Rising?

No. Castle building and much of the open world are the base game, but levels, classes, professions, pets, the economy and the events all follow Sangria Falls rules. See [Sangria Falls vs vanilla V Rising](/getting-started/vs-vanilla/).

### Do I have to pay?

You need to own V Rising. The official guides describe some optional extras that come from donations (VIP, the Bloody Pass, Double Meditation). The wiki marks them with the **VIP** and **Purchase** labels.

### Which language does the server use?

Portuguese, English and Spanish are supported. See [Setting the language to English](/getting-started/language/).
