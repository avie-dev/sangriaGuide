The official guides don't keep a single NPC list. This page collects every NPC they mention by name, with what each one does and where the guides place them. Talk to an NPC with **F**. NPCs that offer a service open a menu. If they're part of one of your quests, the first option is *Continue: quest name*.

The [full NPC reference](/reference/npcs/) also lists Outpost commanders, school professors and event NPCs.

<Aside type="note" title="Class Master names">
The official class pages give some Masters a title, such as *Dama* Lilith Noctarin, *Lady* Aria Bastien and *Lord* Draco Skarn. The table uses the short names that the guides use in running text.
</Aside>

## Talking to NPCs

- Walk up and press **F**. With the interface, a conversation bar opens at the bottom. Click **Continue** to advance and **Finish** on the last line. Without it, the conversation appears in chat as a numbered menu.
- A quest step counts only when you reach **Finish**. Closing early means talking to the NPC again.
- Some NPCs also tell their own story through an *About (name)* option. At the time of writing these are your Class Master, Ornn, Richard, Sebastian and Viktor.

See also [Navigating Sangria Falls City](/getting-started/city/).
