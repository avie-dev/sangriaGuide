NPCs that offer a service open a menu. If they're part of one of your quests, the first option is *Continue: quest name*.

## Talking to NPCs

- Walk up and press **F**. With the interface, a conversation bar opens at the bottom. Click **Continue** to advance and **Finish** on the last line. Without it, the conversation appears in chat as a numbered menu.
- A quest step counts only when you reach **Finish**. Closing early means talking to the NPC again.
- Some NPCs also tell their own story through an *About (name)* option. At the time of writing these are your Class Master, Ornn, Richard, Sebastian and Viktor.
