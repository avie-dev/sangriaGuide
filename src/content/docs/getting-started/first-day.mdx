## The tutorial

<Checklist
	id="first-day-tutorial"
	items={[
		'Leave the coffin (without the interface: choose your language with the NPCs or .lang en)',
		'Follow every tutorial step; each one pays XP',
		'Choose your free first class in the Classes Hall',
		'Pick a variant (Mortak, Sepulkros) or Current (Draekhar) if your class has one',
		'Claim the starter kit from your Mail (F6 or .mail) if your inventory was full',
		"Choose a gathering and a crafting profession with Helena Valerius",
		'Reach the City of Sangria Falls',
	]}
/>

<Aside type="tip" title="Use the welcome gift">
Once you choose your class, character XP counts double for **3 days**. Day one is a good time to level hard. A Quitina won't stack on character XP while the gift is active (it's refused and not spent).
</Aside>

## In the city

<Checklist
	id="first-day-city"
	items={[

## Your first routine

<Checklist
	id="first-day-routine"
