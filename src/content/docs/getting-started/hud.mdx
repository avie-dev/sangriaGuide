This page describes the screen **with the interface** installed. Without it there are no hotkeys or on-screen bars. See [Playing without the interface](/getting-started/playing-without-the-interface/).

## Panel hotkeys

| Key | Opens |
|---|---|
| F1 | Profile |
| F2 | Pets |
| F3 | Quests |
| F4 | Professions |
| F5 | Bank |
| F6 | Mail |
| F7 | Sangrulhos |
| F8 | VIP |
| F9 | Achievements |
| F10 | Meditation |
| F11 | School |
| F12 | Transfusion |
| L | Market (only inside the city) |
| G | Stores your bag items in your castle chests (inside your castle) |
| = | Lists your castle chests (inside your castle) |

**Esc** closes any panel, and pressing a panel's key again also closes it. You can rebind keys in the game's **Controls** options tab. Panels also open from their NPC or by command.

## What's on screen

**Right side: your quests.** The QUESTS list shows up to 3 quests with each one's current step (2 inside the Arena, Tower, Rupture and Judgment). The current objective is always here. Pinned quests go to the top. The *view quests* button opens the full panel (F3). Inside a dungeon, this list gives way to the dungeon board.

**XP bar.** It shows your level next to it (for example "Lv. 35"). Hover over it to see how much XP is left to the next level.

**Prestige Bar.** It appears next to the HUD when something can prestige: your character at level 90, or a weapon or blood at 100. Its **PRESTIGE** button acts instantly, with no confirmation. After prestige 20 it shows **PULSE** and **TRANSFUSE** when they're available.

**Bottom bar: class crest.** Click it to open your class's details and the full class list. A round button next to it switches Draekhar Currents, Sepulkros and Mortak variants, or summons, depending on your class. Your school spell's cooldown also appears next to the crest.

**Pet HUD.** Your summoned pet's health and XP appear just below yours. Click its portrait to open its sheet.

**Active Events board.** Rift Incursions, school events, castle essence warnings and similar events appear here, above the quests.

**XP bonus line.** When a server XP bonus is on, *XP BONUS ACTIVE* appears at the top of the screen. Type `.xp` for details.

**Restart notice.** It appears at the top 30 minutes before a server restart, with a sound, and becomes a countdown in the last 30 seconds.

## Buttons around the minimap

Most of these appear after the opening tutorial or after a specific quest.

| Button | Opens |
|---|---|
| Gear | HUD Settings: turn boxes on or off, change the HUD preset, Boss Tracker settings |
| Quests | The quest panel |
| Teleport | Your teleport panel (after Vlad Parker's quest), plus one-click buttons for your points |
| Golden city button | Teleports to the City of Sangria Falls |
| Bank icon | The Bank |
| Ticket | Battle Pass (a red dot means rewards are waiting) |
| Two people | Group board |
| Trophy | Server rankings |
| Medal | Achievements |
| Book | The Lost Book (after the first tutorial quest) |

## Rearranging the HUD

1. Click the **gear** around the minimap. It appears after the opening tutorial.
2. In **HUD Settings**, turn each box (quests, XP, teleport, bosses and others) on or off.
3. At the top, the **HUD Preset** card changes the main layout: *Sangria Falls* (default), *AION 2*, *League of Legends* or *Dota 2*.

The same screen hides the XP notices: **Experience HUD** (XP gains in the corner), **Prestige Bar** and **XP Bonus Notice**.

<Aside type="caution">
If you hide the Prestige Bar, remember that XP gained at level 90 is lost until you prestige. You can also prestige from the Profile (F1), below the XP bar.
</Aside>
