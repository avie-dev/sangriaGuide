- **Brand new?** Read [What is Sangria Falls?](/getting-started/what-is-sangria-falls/), then [Joining the server](/getting-started/joining/) and [Installing the interface](/getting-started/interface/).
- **Know V Rising or Bloodcraft?** Skim [Sangria Falls vs vanilla](/getting-started/vs-vanilla/) and [Sangria Falls vs Bloodcraft](/getting-started/vs-bloodcraft/). Most of what you know about progression won't carry over.
- **Just finished the tutorial?** Use the [first-day checklist](/getting-started/first-day/) and the [first-week roadmap](/getting-started/first-week/), or follow the [Progression walkthrough](/walkthrough/).
