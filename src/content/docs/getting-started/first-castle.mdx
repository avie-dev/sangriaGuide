Placing a Castle Heart, building walls and crafting stations work as in the base game, and this wiki doesn't document them. This page covers what **Sangria Falls adds**, and why your castle matters early on.

## Why you need a castle early

- **Teleports need it.** A personal teleport point requires a castle, either yours or your clan's. During Vlad Parker's quest *The Blood Baptism*, your first point **must be created inside your castle**. See [Teleports](/getting-started/teleports/).
- **Rift Incursion warnings need it.** You only see Rift Incursions if an **Eye of Twilight** is built in your castle or your clan's castle.
- **Finding a plot:** during the starting mission that asks you to build a castle, *Free Plot* and *Decaying Plot* icons appear on your map. They disappear once you place the Heart. There's no player command to list free plots.

## Store your bag with one key

Your bag goes into the castle chests that already hold the same item. This works on your castle's grounds or your clan's.

<PlayModes>
	<Fragment slot="interface">Press **G**, or double-click quickly on your inventory's sort button. Chat shows a **[STORE]** line with how many items went into how many chests.</Fragment>
	<Fragment slot="chat"><Command cmd=".guardar">Stores your bag in the castle chests that already have the same item.</Command></Fragment>
</PlayModes>

- **Teach a chest first.** An item is only stored if some chest already holds at least one. Put the first unit in by hand, and next time the key handles it.
- Your hotbar (the first 8 slots), your equipped weapon and quest items never leave the bag.
- A chest with **privado** in its name is skipped. Use that for a personal chest.
- It doesn't work in combat or while downed. If a chest fills up, the rest stays in your bag.

## Find any item: Chest Management

Interface only. Inside your castle, press **=** (or type `.baus`) to open CHEST MANAGEMENT:

<Steps>

1. Search by name or click a category. Each line shows the item, the total and which chests hold it. Unnamed chests appear as B1, B2, B3 and so on.
2. Click an item's name, and a beam of light shines over the chests that have it for 15 seconds.
3. Star up to 20 favourites to keep them in the last column.

</Steps>

Name your chests (Wood, Gems, Fish…). The names show in the panel. The **Take** button, which pulls items from the chests straight to your bag, is VIP-only. <Label type="vip" /> Everyone else uses the beam of light and fetches the item by hand.

## Don't let the Heart run dry

When your Castle Heart has **3 days of essence or fewer** left:

- the whole clan gets a **[CASTLE]** warning in chat and a letter titled *YOUR CASTLE WILL FALL*, which even reaches offline members
- the letter repeats at most once a day while the essence stays low, and players are warned again when they log in
- a *Your Castle Will Fall* card appears on the Active Events board, turning red under 24 hours

If the essence runs out, the castle starts to decay. If you lose your Castle Heart (and your clan has none), your teleport points are erased.

## Protected items

Legendary weapons, Dracula armour, T8 amulets and Soul Shards **can't be dropped on the ground**. Chat shows **[PROTECTED ITEM]** and the item stays put. On death, the game's normal rule applies. A new clan member can't move these items into or out of another member's castle chests for 12 hours.

<Aside type="tip">
In a clan? Store and Chest Management work in any castle of your clan. They don't work in a friend's castle outside your clan.
</Aside>
