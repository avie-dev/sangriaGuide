The interface is optional. Without it there are no panels or hotkeys, but almost every system works through **chat**. You get numbered menus you answer by typing a number, and direct commands for common actions. The official site has a chat-only version of every guide (the "without interface" variants). This wiki shows both ways in tabs wherever they differ, and remembers which one you picked.

## The general menu

<Steps>

1. Type `.menu` in chat. A numbered list appears.
2. Type the number of the option you want, for example `2` for Pets.
3. Type `0` to go back. On the first screen, `0` closes the menu.

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution" title="Stand still">
Menus close if you walk more than 5 metres while they're open. Chat tells you when that happens. Type the command again and stay put.
</Aside>

The menu has two screens. The first has Character, Pets, Quests, Teleport, Bank, Market, Mail and Sangrulhos. **More systems** has Battle Pass, School, Achievements, Professions, Meditation, VIP, Dungeons, Rankings, and the weapon, blood and auto-prestige options.

## Talking to NPCs

Interact with an NPC (F) and the conversation appears in chat. Options come numbered under the NPC's name, and you reply by typing the number (`0` exits). Wait for an NPC to finish talking before you interact again.

## Useful commands for your first days

Type commands exactly as shown. Many contain Portuguese words (`.tp criar`, `.pet invocar`), and that's how the server expects them.

<Command cmd=".menu">Opens the server's general menu.</Command>
<Command cmd=".quest">Lists your active quests and the current step of each.</Command>
<Command cmd=".lang en">Switches the server to English (`pt` and `es` also work).</Command>
<Command cmd=".level">Opens the character menu: level, prestige and the cost of your next prestige.</Command>
<Command cmd=".menu bank">Opens the Bank menu: balances, statement, deposit, withdrawal and exchange.</Command>
<Command cmd=".mercado go">Teleports you to the market, in the City of Sangria Falls.</Command>
<Command cmd=".menu teleporte">Opens the teleport menu.</Command>
<Command cmd=".tp criar casa">Creates a personal teleport point called "casa" where you stand.</Command>
<Command cmd=".tp ir casa">Teleports to your point called "casa".</Command>
<Command cmd=".meditate">Opens the meditation menu.</Command>
<Command cmd=".pet">Opens the pets menu.</Command>
<Command cmd=".prof">Opens the professions menu.</Command>
<Command cmd=".class">Opens the classes menu.</Command>
<Command cmd=".online">Shows your online time and Online Reward streak.</Command>
<Command cmd=".mail">Opens your mail.</Command>
<Command cmd=".top">Opens the server rankings.</Command>

The official [commands page](https://www.sangriafalls.com/en/guias/comandos/) lists all 309 player commands by system. It's in Portuguese, even on the English site, and its examples use the Portuguese spellings (for example `.nivel` where the English guides use `.level`).

## Cutting down chat spam

<Command cmd=".level log">Turns character XP messages on or off. `.weapon log`, `.blood log` and `.pet log` do the same for weapon, blood and pet XP.</Command>
<Command cmd=".notice interface">Turns off the reminder to install the interface that appears when you log in. Type it again to turn it back on.</Command>

## The website panel: clicks without the mod

The [website panel](/getting-started/website-panel/) at sangriafalls.com/painel is a second screen in your browser. Sign in with Steam while you're online in game, and you can do most of what the in-game panels do: prestige, meditation, teleports, market, mail, collections and more. The staff describe it as the graphical option for players without the interface.

## What you miss without the interface

The official guides say almost everything works without it, and each guide warns you when a screen exists only in the interface. Examples mentioned in the guides:

- The Profile panel, with picture, summed attributes and statistics. Without it, separate chat commands show your progress.
- Chest Management, the searchable list of everything in your castle chests.
- Hotkeys and on-screen bars, such as the XP bar, Prestige Bar and quest list.

## Common questions

### I typed a number and nothing happened.

The menu closed, either because you walked or because none was open. Type `.menu` again and stand still.

### I can't leave the crypt at the start.

Without the interface you first choose your language. Three NPCs appear when you leave the coffin, one per language. Talk to the English one or type `.lang en`. The crypt door opens only after you choose.
