Every new character starts a guided tutorial. It teaches the server's systems one at a time and pays rewards along the way. The quest that leads to your first class is **The Path of Blood**. Until you finish it, Class Masters won't offer other class journeys or class switches. Follow the tutorial to the end: each step pays XP, and it's the fastest way to earn your first levels.

## What the tutorial covers

According to the official First steps guide, the tutorial introduces:

1. your **level**
2. the **classes** (you choose your first one, for free, in the Classes Hall)
3. **prestige**
4. your **gathering profession**
5. ways to earn **Ourosangue**, in the *Earning Ourosangue* quest: the Shadow Merchant, Balthazar's contracts and the Outposts
6. the **Sangrulhos**

It ends by taking you to the City of Sangria Falls.

Other guides mention more tutorial details:

- **Helena Valerius** lets you choose one gathering and one crafting profession during the tutorial.
- The *Earning Ourosangue* quest includes a one-off apprentice contract from Balthazar: 1,000 wood for 1,500 Ourosangue. It doesn't count towards the daily contract limit.
- **Vlad Parker's** quest *The Blood Baptism* unlocks teleports. During that quest, you must create your teleport point inside your castle.

The official guides don't publish the full step list. Follow the on-screen objective; it always shows what to do next.

## How to follow it

<Steps>

1. **Create your character and leave the coffin.** The first quest appears on its own. (Without the interface, first choose your language. See [Setting the language to English](/getting-started/language/).)

2. **Watch the current objective.**
   <PlayModes>
   	<Fragment slot="interface">It's always on the right side of the screen. Press **F3** for all your quests and their progress.</Fragment>
   	<Fragment slot="chat">It appears in chat with the **[QUEST]** tag. Forgot it? Type `.quest`.</Fragment>
   </PlayModes>

3. **Talk to NPCs when asked.** Walk up and interact (**F**). With the interface, a conversation bar opens. Click **Continue** to advance and **Finish** on the last line. Without it, the conversation appears in chat and you answer by typing numbers.

4. **Choose your class** when the tutorial sends you to the Classes Hall. See [Choosing your first class](/getting-started/first-class/).

5. **Keep going to the end.** The tutorial finishes in the city, at the teleport hub.

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution" title="A step only counts when you reach Finish">
With the interface, closing a conversation early (End conversation or the X) doesn't complete the step. Talk to the NPC again and click through to **Finish**.
</Aside>

## Rewards worth knowing

- **XP at every step**, plus the **welcome gift**: once you choose your class, character XP counts double for 3 days.
- **Currency:** you start with 25 Sangricoins and earn 10,000 Ourosangue during the tutorial. Both go to your Bank (F5 or `.menu bank`).
- **Your class's iron weapons and a starter kit** when you pick your class. Anything that doesn't fit in your inventory goes to your Mail (F6 or `.mail`).

## Some things wait until it's done

The official guides tie a few things to finishing the tutorial:

- **Other classes:** Masters only offer class journeys and switches after *The Path of Blood*.
- **Meditation:** the MEDITATE button only works once the starter tutorial is finished.
- **Pets:** you can't summon a pet during the initial tutorial.
- **Dungeons:** a friend can't invite you until you've finished the starter tutorial.
- **Double XP from Quitina:** it unlocks only after you choose a class in the tutorial.

## Common questions

### Can I skip tutorial steps?

Tutorial quests can't be abandoned, and step skipping only exists on quests that can be abandoned. You need to play it through.

### An NPC repeats the same line and gives me nothing.

Either you already have that quest, or a requirement is missing. Check the current step:

<Command cmd=".quest">Lists your active quests and each one's current step.</Command>
