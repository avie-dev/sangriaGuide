Bloodcraft is a separate V Rising mod used on other servers. **The official Sangria Falls guides never mention it**, and this wiki doesn't treat it as a source. If you've played Bloodcraft servers, some Sangria Falls systems will cover similar ground: character levels, weapon and blood progression, classes, professions and boss companions. Don't carry over Bloodcraft's numbers, commands or rules. Learn the Sangria Falls version below.

<Aside type="caution" title="Commands are different">
Sangria Falls has its own set of over 300 player commands, listed on the official commands page. Don't type commands you remember from another server. Use the ones on each wiki page, exactly as written.
</Aside>

## The Sangria Falls version of each system

| If you're looking for… | On Sangria Falls | Key facts from the official guides |
|---|---|---|
| Character levels | **Level and Prestige** | Levels 1–90; 20 prestiges with a fixed buff each, in a fixed order; paid in Ourosangue (75 for the first, 4,467 for the twentieth). |
| Weapon progression | **Weapon Mastery** | Each weapon type levels 0–100 and prestiges up to 20. XP goes to the weapon in your hand at the moment of the kill. Your class chooses 3 mastery attributes. |
| Blood progression | **Blood Lineage** | Each blood type levels 0–100 and prestiges up to 20. Only draining (the final bite) gives XP. Your class chooses 3 lineage attributes. |
| Classes | **Classes** | 11 classes taught by Masters, 3 paid evolutions each (15,000 / 30,000 / 45,000 Ourosangue). Three classes need evolutions in other classes first. Bonuses need the class weapon in hand. |
| Professions | **Professions** | One crafting and one gathering trade at a time; levels 0–100 and 10 prestiges; bonuses come only from prestige, not level. |
| Boss companions | **Pets** | 1% capture chance per open-world boss kill; tiers T0–T5 raised with souls; levels to 100 and 20 prestiges; Cristais add up to 5 extra attributes. |
| Endgame | **Pulses, Transfusions and Plasmas** | After prestige 20, each return to level 90 is a Pulse; 10 Pulses plus Ourosangue make a Transfusion that yields plasmas for 8 ancestors. |

## Habits to unlearn

These are Sangria Falls rules a returning player may not expect:

- **Class bonuses switch off** when you hold a weapon that isn't your class's.
- **Prestiging is instant**, with no confirmation screen, on the HUD Prestige Bar. The cost comes from your Bank balance, not your inventory.
- **XP at level 90 is lost** until you prestige.
- **Group XP isn't split.** Anyone nearby and in combat at the kill gets full XP.
- **Profession levels give no bonus by themselves.** Only prestige does.
