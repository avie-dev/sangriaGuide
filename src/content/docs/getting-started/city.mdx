The **City of Sangria Falls** is the server's meeting point. Its NPCs, halls, Market and Events Hub are all reached from here. You arrive at the end of the tutorial, landing at the **teleport hub** in the middle of the city.

## Getting to the city

Going to the city is free and instant from anywhere. It has a 30-second cooldown, and it unlocks when Vlad Parker unlocks teleports.

<PlayModes>
	<Fragment slot="interface">Click the **golden city button** next to the minimap, or open the teleport panel and click **Go** on *City of Sangria* under GLOBAL TELEPORTS.</Fragment>
	<Fragment slot="chat">
		<Command cmd=".mercado go">Teleports you to the market in the city, with no wait.</Command>
		<Command cmd=".tp global cidade_sangria">Teleports you to the City of Sangria Falls.</Command>
	</Fragment>
</PlayModes>

## The teleport hub

In the middle of the city stands a ring of braziers. Each one has its destination's name above it. Walk up and interact. They cost nothing and have no wait. Most are round trips, with a brazier on the other side that brings you back, but some are one-way only.

| Destination | What's there | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Schools | Frosthiel, Luminara, Serphentis and Sanguinis headquarters | Only members of that school can pass |
| Sangria Bank | Vaults (in the north of Dunley) | Opens after Vlad Parker's teleport quest |
| Events Hub | Dungeons, Illusory Arena, Tower, Reality Rupture, Goreswine's Judgment | Each event starts with its NPC here |
| Classes Hall | The 11 Class Masters | Where you choose and evolve classes |
| Training Room | Darius, free class testing | |
| Professions Hall | The six profession mentors | |
| Sangrulhos | Richard, master of the Sangrulhos | |
| Meditation | The Meditation Chamber, with Haruki | |
| Museum | | Not described in the official guides yet |

## Other places in the city

The official guides also place these in the city:

- **The Market.** Press **L** (only works inside the city). Players buy shops at stalls named *Empty shop*.
- **City Hall**, where Balthazar, the Master of Contracts, stands. Its second floor, above the teleport hub, has the **Casino** machines.
- **The Winery (Adegaria)**, where Vlad Parker handles prestige.
- **The main building, first floor**, where Sebastian handles Vampiric School enrolment.
- **Mission Boards**: chalkboards labelled *Quadro de Missões* that show the seven Outposts.
- **A-mór, the Cupid**, who handles the Blood Bond (marriage).

## City rules

- **No pets.** If yours is summoned when you teleport in, it's put away automatically. (You *can* summon it inside the Meditation Chamber.)
- **No personal teleport points** can be created inside the city.
- **The Market panel only opens inside the city.**

<Aside type="tip">
Lost? Several panels mark their NPC on your map. The School panel (F11) has a **Mark on map** button for Sebastian, and the Outpost board's **Track** button marks a commander.
</Aside>
