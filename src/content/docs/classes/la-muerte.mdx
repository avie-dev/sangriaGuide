La Muerte fires **death magic from Pistols**. Its abilities are weapon skills, but **all their damage is magic**. Every 4 basic hits, the minion you chose takes the field.

<Requirements items={['Draekhar at Evolution III', 'Sepulkros at Evolution III', 'Carmesyne at Evolution II']} />

<ClassCard id="la-muerte" />

## How it scales

- **Hybrid profile. Benefits from:** Spell Power, Spell Crit, Weapon Skill Power and Minion Damage.
- **Doesn't use:** Physical Power.
- Minions spawn with **your Spell Power, multiplied by your Minion Damage**, and can't crit.
- **Unlock quest:** *A Mãe dos Lamentos* (The Mother of Laments), from **Morrigan LaMuerte**.

## Abilities

| Key | Ability | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Basic | *Eco Fantasma* (Phantom Echo) | Each shot sounds twice, the bullet and its echo: 130% magic on the combo's first shot and 115% on the next two. The echo heals you for 5% of your max health and weakens the target by 15% for 5 s. Every 4 shots, a Phantom Legion spirit fights beside you for 12 s. |
| Q | *Salva Funerária* (Funeral Volley) | Fan the hammer and pour 8 spectral rocks in 2 s towards your aim, each dealing 150% magic to the first target it passes through. |
| E | *Último Lamento* (Last Lament) | Pass through the Veil, briefly immaterial, and fire a Banshee's wail (100% magic). Where it ends, 8 wolf spirits burst out in all directions, each dealing 80% magic to the first target in its path. |

### Passive and summons: *Legião Fantasma* (Phantom Legion)

***Cortejo da Morte* (Death's Procession).** Every 4 basic hits in combat, your chosen minion appears with your Spell Power, at your level and with double attack speed. There are **13 minions** to choose from, in the panel opened by the round button next to your class crest, or with `.lamuerte`.

Locked minions unlock when you defeat that creature while playing La Muerte with a **Minion Collector** in your inventory. Collectors come from dungeon rewards. La Muerte can have at most **4 living minions**.
