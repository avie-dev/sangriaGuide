The **Awakened** (*Despertado*) is the class every vampire wakes up with. It has **no kit of its own**: it uses the game's weapons and spells as they are, with a small general bonus, until you swear to a path in the Classes Hall.

<ClassCard id="awakened" />

## Bonus

| Attribute | Bonus |
|---|---|
| Physical Power | +5 |
| Spell Power | +5 |
| Attack Speed | +5% |
| Ability Speed | +5% |

It has a **hybrid** profile, benefiting from both Physical and Spell Power. There's no weapon restriction.

## Limits

- **No mastery or lineage attributes.** Choose a class to get the weapon mastery and blood lineage bonuses.
- **Can't enter the Tower of the Immortals.** You need one of the 11 classes.
- **No evolutions.**

## Leaving it behind

You choose your first class, for free, during the tutorial quest *The Path of Blood*. See [Choosing a class](/classes/choosing-a-class/).
