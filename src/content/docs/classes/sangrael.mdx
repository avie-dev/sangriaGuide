The official page calls Sangrael the server's **only true support**. It wounds in an area and heals allies with the same strike. Its ultimate raises every downed vampire nearby at full health.

<Requirements items={['Draekhar at Evolution I. Sangrael cannot be your first class.']} />

<ClassCard id="sangrael" />

## How it scales

- **Benefits from:** Spell Power and Spell Crit. Heals come from Spell Power.
- **Doesn't use:** Physical Power or Weapon Skill Power.
- *Sangria Menor*, *Liturgia da Ferida* and *Feixe Carmesim* count as spells. *Feixe Carmesim* does **not** count as an ultimate.
- **The Staff is the game's Spear**, and its mastery counts as Staff.
- **Unlock quest:** *O Voto do Plantão* (The Vow of the Night Shift), from **Markus Sangrael**.

## Abilities

| Key | Ability | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Basic | *Sangria Menor* (Minor Bloodletting) | Bursts blood where your cursor points: 132% magic in an area with knockback. The same pulse heals allies in the area for 50% of your Spell Power. |
| Q | *Liturgia da Ferida* (Liturgy of the Wound) | Recite for 1.8 s, immune to knockback, then burst around you: 200% magic and Vampiric Curse on those hit. After 3 s the curse detonates for 150% of your magic damage and drains 30% of it to you as health. Nearby allies recover 150% of your Spell Power, even beyond the limit of the flesh (the black bar). |
| E | *Feixe Carmesim* (Crimson Beam) | Channel a beam that deals continuous magic damage and applies Drain to enemies, while healing allies in it based on your Spell Power. Each target hit also heals you. |
| Ultimate | *Alta Carmesim* (Crimson Altar) | After a 1.5 s cast, raises **every downed vampire within 12 m at full health**, ally or not. The class summary adds that it also brings back your last pet that died in the field. Scales with Spell Power and Ultimate Efficiency. |
| Passive | *Reviver Rápido* (Quick Revive) | Reviving a downed ally takes under a second instead of eight, once every 180 s. |
