<Aside type="note" title="No tier list">
The staff publish no balance or damage data, and this wiki has no tested numbers of its own. So there's no "best class" here. The table below sorts classes by **what the official pages say they do**, so you can pick what sounds fun. Then test it in the [Training Room](/classes/training-room/), which has a damage dummy that reports your DPS.
</Aside>

## What do you want to play?

| If you want… | Look at | Why (from the official pages) |
|---|---|---|
| To be the wall | **Mortak (Unshakable)** | Lots of health and resistance; Blood Rage taunts everything within 20 m (with the achievement); an ultimate that shields you in ice |
| A heavy hitter in plate | **Mortak (Relentless)** | Less health, more damage: physical crit and faster strikes |
| Ranged physical damage | **Noctarin** | Longbow or Crossbow; every hit marks the target for chain lightning |
| Reach and control | **Bastien** | Spear and Whip; long impales and knockdowns |
| A crit duelist | **Carmesyne** | Swap Pistols and Sword to open and close distance |
| Fast melee with a pet beast | **Skarn** | Fury stacks attack speed; a beast you choose joins every 15 hits |
| A necromancer army | **Sepulkros (Summoner)** | A servant rises every 7 basic hits |
| A physical scythe | **Sepulkros (Reaper)** | Wide cuts; condemned enemies rise as your skeletons |
| A pure mage | **Draekhar** | All damage from Spell Power; 6 elemental Currents; −40% max health |
| Burst and mobility | **Umbrellis** | Three weapon types; Veil cooldown bonuses; no resistances from the class |
| To heal and revive | **Sangrael** | Damages and heals with the same strike; its ultimate revives every downed vampire within 12 m. Needs Draekhar I. |
| A magic gunslinger with minions | **La Muerte** | Pistols with all-magic damage; a minion every 4 basic hits. Long prerequisites. |
| A martial commander | **General** | Twinblades; skills that drain health. The longest prerequisites. |

## Things to weigh

- **Prerequisites.** Sangrael, La Muerte and General can't be your first class. If you're aiming for one, pick a class on its path. For example, Draekhar leads to both Sangrael (Evolution I) and La Muerte (Evolution III).
- **Weapons.** Class bonuses need the class weapon in hand, and each weapon type has its own mastery. Switching classes later means levelling a different weapon's mastery.
- **Health trade-offs.** Draekhar (−40%), Mortak Relentless (−20%) and Umbrellis Daggers (−20%) reduce max health. Umbrellis also lists no physical or spell resistance and no damage reduction.
- **Group content.** The official pages describe Sangrael as the only real support.

## It's not permanent

Your first class is free. Each class you unlock stays yours, and switching to one costs 3,000 Ourosangue at its Master. You lose nothing: evolutions, Currents, variants and attributes are all kept. See [Class evolutions and advanced classes](/classes/evolutions/).
