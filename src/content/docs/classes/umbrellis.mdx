Umbrellis is **shadow mobility and burst damage in a short window**. It has more weapons than any other class and gains **Veil cooldown** bonuses. Its attribute tables also carry **No Physical Resistance, No Spell Resistance and No Damage Reduction**.

<ClassCard id="umbrellis" />

## How it scales

- **Benefits from:** Physical Power, Physical Crit and Weapon Skill Power.
- **Doesn't use:** Spell Power. Lucile's Lethal Injection burst is converted to physical damage.
- **Unlock quest:** *A Arte da Sobrevivência Silenciosa* (The Art of Silent Survival), from **Mister Nyx Umbrellis**.
- **Required for:** General needs Umbrellis at Evolution II.

## Abilities

### Slashers

| Key | Ability | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Basic | Primary attack | Each combo strike releases one of Livith's wind blades (4.5 m range, 50%). |
| Q | *Golpe Elusivo* (Elusive Strike) | Replaces the dash with Livith's double sliding cut: two 12 m cuts towards your aim (80% and 90%) that stop at walls. 10 s cooldown. |
| E | *Camuflar* (Camouflage) | The standard camouflage. The exit strike brings Lucile's Lethal Injection burst in a 5 m radius (180%, converted to physical). |

### Twinblades

| Key | Ability | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Basic | Primary attack | Each combo strike releases a fan of three of Livith's wind blades at 45° (2.8 m range, 13% each). |
| Q | *Zagaia* (Javelin) | The standard javelin. Where the blade lands, a ring of eight wind blades bursts out (75% each). |
| E | *Queda Velada* (Veiled Fall) | Leap through the shadows to a point up to 15 m away, passing through everyone in between. On landing, the twinblades cut everything within 3.5 m (250% physical). |

### Daggers

| Key | Ability | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Q | *Chuva de Adagas* (Dagger Rain) | The standard Dagger Rain, with the class's own visual effect at the start of the throw. |

The official page lists no other changes for Daggers.
