Draekhar is the server's **pure mage**: all its damage scales with Spell Power. Instead of a fixed subclass, you choose a **Current** (*Corrente*, also translated "Chain"). Each one swaps your basic attack, Q and E for an elemental set at once.

<ClassCard id="draekhar" />

## How it scales

- **Benefits from:** Spell Power and Spell Crit.
- **Doesn't use:** Physical Power or Weapon Skill Power.
- The basic and Q are spells in every Current. The E is too in Glacial, Chaos and Storm. In Luminous, Blood and Corrupted, the E summons something that inherits your Spell Power and spell crit, so it can crit.
- **The Staff is the game's Spear.** A Spear held by a Draekhar counts as Staff for mastery.
- **Unlock quest:** *O Mapa Começa Aqui* (The Map Starts Here), from **Kaelmor Draekhar**.
- **Required for:** Sangrael (Draekhar Evolution I) and La Muerte (Draekhar Evolution III).

## The six Currents

Your **first Current is free**, chosen when you finish unlocking the class. The other five are quests from Kaelmor (*Variant* in his dialogue). With two or more, switching is free: click the round button next to your class crest and **Use**, or use `.class corrente`.

## Current abilities

**Glacial.** Basic: fires the Archmage's Crystal Lance (0.5 s cooldown). Q: the Frost Ranger's ice spikes run along the ground towards your aim (8 s). E: hurls the Winter Yeti's stalactite, 230% magic on impact (10 s). It also extends the *Arctic Storm* spell to 5 s instead of 1.2 s: 25 ice cones, freezing at the end (12 s).

**Chaos.** Basic: a projectile dealing about 52% magic that ignites the enemy. Q: a chaos tremor travels in a straight line, about 196% magic, leaving burning ground. E: a chaos dart that explodes on the target, about 225% magic, and ignites it.

**Storm.** Basic: the Gloomrot Stunner's electric dart (0.5 s). Q: the Spider Tank's heavy shot, 200% magic (11 s). E: two of Iva's electric darts, 150% magic each (9 s).

**Corrupted.** Basic: Livith's wind blade (0.5 s). Q (summon, scales with your Spell Power): throws a corruption bubble up to 15 m. It explodes in a 2.5 m radius for 200% magic and splits into 6 splashes (200% each, 1.9 m). Each leaves a corruption pool for 12 s dealing 35% magic per second (13 s cooldown). E: three projectiles from the Butcher's leap, 150% magic each (6 s).

**Luminous.** Basic: the Cardinal's Aide's holy projectile (0.5 s). Q: the Cardinal's Light Nova (16 s). E (summon): places a cocoon up to 10 m away that throws the Paladin's holy discs for 10 s, with 233% of your Spell Power and your crit (30 s).

**Blood.** Basic: Blood Spiral, 80% magic (0.5 s). Q: five projectiles from the Legion's Blood Prophet, 50% magic each (10 s). E (summon): seals a blood pact up to 10 m away, an 8 m explosion for 250% magic. As it fades, it bursts 6 times (one in the centre and five around, 2.5 m each) for 150% magic each (12 s).
