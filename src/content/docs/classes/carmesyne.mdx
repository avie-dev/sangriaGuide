Carmesyne is about **crit and mobility**. It alternates **Pistols and Sword** to open and close distance.

<ClassCard id="carmesyne" />

## How it scales

- **Benefits from:** Physical Power, Physical Crit and Weapon Skill Power.
- **Doesn't use:** Spell Power. On the sword's E, the dash, the final impact and the Holy Explosion start as magic and are converted to physical.
- **Unlock quest:** *A Pistola Prateada* (The Silver Pistol), from **Sylar Carmesyne**.
- **Required for:** La Muerte needs Carmesyne at Evolution II.

## Abilities

### Pistols

| Key | Ability | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Basic | Primary attack | The standard pistol combo without the 1-second pause on the third shot: continuous fire. |
| Q | *Giro Cruel* (Cruel Spin) | 45 bullets in 4.5 s, each dealing 85%, at −20% movement speed during the burst. 16 s cooldown. |
| E | *Bala Explosiva* (Explosive Bullet) | 50% more range. On impact it bursts a shock area on the target and applies Chaos Burn, continuous physical damage. |

### Sword

| Key | Ability | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Basic | Primary attack | The standard sword combo, with the class's own visual effects. |
| Q | *Redemoinho* (Whirlwind) | The spin is stretched to 4.5 s, followed by a Power Wave for 7.5 s when it ends. 16 s cooldown. |
| E | *Onda de Choque* (Shockwave) | Replaces the wave with Merciless Charge's dash: grabs the first enemy, brakes and bursts at the end with ignite, stun and a Holy Explosion converted to physical. 14 s cooldown. |
