Bastien mixes **reach and survival**. It controls space with the Spear and the Whip. The kit's Holy Explosion, at the whip's tip and at the end of the spear's Q, is **converted to physical damage**.

<ClassCard id="bastien" />

## How it scales

- **Benefits from:** Physical Power, Physical Crit and Weapon Skill Power.
- **Doesn't use:** Spell Power.
- **Unlock quest:** *A Lança Alugada da Luz* (The Light's Hired Lance), from **Aria Bastien**.
- **Required for:** General needs Bastien at Evolution III.

## Abilities

### Spear

| Key | Ability | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Basic | Primary attack | Each thrust is a long impale: 60% of Physical Power in a 6 m strip ahead, hitting up to 10 enemies. The normal strikes still work for harvesting. |
| Q | *Mil Lanças* (Thousand Spears) | Eight quick thrusts (17%–20% of Physical Power each) plus Valencia's spear barrage channelled alongside (200% of Physical Power in total). It ends with a Holy Explosion in an area (100%). Pressing again during the channel releases Impale (60%). |
| E | *Arpão* (Harpoon) | Throws the harpoon (220% of Physical Power), which doesn't pull the target or you. Where it lands, the Paladin's slam hits (115%, incapacitating those nearby) and holy spears rain on the nearest enemy (56% per spear). |

### Whip

| Key | Ability | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Basic | Primary attack | Each lash sets off two Thunderclaps: one at your feet (25%) and one at the tip, 5.5 m away (30%). |
| Q | *Turbilhão do Chicote Aéreo* (Aerial Whip Whirl) | The standard spin plus six Castleman crosses in two fans of three, forward and back, each dealing 35%. |
| E | *Chicote Envolvente* (Entangling Whip) | The standard entangle plus a Holy Explosion at the whip's tip, 6 m away (80%, converted to physical). |
