General is **commanding melee** with the Twinblades. Its two heavy skills mark enemies with **Bleed** (*Sangria*) and **Vampiric Curse**, draining health to you. The official page notes it has **the longest prerequisite** of any class.

<Requirements items={['Umbrellis at Evolution II', 'Mortak at Evolution III', 'Bastien at Evolution III']} />

<ClassCard id="general" />

## How it scales

- **Benefits from:** Physical Power, Physical Crit and Weapon Skill Power.
- **Doesn't use:** Spell Power. The basic attack, *Maça da Tempestade* and *Salto Empalador* are weapon strikes.
- **Unlock quest:** *A General Carmesim* (The Crimson General), from **Astra General**.

## Abilities

| Key | Ability | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Basic | Primary attack | The General's Twinblades combo. Every three strikes, the class's combat bonus is renewed. |
| Q | *Maça da Tempestade* (Storm Mace) | Throws the storm mace (60% physical) with a blade of light cutting the same line (140% physical). Anyone hit by the mace is marked with Bleed and Vampiric Curse, draining health to you. |
| E | *Salto Empalador* (Impaling Leap) | Leap to the target point and crash down: 140% physical around you, with knockback and slow. A rain of spears falls along your path, each dealing 110% physical. Anyone hit by the impact or the spears is marked with Bleed and Vampiric Curse. |
