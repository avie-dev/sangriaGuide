Noctarin is **ranged damage with mobility**. Every hit applies **Static** to the target, and the mark discharges as chain lightning whenever that target takes damage.

<ClassCard id="noctarin" />

## How it scales

- **Benefits from:** Physical Power, Physical Crit and Weapon Skill Power.
- **Doesn't use:** Spell Power. Every hit is a basic attack or weapon skill.
- **Unlock quest:** *A Flecha da Névoa* (The Arrow of the Mist), from **Lilith Noctarin**.

## Abilities

### Longbow

| Key | Ability | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Basic | Primary attack | The bow's arrow plus two lightning tendrils that converge at 9 m, the arrow's range. 0.5 s cooldown. Hits apply Static. |
| Q | *Tiro Múltiplo* (Multishot) | A single volley of 6 arrows in a 50° fan, each followed by a Storm Veil arrow at the same angle. One use per cooldown. |
| E | *Flecha Guiada* (Guided Arrow) | The standard homing arrow, out and back. Each hit drops the crossbow's Rain of Arrows centre arrow on the target, for up to 6 s. |

### Crossbow

| Key | Ability | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Basic | Primary attack | The crossbow bolt plus two lightning tendrils in a cone 5° to each side. Hits apply Static. |
| Q | *Chuva de Flechas* (Rain of Arrows) | The crossbow's standard Rain of Arrows, with the class's own visual effect on each bolt. |
| E | *Disparo Rápido* (Quick Shot) | Three arrows in a tight cone (0° and 5° each side). The first hit sets off a Thunderclap on the target. |

### Passive

- ***Marca Estática* (Static Mark).** Every hit in the kit applies Static. The mark discharges as chain lightning when the target takes damage. It doesn't scale with power.
