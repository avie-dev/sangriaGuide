Skarn is **fast melee that fights with company**. Every 15 hits in combat, the beast you chose appears at your side.

<ClassCard id="skarn" />

## How it scales

- **Benefits from:** Physical Power, Physical Crit, Weapon Skill Power and Minion Damage.
- **Doesn't use:** Spell Power.
- Beasts spawn with **85% of your Physical Power, multiplied by your Minion Damage**, and can't crit.
- **Unlock quest:** *O Uivo entre as Ruínas* (The Howl among the Ruins), from **Draco Skarn**.

## Abilities

The basic attack is the same with both weapons.

| Weapon | Key | Ability | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Both | Basic | Primary attack | Each combo strike comes with the Werewolf Chieftain's claw: a 3.6 m cone dealing 70%. Each hit stacks fury, +40% attack speed per stack, up to 4 stacks for 3 s. |
| Claws | Q | *Estocada* (Lunge) | The standard lunge plus the Dire Bear's roar (no damage): doubles your movement speed for 8 s. 12 s cooldown. |
| Claws | E | *Salto Perfurante* (Piercing Leap) | On landing, the Dire Bear's slam (200% in a 2.8 m radius) and 32 stones over 2 s (70% each). The stun and second leap are removed. 14 s cooldown. |
| Axe | Q | *Frenesi* (Frenzy) | The standard Frenzy plus the Dire Bear's roar (no damage): doubles your movement speed for 8 s. 12 s cooldown. |
| Axe | E | *Golpe Cruzado* (Cross Strike) | The standard Cross Strike plus two of Simon's axes 0.4 s later, landing 4–7.5 m around the point. 12 s cooldown. |

### Passive and summons: *Matilha Selvagem* (Wild Pack)

***Fera Interior* (Inner Beast).** Every 15 hits in combat, your chosen beast appears 3 m from you, with 85% of your Physical Power, 5 levels below yours and 50% faster. It deals physical damage and can't crit. There are **18 beasts** to choose from, in the panel opened by the round button next to your class crest, or with `.skarn`.

Locked beasts unlock when you defeat that creature while playing Skarn with a **Minion Collector** in your inventory. There's a chance on each kill, and the Collector is only consumed when it works. Collectors come from dungeon rewards. Skarn can have at most **4 living minions**, and going over kills the oldest.
