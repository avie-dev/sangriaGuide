<Aside type="note" title="No prices here">
The official site doesn't publish donation amounts, so this wiki doesn't list or estimate any. Ask the team in a Discord ticket.
</Aside>

| Unlock | Gives | Duration | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| VIP | 12 benefits (see [VIP](/support/vip/)) | A set period | Discord ticket |
| Bloody Pass | The BLOODY reward row of the Battle Pass | One season | Discord ticket |
| Double Meditation | Two meditation paths at once, each at full XP and cost | Not stated | Discord ticket |
| Extra clan slots | 0–4 extra slots per player; a clan maxes at 8 members | Not stated | Discord ticket |
| Sangricoins | Listed among Sangricoin sources ("donations") in the Bank guide | — | Not described |

**How:** join the official Discord (discord.gg/SangriaFalls) and open a ticket. The team explains the options and activates the unlock on your account. **There's no purchase inside the game.**

## Things to know

- None of these is part of another. **VIP doesn't include the Bloody Pass or Double Meditation.**
- A clan member's extra slots stay **locked to the clan** while in use. That player can't leave or be kicked until the clan fits without them.
- See [Free vs VIP](/support/free-vs-vip/) for what VIP changes in practice.
