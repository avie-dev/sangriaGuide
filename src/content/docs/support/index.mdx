| Unlock | What it is | Part of VIP? |
|---|---|---|
| [VIP](/support/vip/) | A single, time-limited supporter status with 12 benefits | — |
| Bloody Pass | The second reward row of the [Battle Pass](/support/battle-pass/), for one season | No |
| [Double Meditation](/meditation/double-meditation/) | Two meditation paths at once | No |
| Extra clan slots | Up to 4 per player (clan maximum 8) | No |

All of these are **unlocked by donation**: open a ticket on the official Discord (discord.gg/SangriaFalls), and the team explains the options and activates them. **There's no purchase inside the game.** This wiki doesn't list prices, because the official site doesn't publish them. See [Optional purchases](/support/optional-purchases/).

The free **Battle Pass** row is for everyone.
