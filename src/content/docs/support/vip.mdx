VIP is a **single status**, with no plans or tiers, lasting for a set period. It's **unlocked by donation**: open a ticket on Discord (discord.gg/SangriaFalls) and the team activates it. There's **no in-game purchase**. Benefits start immediately, or at your next login if you're offline.

## Benefits

<FilterTable placeholder="Filter benefits…">

| Benefit | What it does |
|---|---|
| Enhanced Speed | +1.6 movement speed, all the time, even after dying |
| Auto-Prestige | Prestiges character, weapon, blood and pet automatically, and pulses ([details](/meditation/auto-prestige/)) |
| Experience Bonus | +50% XP **when meditating** (cost unchanged) |
| VIP Teleport | 2 personal teleports instead of 1 |
| Willfred Primal Pet | An exclusive pet while VIP lasts |
| Boosted Horse | Speed 15 instead of 10, acceleration 8 instead of 7, faster turning (summon it and dismount once to apply) |
| Exclusive Auras | One of 28 visual-only auras |
| Login Phrase | Your own coloured phrase, announced in global chat when you log in (registered by the team) |
| Chest Management Take | Pull items from castle chests straight from the = panel |
| Transfusion Discount | 8,000 Ourosangue instead of 10,000 |
| Plasma Presets | 3 saved distributions instead of 1 |
| Sangrulho Incubation | 4 eggs at once instead of 2 |

</FilterTable>

**Not included:** the Bloody Pass and Double Meditation are separate unlocks, also by ticket.

## Checking your VIP

Press **F8**, click the crown by the minimap, or type `.vip panel`. The top card shows **VIP ACTIVE** ("Expires in 12d 4h 30min") or **YOU DON'T HAVE VIP**. Non-VIPs can open the panel too, with the benefits greyed out.

<Command cmd=".vip time">Shows the time left and the exact end date.</Command>

Benefit badges: **APPLIED**, **CONFIGURE** (Auto-Prestige), **1/2** (teleports), **1/3** (presets), **IN YOUR DECK** (Willfred), **SET/DEFAULT** (login phrase), **CHOOSE/CHANGE** (auras).

## Auras

F8 → **CHOOSE** on Exclusive Auras. Click one of the 28, and it applies at once, marked in gold. **No aura** is last in the list. After choosing, you wait **5 minutes** before choosing another, but removing it is always free. The aura survives death. Non-VIPs can preview them with `.vip aura`.

## When VIP ends

<Aside type="caution">
The benefits leave within a minute, and you get a notice. You lose the speed, Auto-Prestige, the Willfred Primal pet, and **your second teleport** (the most recently created one is erased). The plasma distribution **in use** stays active, but presets 2 and 3 lock. Transfusions go back to 10,000. Your aura choice is saved for renewal, and Willfred returns at the level he had.
</Aside>
