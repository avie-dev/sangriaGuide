## What the guides don't list

The official VIP guide and page list exactly the benefits above. They mention no effect on drop chances, capture chances, event rewards or access to content, and no such effect appears in the other guides. Meditation **cost** is explicitly unchanged. The Bloody Pass and Double Meditation are separate unlocks.

Every advantage above comes from the official VIP, Meditation, Transfusion, Sangrulhos and Teleports guides. The totals (2,500,000 vs 2,000,000) are arithmetic from the per-Transfusion cost.
