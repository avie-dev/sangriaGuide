## Earning pass XP

| Source | Pass XP |
|---|---|
| Open-world V Blood | 2–10, by boss (killer and anyone nearby in combat get the full amount) |
| Reality Rupture win | 20–70, by difficulty |
| Goreswine's Judgment win | 50 |
| Tower of the Immortals | Half the normal value per boss, only for whoever got the kill |
| Illusory Arena | None |

Each gain shows on the right as **[PASS] +5 XP**, with your level and percentage. There's one level, and it applies to both rows.

**No XP?** The usual reasons: it was in the Arena, the season has ended, you're at max level, or you didn't get the kill and weren't nearby and in combat.

## Claiming rewards

Levelling up doesn't hand anything out. **You claim each reward.** A red dot on the ticket means rewards are waiting, and the panel opens on your next level's page.

- The top row is **FREE**, and the bottom row is **BLOODY**. Each page shows 6 levels.
- Slot states: **AVAILABLE** (claim it), **CLAIMED**, **LEVEL 25** (not reached yet), **EXCLUSIVE** (Bloody only).
- **CLAIM ALL** at the top takes everything.

<Aside type="caution" title="Inventory space">
Claiming a level with an item needs **10 free slots**, and **CLAIM ALL** needs 5 per level. If space runs out, claiming stops, tells you how far it got, and nothing is lost. Currencies (Sangricoins, Ourosangue, Quitina, Ressonador) go straight to the Bank.
</Aside>

<Command cmd=".pass level 25">Shows level 25's rewards.</Command>

## The Bloody Pass

It unlocks the BLOODY row, a second reward at every level. It's obtained **by donation** through a Discord ticket. There's no in-game purchase, and **it doesn't come with VIP**. It counts **only for the season it's activated in**. Activating mid-season unlocks the Bloody rewards for levels you've already passed, so use **CLAIM ALL**. The top of the panel shows FREE or BLOODY.

## Season turnover

The panel shows **TIME LEFT**. When a season ends, XP freezes, but **claims stay open**. At the next season only levels reset, and what you claimed stays yours. The Bloody Pass doesn't carry over. Claim everything before the turnover to be safe.

**Pet Souls** from the pass: drag the item out of your inventory to add +1 soul to a random pet of that act.

**After a server wipe**, your pass level and claims stay on your account. When the team enables it, `.pass reclaimwipe` returns items from claimed levels. Currencies, pet Souls, the Bloody Pouch and the Bloody Bag don't come back.
