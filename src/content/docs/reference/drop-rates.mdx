## Published

| What | Chance |
|---|---|
| Pet capture, open-world boss kill | **1%** (a soul if you already own it) |
| Pet capture during your School's Sunday bonus | **Doubled** |
| Illusory Arena win: rare prize (1 Ressonador or 1 Pet Soul) | **5%** |
| Dungeon weapon matching your affinity, when a weapon drops | **40%** |
| Sangrulho Rare guarantee | After **15** uses without a Rare |
| Sangrulho Legendary guarantee | After **40** uses without a Legendary (**30** on Featured) |
| Sangrulho fusion catalyst | **+10%** chance |
| Fisher extra fish | **+1** every 2 prestiges (guaranteed, not a chance) |
| Miner and Woodcutter extra resources | **+5%** per prestige, rounded up |
| Outpost specialty loot | A chance on the daily; **always** on the weekly (from Rank III) |

## Mentioned but not quantified

- Dungeon weapon drop chance and material rolls. The panel at Mark shows the chances in game.
- The Master and Nightmare Special roll (Perfect Gems, Cristais, Ressonador).
- Incursion gate boss key draws ("small, not guaranteed"; high tiers rarest).
- Refining components from gathering prestige 5+ ("small", improving up to prestige 10).
- Sacrificed Souls from gathering prestige 6–9.
- Pet Cristal evolve chance (shown as CURRENT CHANCE, and rises after failures).
- Sangrulho rarity odds (shown in the Ressonador panel).
- Soul Shard chance from Solarus, Megara, Dracula, Talzur and Adam.
- Outpost Rank V rare item and legendary weapon chance.
- Casino symbol odds.

If you record any of these in game (with screenshots), please share them on the [open questions page](/about/open-questions/).
