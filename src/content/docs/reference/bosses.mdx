## General rules

- **Pet capture:** a 1% chance per open-world boss kill. Arena and dungeon bosses give nothing. Tower and castle-summoned bosses only give a soul for a pet you own. See [Capturing pets](/companions/pet-capture/).
- **Boss ranking:** only open-world kills count.
- **Boss Tracker:** shows open-world V Bloods' respawn timers. See [V Blood hunting](/events/v-blood-hunt/).
- **Battle Pass:** 2–10 pass XP per open-world V Blood.
- You can't teleport **into** a boss area, but you can teleport out.
