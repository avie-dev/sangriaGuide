Duplicates are for **fusion**: two Sangrulhos of the **same lineage** become one, for **2,000 Ourosangue**. **The result is never worse** in rarity or value, and it's born at full energy.

<Steps>

1. Click a Sangrulho → **FUSION** (or Richard → *Fuse two Sangrulhos*). It goes into **SLOT A**.
2. In the COLLECTION, click **+** on another of the same lineage. It goes into **SLOT B**.
3. Optional: add a **CATALYST** for **+10% chance**. It's consumed too.
4. Read the preview: the chance to rise in rarity and what happens to the attribute.
5. Click **FUSE**.

</Steps>

## What the result keeps

- **Two of the same type:** the best value is kept, and it can rise.
- **Different types:** the game rolls which of the two types stays.
- Rarity and value never get worse.
- Eggs can't be fused. Incubate them first.
