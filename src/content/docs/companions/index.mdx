| Companion | What it is | Open |
|---|---|---|
| [Pets](/companions/pets/) | A world boss you captured, fighting beside you. One summoned at a time; level 1–100, 20 prestiges, tiers T0–T5. | F2 or `.pet` |
| [Sangrulhos](/companions/sangrulhos/) | Collectible creatures hatched from Ressonador eggs. Up to 2 equipped, each giving one attribute bonus. | F7 or `.sangrulhos` |
| Class summons | Skarn's beasts, Sepulkros's servants and La Muerte's minions, raised by basic attacks. | See each [class page](/classes/) |
