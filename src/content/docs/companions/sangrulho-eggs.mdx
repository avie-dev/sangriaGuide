## Using Ressonador

<Steps>

1. Talk to **Richard** → *Acquire a Sangrulho*, or type `.sangrulhos resonator`.
2. The RESSONADOR panel shows your balance, the odds of each rarity, and the uses left before a guarantee.
3. Click **USE 1**, **USE 5** or **USE 10**. Each use spends 1 Ressonador from your Bank and gives one egg.
4. Flip the cards (or **REVEAL ALL**), then **CLOSE**.

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution">
No confirmation: once you click USE, it's spent. The eggs enter your collection the moment you use the Ressonador, so dropping from the server during the cards loses nothing.
</Aside>

## Guarantees

| Guarantee | After | Resets when |
|---|---|---|
| Rare | 15 uses without a Rare | You get a Rare (or a Legendary) |
| Legendary | 40 uses without a Legendary (**30 on Featured**) | You get a Legendary. A Rare doesn't reset this count. |

## Banners

- **RANDOM:** any type can come out.
- **FEATURED:** every Legendary that comes out is the featured Sangrulho.

Switching is free, and the panel shows the current feature and the upcoming ones. The official advice: use Featured only if you want that Sangrulho as a Legendary.

## Incubating

| Rarity | Incubation |
|---|---|
| Common | 8 hours |
| Rare | 24 hours |
| Legendary | 72 hours |

**2 eggs incubate at a time, or 4 with VIP.** F7 → **EGGS** → **INCUBATE**. Time runs while you're offline. When it's ready, click **HATCH**. The attribute value is only revealed then, and eggs that finished while you were offline hatch when you log in. A ready egg frees its incubation slot even before you hatch it.

**Speed-ups** (click an incubating egg): +1h for 15, +6h for 80, +24h for 300 Sangricoins, or **HATCH NOW**. Speeding up doesn't change what hatches.

## Where Ressonador comes from

- Illusory Arena (a rare prize: 5% chance per win of a Ressonador or Pet Soul)
- **Master and Nightmare** dungeons (Special roll)
- Battle Pass and events
- Richard's quest (the first 2)
