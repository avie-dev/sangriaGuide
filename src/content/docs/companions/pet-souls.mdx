Souls raise a pet's **tier**, from **T0 to T5**. Each tier makes the pet stronger, caps how far its Cristais can go, and gives it an aura (a crown at T5).

## Tier costs

| Raise to | Souls of the pet itself | Sacrificed souls from its act |
|---|---|---|
| T1 | 1 | — |
| T2 | 1 | 1 |
| T3 | 1 | 2 |
| T4 | 1 | 4 |
| T5 | 1 | 8 |
| **Total T0 → T5** | **5** | **15** |

<PlayModes>
	<Fragment slot="interface">F2 → the pet → **RAISE TIER**. The sheet shows the cost. Click **Raise** to confirm.</Fragment>
	<Fragment slot="chat"><Command cmd=".pet tier 3">Raises pet 3's tier, with no confirmation.</Command></Fragment>
</PlayModes>

## Sacrificed souls

Sacrificed souls are a **shared stock for the whole act**, and any pet from that act can donate to it.

- F2 → the donating pet → **SACRIFICE SOUL** → **Sacrifice**. Or use the **SOULS** tab, which lists every pet with a stored soul, even ones you haven't captured, then **SACRIFICE** → **CONFIRM**.
- Without the interface: `.pet sacrificar 3` sacrifices one soul from pet 3, with no confirmation.

<Aside type="caution">
Sacrificing is **permanent**. The soul doesn't return to the pet.
</Aside>

## Where souls come from

| Source | Gives |
|---|---|
| Open-world boss kill when you already own that pet | +1 soul (1% chance) |
| Tower of the Immortals boss / castle-summoned boss | A soul, if you own the pet |
| **Soul items** (Act 1–4 Soul, Primordial Soul, Special Soul) | +1 soul to a random pet of that group. Drag the item out of your inventory to use it. |
| Battle Pass | Pet Soul items at some levels |
| Illusional Arena win | 5% chance of a rare prize: 1 Ressonador or 1 Pet Soul |
| Gathering prestige 6–9 | **Sacrificed Souls**, one more act per prestige |
| Oakveil Woodlands Outpost | Specialises in pets, souls and Cristais (from Rank III) |
