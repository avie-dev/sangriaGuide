## The panel

Press **F2**. The tabs at the top are the collections: **ACT 1 to ACT 4, PRIMAL, SPECIAL, SANGRIS**, plus **SOULS** and **CRISTAIS**. Pets you don't have yet appear in black and white. Click a pet you own to open its sheet.

*Special Pets* and the *Sangris* pets (Alfa Sangris, and Homem & Brutus Sangris) only come from server events. Homem fights beside Brutus and shares his tier, Cristais, aura and crown. Rift Incursion gate bosses can also become pets, in the **Primordial** category.

## Summoning and dismissing

<PlayModes>
	<Fragment slot="interface">**F2** → click the pet → **SUMMON** (or **DISMISS**). Star up to **3 favourites** for the FAVORITE PETS strip, one click from summoning. The **Clap** emote summons your last pet, and clapping again puts it away. Click the pet's portrait on the HUD to open its sheet.</Fragment>
	<Fragment slot="chat">
		<Command cmd=".pet invocar 3">Summons pet number 3 (the number appears before each name in the menu list). Short: `.pet i 3`.</Command>
		<Command cmd=".pet retirar">Puts away the active pet (short: `.pet di`).</Command>
		<Command cmd=".pet info">The summoned pet's sheet; `.pet info 3` shows another's.</Command>
	</Fragment>
</PlayModes>

There's a 5-second wait between summons. You can't summon in combat, while downed, in bat form, in Dominant Presence or during the initial tutorial.

## Where pets can go

| Allowed | Not allowed (the pet is stored automatically) |
|---|---|
| Open world, Reality Rupture, Illusional Arena, Tower of the Immortals, the Meditation Chamber | City of Sangria Falls, dungeons, Goreswine's Judgment (the Trial), the class test room |

After bat form or travel, the pet comes back on its own. After the city, a dungeon or the test room, summon it again if it doesn't. If your pet dies, nothing is lost: leave combat and summon it again.

## Aura and crown

A pet with a tier gains an **aura**, and at **T5** a **crown**. To hide them: F2 → the pet → **VISUAL** → toggle *Body aura* or *Crown (Tier 5)*. This is cosmetic only, and the tier bonuses stay.
