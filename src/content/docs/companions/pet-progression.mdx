## Levelling

- **Hunt with the pet summoned.** It earns XP from each of your kills, and bosses give more. Its health and XP show on the pet HUD below yours. Its kills also count towards your own XP.
- **Meditation, Pet path only.** Summon the pet *inside* the Meditation Chamber and choose the Pet path. Meditating any other path gives the pet nothing. On this path the aura shows on the pet.

## Prestige

At **level 100**, a pet **PRESTIGE** button appears next to your HUD, with the pet's portrait.

<PlayModes>
	<Fragment slot="interface">Click the pet PRESTIGE button. The pet must be summoned.</Fragment>
	<Fragment slot="chat"><Command cmd=".pet prestige">Prestiges the summoned pet at level 100 (short: `.pet pg`).</Command></Fragment>
</PlayModes>

- The pet returns to level 1 but **gets stronger with each prestige**, up to 20.
- **Each prestige reduces its XP gain by 3%.**
- **Pet prestige is free.** It costs no Ourosangue.
- VIPs can let [Auto-Prestige](/meditation/auto-prestige/) handle it for the summoned pet. <Label type="vip" />
