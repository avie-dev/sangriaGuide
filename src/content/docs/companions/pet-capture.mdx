## The basic rule

Defeat a boss **in the open world**. Each kill has a **1% chance** to give you that boss as a pet. If you already own it, a successful roll gives **+1 soul** of it instead.

## Where capture works

| Where you kill it | Result |
|---|---|
| Open world | 1% pet chance (or a soul if owned) |
| Rift Incursion gate boss | Can become a pet (Primordial category) |
| Tower of the Immortals | Only a soul, and only if you already own that pet |
| Boss summoned in your castle | Only a soul, and only if you already own that pet |
| Illusional Arena | Nothing |
| Dungeons | Nothing |

## Bosses that can't be captured

**Sir Erwin, Gaius, Solarus, Talzur, Adam, Megara and Dracula** never give the pet. If you already own one of them as a pet (from an event, for example), killing it can still give a soul.

## Doubling the chance

<Aside type="tip" title="School Sunday bonus">
During your Vampiric School's **Sunday bonus** (from 19:00 Brasília time, up to 3 hours depending on the school's weekly points), your capture chance **doubles**. Schools need character prestige 20. See [Schools](/schools/).
</Aside>

## Finding bosses

The **Boss Tracker** (minimap gear → Boss Tracker → Manage) shows which V Bloods are alive and when the others respawn. Pin up to 8. It tells you *when*, not *where*: use the game's Blood Altar for directions.

VIPs also get the exclusive **Willfred Primal** pet for as long as their VIP lasts.
