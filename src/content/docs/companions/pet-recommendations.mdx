<Aside type="note" title="No pet rankings">
The official guides don't publish stats for individual pets, and this wiki has no tested data. So there's no "best pet" list here. The advice below is this wiki's, built only on published mechanics. <Label type="community" />
</Aside>

## Principles

- **Pick a pet you'll keep summoned.** A pet only levels while summoned (or on the Pet meditation path), and your favourite pet's kills also level you.
- **Concentrate sacrifices in one act.** Sacrificed souls are pooled per act, and a T5 needs 15 from its act. Spreading pets across acts splits that pool.
- **Plan Cristais around tiers.** Cristais can't evolve past the tier cap (+2 per tier), so raising the tier first avoids wasted attempts.
- **Choose Cristal attributes carefully.** The choice is final unless you pay a 75 Sangricoin RESET.
- **Remember the XP penalty.** Each pet prestige makes it gain 3% less XP. Prestiges make it stronger, but every climb takes longer.
- **Save capture hunts for Sunday.** Your School's Sunday bonus doubles the capture chance (from character prestige 20).

## Comfort tips

- Star up to 3 favourites for one-click summoning.
- The **Clap** emote summons your last pet, and clapping again dismisses it.
- The pet is stored when you enter the city, dungeons or the class test room. Summon it again afterwards if needed.
