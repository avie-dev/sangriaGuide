Each Sangrulho gives **one attribute bonus** while equipped. Two of the same type equipped add their bonuses together. The panel's **?** button opens the SANGRULHOS GUIDE, with each type's attribute range and the fusion rules.

## Getting started

**Richard's quest** gives your first **2 Ressonador** and **30 Vampiric Dust**. The tutorial also introduces the Sangrulhos. See [Sangrulho eggs](/companions/sangrulho-eggs/).

## Equipping

F7 → click a Sangrulho → **EQUIP** (or **UNEQUIP**). The strip at the bottom shows your equipped Sangrulhos and incubating eggs.

## Energy and Vampiric Dust

Energy drops **only in combat**, and only while equipped.

| Energy | Bonus |
|---|---|
| 40% and up | Full |
| Below 40% | 75% |
| Below 10% | 50% |
| 0% | None (it doesn't disappear) |

**FEED** uses 1 **Vampiric Dust** from your inventory and restores 20% energy. Below 40%, the Sangrulhos button on the HUD (the crow) shows a red dot until you feed or unequip.

<Aside type="tip" title="Vampiric Dust">
After Richard's 30, it comes from the quests of the **Oakveil Woodlands Outpost**.
</Aside>

## Collection

The tabs split your collection by lineage (**DRACONIC, GARGOYLE, RAVEN, BAT**), and the **EGGS** tab holds unhatched eggs.
