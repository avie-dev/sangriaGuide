**Cristais** give a pet extra attributes, such as health, crit and speed. Each pet chooses **up to 5 attributes**, and each can reach **+10**.

## Evolving

<Steps>

1. F2 → the pet → **CRYSTAL SYSTEM**.
2. Under AVAILABLE ATTRIBUTES, click **+** on the attribute you want.
3. Click **EVOLVE**. Each attempt spends **1 Cristal** from your Bank. The panel shows the CURRENT CHANCE.

</Steps>

**On failure**, you lose only the Cristal. The level doesn't drop, and the next chance rises a little.

<Aside type="caution">
The attribute **choice is final**. Only RESET undoes it.
</Aside>

## Tier caps

| Pet tier | Max attribute level |
|---|---|
| T0 | Can't evolve |
| T1 | +2 |
| T2 | +4 |
| T3 | +6 |
| T4 | +8 |
| T5 | +10 |

See [Pet souls and tiers](/companions/pet-souls/).

## Redistribute or reset

| Option | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **REDISTRIBUTE** | 15,000 Ourosangue | Resets the levels but keeps the chosen attributes |
| **RESET** | 75 Sangricoins | Also erases the attribute choice |

Both return **1 Cristal per level** the pet had. These returned Cristais are tied to that pet and **evolve without a roll**.

## Getting Cristais

- **Master and Nightmare dungeon rewards** (the Special roll), straight into your Bank.
- The **Oakveil Woodlands Outpost** specialises in pets, souls and Cristais (from Rank III).
- Have the item in your inventory? Deposit it:

<Command cmd=".banco depositar cristal">Deposits the Cristais from your inventory into the Bank.</Command>
<Command cmd=".pet cristais 3">Shows pet 3's Cristais.</Command>
