Every **weapon type** (sword, axe, spear, pistols…) has its own level and prestige. The more you use a weapon, the stronger the bonus it gives your class.

## Gaining mastery XP

- XP from each kill goes to **the weapon in your hand at that moment**. Fighting unarmed gives nothing.
- **V Bloods** give more.
- **Meditation** on the Weapon Mastery path raises the weapon equipped that minute.
- **No weapon XP** from boss summons or from creatures from tombs, worm nests and summoning circles.

Track gains on the Experience HUD in the corner of the screen, or with `.weapon log` in chat.

<Aside type="note" title="Draekhar and Sangrael">
For these two classes, the Spear counts as **Staff**. Its XP and prestige go to the Staff.
</Aside>

## What mastery gives

Mastery feeds **3 attributes** to your class, such as power, crit, attack speed and health. They grow with the level and prestige of **the weapon you're holding**, so a low-level weapon gives a low bonus. Each prestige returns the level to 0 but **keeps** the bonus you've earned, and it raises the cap for the next climb. Prestige doesn't slow XP.

The **Awakened** has no mastery attributes. You need a class for the bonus.

## Choosing your 3 attributes

Each class comes with 3 mastery attributes pre-selected (listed on each [class page](/classes/)). Changing them is **free**, can be redone at any time, and is saved per class. You can only change the **active** class's attributes.

<PlayModes>
	<Fragment slot="interface">
		1. Open your class's **MASTERY & BLOOD** tab.
		2. In *Weapon mastery*, check 3 attributes (the counter shows "3/3 selected"). To swap one, uncheck another first.
		3. The *Blood lineage* part must also have 3 checked.
		4. Click **CONFIRM SELECTION**. **RESET** goes back to the class default.
	</Fragment>
	<Fragment slot="chat">Type `.weapon` and choose *Change mastery attributes*.</Fragment>
</PlayModes>

## Prestiging a weapon

<Aside type="tip" title="The first one is free">
The first time a weapon reaches 100, the *Weapon Mastery* mission appears. Talk to **Vlad Parker** in the Winery before clicking PRESTIGE, and he does that first prestige for free.
</Aside>

<PlayModes>
	<Fragment slot="interface">Hold the level-100 weapon, keep the cost in your Bank, and click **PRESTIGE** on the weapon line of the Prestige Bar. It's instant and charges immediately, with no confirmation.</Fragment>
	<Fragment slot="chat"><Command cmd=".prestige weapon">Prestiges the weapon in hand immediately, without confirmation (short form `.pg a`).</Command></Fragment>
</PlayModes>

To see the cost first, ask Vlad Parker: *I want to prestige.* → *Weapon Mastery.* He only prestiges after **Confirm.** VIPs can automate weapon prestige with [Auto-Prestige](/meditation/auto-prestige/).

## Common questions

### I switched weapons and my bonuses changed.

That's expected. Only the mastery of the weapon in your hand counts.

### I can't prestige.

The weapon in your hand must be level 100 and the Ourosangue must be in your Bank. Chat says what's missing.
