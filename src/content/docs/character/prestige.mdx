At level 90, XP stops counting. **Prestige** sends you back to level 1 and gives you a **permanent buff**. There are 20 prestiges, each with its own buff, always in the same order. Buffs stack and are never lost. **Vlad Parker** teaches prestige in the quest *The Cycle of Blood*, in the Winery (Adegaria) of the City of Sangria Falls.

In game, `.level buffs` lists them and marks the ones you have. The Profile's PRESTIGES column (F1) lights up the ones you own and highlights the next.

## Cost

<CostTable id="character-prestige" />

The cost comes from your **Bank balance**, not your inventory. Weapon mastery and blood lineage prestiges use the same range, from 75 to 4,467.

## How to prestige

<PlayModes>
	<Fragment slot="interface">
		1. Reach level 90.
		2. Make sure the cost is in your Bank (F5).
		3. Click **PRESTIGE** on the Prestige Bar next to the HUD, or on the Profile (F1) below the XP bar.

		You're back at level 1 with the new buff active. **There's no confirmation screen.**
	</Fragment>
	<Fragment slot="chat">
		<Command cmd=".level">Opens the character menu; the Prestige option shows the cost.</Command>
		<Command cmd=".prestige exp">Prestiges immediately, without confirmation (short form `.pg exp`).</Command>
	</Fragment>
</PlayModes>

<Aside type="tip" title="See the cost first">
Talk to Vlad Parker and choose *I want to prestige.* → *Character.* He shows the cost and only prestiges after you click **Confirm.** You can also prestige from the [website panel](/getting-started/website-panel/).
</Aside>

## Automatic prestige <Label type="vip" />

With VIP, **Auto-Prestige** can prestige your character automatically the moment you hit 90, with a target and a spending cap. See [Auto-Prestige and Auto-Pulse](/meditation/auto-prestige/).

## What prestige 20 changes

- You can enrol in a **Vampiric School**. See [Schools](/schools/).
- The PRESTIGE button becomes **GIVE PULSE**, the start of Pulses and Transfusions. See [Endgame](/endgame/).

## Common questions

### I can't prestige. Why?

Either you're not at 90 yet, or your Bank is short. The chat message shows the cost and your balance.

### I hid the Prestige Bar. How do I prestige?

Use the PRESTIGE button on the Profile (F1), or `.prestige exp`.
