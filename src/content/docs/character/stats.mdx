## Where your attributes come from

| Source | What it adds | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Base game | Your vampire's normal stats | Always |
| Class and evolutions | The class's attribute table, growing with each evolution | **Only with the class weapon in hand** |
| Weapon mastery | 3 attributes your class chooses | Grows with the level and prestige of the weapon in hand |
| Blood lineage | 3 attributes your class chooses | Grows with the level and prestige of your current blood |
| Character prestige | 20 fixed buffs (Damage Reduction, Max Health, Physical Power…) | Permanent |
| Plasmas | Bonuses **and penalties** from 8 ancestors | After Transfusions |
| Gear fortification and refinement | Physical Power (weapon), Health (armour), Magic Power (necklace) | Saved on the item |
| Vampiric School spell | An extra spell that fires with your basic attack | Not a stat, but extra damage |

## Seeing the breakdown

Press **F1**. The **ATTRIBUTES** column lists everything your character has today, by category (OFFENSIVE, DEFENSIVE, MOBILITY…). **Hover over an attribute** and a box shows where each part comes from (*Base Game*, class, prestige, plasmas…) and the total. This panel exists only with the interface. Without it, use separate commands such as `.level`, `.weapon info`, `.blood info` and `.tf info`.

## Class scaling profiles

Each official class page says which stats a class benefits from and which it doesn't use:

| Profile | Benefits from | Doesn't use | Classes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Physical | Physical Power, Physical Crit, Weapon Skill Power | Spell Power | Noctarin, Bastien, Carmesyne, Skarn, Umbrellis, General |
| Physical (no crit) | Physical Power, Weapon Skill Power | Spell Power, Physical Crit | Mortak |
| Magic | Spell Power, Spell Crit | Physical Power, Weapon Skill Power | Draekhar, Sangrael, Sepulkros |
| Hybrid | Spell Power, Spell Crit, Weapon Skill Power, Minion Damage | Physical Power | La Muerte |

Skarn, Sepulkros and La Muerte also benefit from **Minion Damage**. The Sepulkros Reaper variant and Mortak Relentless change some of this. See their class pages.

## How bonuses combine

## Attribute glossary

The official class pages are in Portuguese. This wiki translates their attribute names as below. Where an English guide already uses a name, such as the prestige buffs, the wiki uses that name. Filter by either language.

<Aside type="note" title="Weapon Cooldown">
The class pages list *Tempo de Recarga da Arma* ("weapon recharge time") with positive values, such as +8%. The pages don't say whether a higher number means faster cooldowns. The wiki shows the value as published.
</Aside>
