## Where XP comes from

- **Killing creatures.** V Bloods give much more than normal creatures.
- **Quests.** Every tutorial step pays XP, and so do many other quests.
- **Meditation.** XP every minute while standing in the Meditation Chamber. See [Meditation](/meditation/).
- **The Illusory Arena.** Its enemies give **2.5× character XP**.

## What changes how much you get

| Situation | Effect |
|---|---|
| Creature weaker than you | Less XP; the bigger the gap, the less |
| Creature at your level or above | Full XP |
| Group play | Everyone nearby and in combat at the kill gets **full** XP. They don't need to be in your clan, and the XP isn't split. |
| Your pet gets the kill | It counts for you |
| Docile creatures; creatures from tombs, worm nests and summoning circles | Less XP |
| Boss summons | No XP |
| Level 90 | **XP stops counting and is lost** until you prestige |

## Common questions

### Does prestiging make XP slower?

No. The official guide says prestiging doesn't make XP slower.

### I'm at 90 and still hunting. Am I banking XP?

No. XP at 90 is lost. Prestige first. See [Character prestige](/character/prestige/).
