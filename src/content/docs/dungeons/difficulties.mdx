| Tier | Key recipe | Other key sources | Extra reward |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adventurer | 500 Stygian Shard + 150 Copper Ingot | Incursion I–III boss | — |
| Veteran | 750 Stygian Shard + 20 Reinforced Plank | Fuse 5 Adventurer; incursion boss | — |
| Master | 500 Greater Stygian Shard + 15 Dark Silver Ingot | Fuse 3 Veteran; incursion II–III boss | **Special roll** |
| Nightmare | 1,000 Greater Stygian Shard + 5 Power Core | Incursion II–III boss only | **Special roll** |

- **Enemies are always level 90.** The higher the tier, the harder it is and the better the prize.
- The **Special roll** on Master and Nightmare can give **Perfect Gems, Cristais or Ressonador**.
- The panel at Mark shows the chance of each prize, tier by tier.
- Some achievements ask for Nightmare clears. For example, Mortak's *Unshakable Will* needs 2 Nightmare dungeons.

The guides don't publish enemy health or damage per tier.
