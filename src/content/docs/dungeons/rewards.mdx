## What a win pays

Each player rolls their own prize.

- **20% of the key's materials** back, on every win.
- **Rolled materials.**
- **A chance at a weapon:** Blue, Purple or Legendary.
- **Master and Nightmare:** a **Special roll**, which can give **Perfect Gems, Cristais or Ressonador**.
- **Minion Collectors** (for unlocking Skarn, Sepulkros and La Muerte summons) come from dungeon rewards.

The panel at Mark shows the **chance** of each prize, tier by tier.

<Unverified q="Q-022">The guides call dungeon weapons "Blue, Purple or Legendary", while the Market treats Blue, Purple and Orange all as legendaries.</Unverified>

## Weapon affinity

Before entering, set an **affinity** at Mark (**CHANGE** at the top of the panel). When a weapon drops, it has a **40% chance** of being that type. Affinity doesn't raise the chance of getting a weapon. See [Weapon affinity](/equipment/weapon-affinity/).

## Claiming

<Aside type="caution" title="15 free slots">
The reward comes **by mail** from the *Dungeon System*. Press **F6**, open the letter and click **Claim reward**. You need **15 free inventory slots**. Without them, the claim waits in your mailbox.
</Aside>

## Records

The panel's **RECORDS** tab is a ranking scoreboard with no prize.

## Feats

Dungeon clears count towards the *Explorer of the Depths*, *Lord of Nightmares* and *Conqueror of the Abyss* medals. Each medal stage needs a total count from the start. For example, Silver on *Explorer of the Depths* needs 100 dungeons in total. See [Feats, Fame and titles](/world/feats-fame-titles/).
