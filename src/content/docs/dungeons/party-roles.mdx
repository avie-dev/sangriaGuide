These suggestions are this wiki's reading of the official class pages, not a tested composition. <Label type="community" />

## Roles the class pages describe

| Role | Classes | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Tank | **Mortak (Unshakable)** | Health and resistances; with the class achievement, Blood Rage taunts everything within 20 m; the ultimate's ice shield gives crowd-control immunity |
| Healer / reviver | **Sangrael** | Area heals from Spell Power; ultimate revives every downed vampire within 12 m; passive near-instant revive every 180 s |
| Ranged damage | Noctarin, Draekhar, La Muerte | Bows and crossbows, staff spells, pistols |
| Melee damage | Carmesyne, Skarn, Umbrellis, General, Bastien, Mortak (Relentless), Sepulkros (Reaper) | Physical damage up close |
| Summoners | Skarn, Sepulkros (Summoner), La Muerte | Extra bodies from basic attacks |

## Why it matters in dungeons

- **Going down isn't death, but a full wipe is a defeat.** Revives matter, and Sangrael's kit is built for them.
- **Doors close 10 seconds** after the first player passes, so move as a group.
- **15 minutes** for the whole run.
- **No pets** inside, so summoner classes are the only extra bodies.

The guides don't document individual dungeons' rooms or bosses. See the dungeon pages for what is known.
