Each player spends **1 key of the chosen tier** on entry. The same key works for all four dungeons.

## Forging (at Mark)

| Tier | Recipe for 1 key |
|---|---|
| Adventurer | 500 Stygian Shard + 150 Copper Ingot |
| Veteran | 750 Stygian Shard + 20 Reinforced Plank |
| Master | 500 Greater Stygian Shard + 15 Dark Silver Ingot |
| Nightmare | 1,000 Greater Stygian Shard + 5 Power Core |

<Steps>

1. At the Events Hub, talk to **Mark**. The Sangria Dungeons panel opens.
2. Click the tier. The **KEY** column shows the materials and how many you have. They must be in your inventory.
3. Choose **x1, x5, x10 or x15** and click **Forge** (**Cancel** undoes the choice).

</Steps>

## Fusing spare keys

| Fuse | Into |
|---|---|
| 5 Adventurer keys | 1 Veteran key |
| 3 Veteran keys | 1 Master key |
| — | **Nightmare can't be fused.** It comes only from forging or an incursion boss. |

Click the tier you want to *receive* (Veteran or Master). Then, in the KEY column, **Fuse** makes one and **All** fuses as many as possible. The keys must be in your inventory.

## Where the materials come from: Rift Incursions

**Stygian Shards** and **Greater Stygian Shards** come from [Rift Incursions](/events/rift-incursions/). Each portal or gate destroyed gives **50** to everyone inside its circle, so a full rift (2 portals + 1 gate) gives 150. Incursion I pays Stygian Shard, and II and III pay Greater Stygian Shard.

## Ready-made keys

When an incursion's **gate boss** dies, each player in the circle or near the body gets their own draw for a key, delivered by **Mail** (a letter from *The Order of the Observers*).

| Incursion | Possible keys |
|---|---|
| I | Adventurer or Veteran |
| II and III | Any tier; high tiers are rarest |

<Aside type="caution" title="Not from anywhere else">
Dungeons don't drop keys, and keys can't be crafted at a workbench or fed to the Devourer. A boss summoned in your castle (Blood Soul ritual) doesn't give a key either.
</Aside>
