**Blood Dominion** is one of the four Sangria Falls dungeons, along with Sanctuary of Redemption, Grove of Disenchantment, Corrupted Temple.

## What the official guide says

- **You can't choose it.** The dungeon is drawn at random on entry from the ones that are free. You only choose the tier.
- It uses the same **keys** as the other three, in the same four tiers (Adventurer, Veteran, Master, Nightmare).
- Groups of **2 to 4**, **15 minutes**, enemies at **level 90**, one group at a time.
- The same **rewards** on a win, by mail. Each player rolls their own.

See [Rules and risks](/dungeons/rules-and-risks/) and [Rewards](/dungeons/rewards/).
