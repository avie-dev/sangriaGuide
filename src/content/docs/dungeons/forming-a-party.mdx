## At Mark

<PlayModes>
	<Fragment slot="interface">
		1. Choose the tier.
		2. Ask your friends to stand near Mark. They appear in the **NEAR MARK** list. Click **Invite**. Anyone marked "No key" can't be invited.
		3. Each invited player gets a notice and has **30 seconds** to click **Accept** (or Decline).
		4. Check the **READY TO ENTER?** board: 2–4 players, everyone with a key, a free room, and nobody in combat or downed.
		5. Click **Enter the Dungeon**. Everyone's key is spent now.
	</Fragment>
	<Fragment slot="chat">
		<Command cmd=".dungeon">Mark's menu: lead, invite, start and affinity.</Command>
		<Command cmd=".dungeon accept">Accepts a group invite (30 seconds). `.dungeon decline` declines.</Command>
		<Command cmd=".dungeon start">Starts the run (leader only).</Command>
		<Command cmd=".dungeon leave">Leaves or disbands the group before the run starts.</Command>
	</Fragment>
</PlayModes>

**Whoever invites becomes the leader.** Only the leader invites, removes someone (the red button by their name) and starts. **End Group** disbands, and invited players can **Leave Group**. Pets are collected when the group forms and come back after the run.

### Why can't I invite my friend?

They must be near Mark, have a key of the same tier, have finished the starter tutorial, and not be in another group.

## Finding people: the group board

<Steps>

1. Open the **group board**: the round two-people button by the minimap, or `.group`. It works anywhere on the map.
2. On the **Dungeon** row, click the tier (Adventurer, Veteran, Master or Nightmare). That click marks you. Don't click MARKED afterwards, or you'll unmark yourself.
3. Your mark lasts **30 minutes**, and opening the board renews it. Make sure you have that tier's key first.
4. A leader at the Hub sees you in **LOOKING FOR GROUP** and clicks **Call**. A card appears on your screen. Click **On my way**.
5. Travel to the Events Hub through the city teleporter (nobody is teleported for you). The invite arrives when you get there.

</Steps>

Leaders can call the same person again after 2 minutes, and anyone called gets at least 10 minutes of mark to arrive. The board also works for the Rift, the Illusory Arena and the Judgment. See [Group board](/events/group-board/).
