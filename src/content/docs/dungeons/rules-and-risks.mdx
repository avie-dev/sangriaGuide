## During the run

- Talk to the character at the entrance. They open the first door.
- Follow the **Step** shown in the corner of the screen. Progress counts for the whole group. (Your quest list gives way to the dungeon board while you're inside.)
- **Stay together:** a door closes **10 seconds** after the first player goes through, and anyone left behind is pulled into the room.
- **15 minutes** to finish.
- At the end, win or lose, everyone returns to the Events Hub on their feet and at full health.

## Going down

- You stay down until someone revives you or the run ends.
- If everyone is down and nobody gets back up within a few seconds, it's a **defeat**.
- **A defeat or time-out gives no prize, and keys aren't returned.**

<Aside type="danger" title="Don't press X while downed">
Pressing X while downed means **giving up**. You die, leave with no reward, and **your item bag stays inside the dungeon**. The group continues without you.
</Aside>

## Disconnects

Your slot is saved. Come back quickly: you respawn at the entrance and are pulled into the group's room.

## Pets

Your pet is collected when you form or join a group and stays locked until the run ends.

## What isn't documented

<Unverified q="Q-006">The Dungeons guide doesn't say whether group members can hurt each other. The Reality Rupture and Goreswine's Judgment guides say group members become allies, but that isn't stated for dungeons.</Unverified>
