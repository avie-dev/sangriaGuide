The spells are **the same in all four schools**. Only the story, headquarters and NPCs differ. A school pays no currency or items.

## Enrolling

<Steps>

1. Go to the city and press **F11** → **Mark on map**. Sebastian is on the first floor of the city's main building.
2. Talk to him → **View the schools**.
3. Read all four and click **I'M INTERESTED** on one. Enrolment is immediate. A school showing *SCHOOL FULL. UNAVAILABLE RIGHT NOW* can't take you yet.
4. From now on, your school's brazier at the city teleport hub lets you through to the headquarters.

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution" title="Stay active">
Score at least once **every 7 days**. If you go 7 days without scoring, or **14 days without logging in**, you leave the school and get a mail about it. Your rank and points are kept, so you can just enrol again.
</Aside>

## Switching schools

Talk to Sebastian → *I want to request a school transfer*. It's a **swap**: choose a destination and click **Register Transfer**. When a student from that school clicks **SWAP** on your request, you change places. The first transfer is free, and later ones cost more Ourosangue each time. **Cancel Request** doesn't refund what you paid. Your rank in each school is saved.

## School chat

<Command cmd="!escola anyone for the stake?">Sends a message only to your school. `!escola` on its own pins the school channel, and `!global` returns to normal chat.</Command>
