You earn **school points** and climb **ranks 0 to 10**. Points are never lost. Rank unlocks spell tiers: **Tier 1 at rank 1, Tier 2 at rank 4, Tier 3 at rank 8**. Each rank also shortens your spell's cooldown by 5%. Rank 1 needs **800 points**, which is two daily missions. The guides don't publish the points needed for later ranks.

## Daily mission (+600)

At your headquarters, talk to your professor → *Today's tasks*. You get three simple tasks inside the headquarters: interact with marked points, spend time in a study room, or deliver a material to the professor. Locations are marked with your school's icon and tracked in F3. One mission per day, resetting at midnight (Brasília time), and an unfinished one disappears.

| School | Professor |
|---|---|
| Frosthiel | Aldren Alvaneve |
| Luminara | Lumielle Solenne |
| Serphentis | Angi Viper |
| Sanguinis | Sangrev Rubrescarlate |

Each professor only serves their own school's students.

## School events (up to 800 each)

Three 20-minute events a day, **Monday to Saturday** (Brasília time):

| Days | Times |
|---|---|
| Monday, Tuesday | 10:00, 15:00, 20:00 |
| Wednesday, Thursday | 08:00, 17:00, 22:00 |
| Friday, Saturday | 11:00, 16:00, 21:00 |

Chat announces each event, and it appears on the ACTIVE EVENTS board. The F11 **EVENT** column shows the rules, your points and what's next.

| Event | How you score |
|---|---|
| Stake of Domination | 6 map points are invaded; each invader defeated is worth 8 |
| Resource Expedition | Gather across the map: tree 4, stone or ore 8, fish 8 |
| Material Delivery | Deliver materials to your school's delivery NPC; rarer items score more |
| Ritual of Essences | Deliver Greater or Primordial Blood Essence to the same NPC; when the school fills the collection, the Ritual Guardian appears, so defeat it within 5 minutes |

Delivery NPCs (marked on the map during events): Motte Invernal (Frosthiel), Aureon Sol (Luminara), Seth Malefic (Serphentis), Vespera Hemoria (Sanguinis). The Delivery panel shows only accepted materials, and the weapon in your hand never appears.

## Sunday bonus

The points **your school** gathers in the week's events become bonus time on **Sunday from 19:00** (Brasília time). **10,000 points buy 3 hours**, and less is proportional (5,000 buys 1h30). During it, **your pet capture chance doubles**. Each school has its own time, and there's no winning school. The **WEEK · COLLECTIVE PROGRESS** bar shows your school's progress and resets on Monday.

<Aside type="note">
The guides say "the points the school gathers in the week's events". Whether the daily mission's +600 also counts towards the Sunday total isn't stated.
</Aside>
