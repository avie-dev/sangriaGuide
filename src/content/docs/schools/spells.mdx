- **44 spells**, the same in every school. What unlocks a spell is your **rank**.
- **Tier 1** at rank 1, **Tier 2** at rank 4, **Tier 3** at rank 8.
- **Only one spell is active** at a time.
- It **fires on its own** with the **basic attack of any weapon**, whenever its cooldown ends, so it doesn't depend on your class.
- Each rank makes the cooldown **5% shorter**.

## Choosing a spell

<Steps>

1. Press **F11** and look at the **GRIMOIRE**, sorted by TIER.
2. Click the **eye icon** to see a spell in action.
3. Click **Select**. **Deactivate** removes it.

</Steps>

A locked spell shows the rank it needs and how many ranks you're missing. The cooldown appears as an icon next to your class crest on the bottom bar.

<Aside type="note">
The official guide doesn't list the 44 spells or their effects. They're shown in game in the F11 grimoire. This wiki will add them once they're documented.
</Aside>

## The spell didn't fire

It's on cooldown, it's deactivated, or the hit wasn't a basic attack.
