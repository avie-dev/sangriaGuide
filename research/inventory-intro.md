## Findings that shape the wiki

1. **Large parts of the "English" site are Portuguese.** All 12 class pages, the class hub, the Commands reference (309 commands), Interface shortcuts, the interface install page and the VIP page are served under `/en/`, but their body text is wholly or mostly Portuguese. Translating these accurately is the single biggest way this wiki can help English speakers, and it is real work for Phase 5 and the reference section.
2. **Every guide has two modes.** "With the interface" (ScarletInterface panels and F-keys) and "Without the interface" (chat commands and numbered menus). The wiki documents both, for example with tabs, instead of assuming the interface.
3. **The interface is now ScarletInterface**, installed through the Sangria Falls Launcher. The install video still shows the older interfaces (Eclipse, SangrisInterface), which the page itself points out.
4. **The official guides are global.** Nothing found mentions RPG 04, a 2X rate or per-server settings. Every "RPG 04 SPECIFIC" claim therefore needs in-game or staff confirmation.
5. **Five server currencies** live in the Bank (per the Bank guide). Quitina (the brief's "Chitin") is the only one you can withdraw as an item; one Quitina gives 2x XP for 30 minutes on Character, Weapon or Blood.
6. **Some of the brief's terms don't match official names.** "Boss Souls" and "Act Souls" correspond to a pet's own souls and the items *Act 1–4 Soul*, *Primordial Soul* and *Special Soul*. "Spell jewels" appears nowhere in the guides. "Minion Collectors" appears only in the Classes guide. Articles use the official names, with the brief's wording as search aliases.
7. **Internal inconsistencies already spotted.** Draekhar's chain table names the Corrupted Q "Redemoinho do Carniceiro" while the ability list calls it "Jorro Corrompido". Chain names differ between the list (Glacial, Caos, Tempestade, Corrompida, Luminosa, Sanguínea) and the ability headings (Glacial, Caótica, Trovejante, Corrompida, Sagrada, Sangrenta). These are logged in the open questions, not silently resolved.
8. **Dated content exists.** Profession Services cites a rule "in force since 30/09/2026", and Feats lists Sangrisween 2026 medals. Seasonal content needs a last-verified date and a review cadence.
